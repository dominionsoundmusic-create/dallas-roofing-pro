Common questions

Is Dallas Metro Roofing Pro a roofing company?

No. It is a phone line and website that connects you with independent local roofing companies, which inspect, quote and do the work.

Who answers the phone?

An automated assistant answers any time, asks what you need and transfers you to a local roofing company, or passes your details along if it cannot pick up.

Does it cost anything?

No. The line may be paid a referral fee by the companies it connects callers with; you never pay it.

Are roofers licensed in Texas?

No. Ask for a certificate of insurance, a written scope and local references.

Can a roofer pay my deductible?

No. Texas law makes it illegal for a contractor to waive, rebate or absorb your deductible.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the city. Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, McKinney and Grand Prairie each have different rules, covered on the city pages.

Can I cancel a contract signed at my door?

Usually within three business days under the FTC's Cooling-Off Rule for sales of $25 or more made at your home.

What areas are covered?

Dallas-Fort Worth, including Fort Worth, Arlington, Garland, Plano, Frisco, McKinney, Richardson, Allen, Irving, Denton, Flower Mound, Grand Prairie, Carrollton, Mesquite and Lewisville.

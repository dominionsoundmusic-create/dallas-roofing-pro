Get the two-page checklist

Much is settled in the days right after hail: which photos exist, how much more water gets inside, who ends up on your roof, and whether the insurer hears about it in time. This checklist puts those steps in order on two pages you can stick to the fridge.

Nobody buys or receives your details for marketing. The privacy policy has more.

What the seven sections cover

  1. The first hour. Power lines, keeping people out of wet rooms, staying off the roof, and writing down when the storm hit and how big the hail was.
  2. Photos. Which pictures count most, from bent AC unit fins to dinged gutters, vents and torn screens, since they prove how big the hail was at your house.
  3. Limiting damage. Buckets, moving belongings, asking a roofer for an emergency tarp, and keeping receipts.
  4. Your policy. The percentage wind and hail deductible, replacement cost versus actual cash value, and the time limit to report.
  5. Opening the claim. The claim number, the roofer's inspection, and the adjuster's visit.
  6. Hiring safely. Insurance certificates, permits, a written scope, how much to put down, and why no Texas contractor may cover your deductible.
  7. After the work. Final invoice, city paperwork, warranty papers, and getting back any depreciation the insurer withheld.

Why a checklist helps after a DFW hailstorm

{{ img('hail-damaged-shingles-closeup.jpg', 'Close view of asphalt shingles with dark round hail marks where granules were knocked off', 1920, 1080) }}

Hail damage is easy to underestimate from the yard. Bruised shingles can look fine for months before they start to leak, and by then the storm date is hard to remember. A short written record made in the first days, with dated photos, makes the conversation with your insurer and your roofer much easier.

The checklist also helps you slow down. After a large storm, crews from outside the area often go door to door. A list of what to ask, and the reminder that Texas law does not allow a contractor to cover your deductible, keeps the decision yours.

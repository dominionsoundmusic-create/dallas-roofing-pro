A railroad stop that turned into a fast-growing suburb

Burleson began as a depot on the Missouri-Kansas-Texas Railroad after the stretch from Fort Worth to Alvarado was laid out in 1880. Today the city sits mostly in Johnson County with a piece in Tarrant County, and it is growing fast: about 36,690 residents in 2010, about 47,600 in 2020, and an estimated 57,625 in 2024.

Growth that fast changes what roofing looks like. A big share of homes are young, so the typical Burleson call is a first hail claim or a first full replacement, not a third. On the city's western edge, Chisholm Summit was the first master-planned community proposed in Burleson and is planned for about 3,066 homes. Tallgrass, at the Chisholm Trail Parkway and FM 1902, was announced at more than 600 acres and about 4,000 homes.

When rotten decking shows up halfway through

Most Burleson re-roofs start as a shingle job. The surprise comes after tear-off, when a crew finds soft or water-stained plywood under the old shingles. In Burleson, that is the moment the job can cross into permit territory, because replacing decking is the trigger the city names.

Settle it before anyone climbs up. Ask each company to write down:

  • The price per sheet for replacement decking, and how many sheets the bid assumes.
  • Who applies for the permit if decking is replaced, and who pays the fee.
  • That the old roof is fully removed and the decking is checked before underlayment goes on.
  • That you will get photos of any decking it replaces.

Six questions for Burleson roofers before you sign

  1. Who is the owner, and where is the business based? Write down the name and a street address, not just a cell number.
  2. Can your insurer send me your liability certificate? The insurer, not the salesperson.
  3. Does this job need a Burleson permit? If decking is replaced, it should.
  4. Is every item written into the scope? Removal of the old layer, new underlayment, edge metal, flashing, vents and haul-off should each have a line.
  5. How will you handle my insurance claim paperwork? A roofer may document damage, but only a licensed public adjuster may negotiate for you.
  6. Who registers the shingle warranty? Ask for proof in your name.

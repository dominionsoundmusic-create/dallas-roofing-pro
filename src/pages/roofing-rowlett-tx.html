A lake town that is mostly newer than the lake

Roofing in Rowlett, TX starts with Lake Ray Hubbard. The reservoir was finished in 1971, and Rowlett, bordered by it on the east and south, grew around it.

In 1970, while the new lake was still filling, Rowlett had about 2,200 people. The census found 7,522 residents in 1980, 23,260 in 1990 and 44,503 in 2000, and the 2020 count was 62,535. Put another way, most houses in Rowlett went up after 1980, and a large share of them in the building wave of the 1990s. State resolutions describe the city as having about 30 miles of lake shoreline, and a lot of that growth faced the water.

The city straddles two counties, Dallas and Rockwall, but one set of city building rules covers both sides. Rowlett Creek forms the western edge, and Garland lies beyond it.

Rowlett storm timeline

Two tornadoes in four years shaped the roofs on many Rowlett streets. Here is what the National Weather Service and local reporting documented.

  1. December 26, 2015. A tornado that formed in Sunnyvale crossed Garland and entered southeast Rowlett. NWS Fort Worth rated it EF-4 overall and EF-3 in Rowlett, and its records list one death in Rowlett. Harbor Drive was among the hardest-hit streets.
  2. December 2016. CBS DFW counted 1,296 Rowlett homes damaged or destroyed in that storm, with nearly 1,170 rebuilt or under construction a year later.
  3. October 20, 2019. During the night of the Dallas tornado outbreak, NWS surveyors found EF-1 damage on Hickox Road near the President George Bush Turnpike, with winds estimated at 100 mph.
  4. December 2023. Rowlett's mayor told WFAA that only four slabs remained where homes had stood before 2015.

The result is a city with an unusual mix: lake-era houses from the 1980s and 1990s on second roofs, and hundreds of homes rebuilt or re-roofed in 2016 and 2017 that are now about a decade old.

Registration first, permit second

Rowlett is direct about contractors. Its building FAQ calls registration with the city mandatory before a contractor begins any work, and its registration form files roofers under the general contractor category. Applications for both registration and permits go through the city's online permitting system, which is also where inspections are requested.

The city lists a new roof among the residential projects that need a building permit. A homeowner's part is simple: ask the roofer for its Rowlett registration and the permit number, and keep both with the contract. Rowlett Building Inspections is at 5702 Rowlett Road and answers at 972-412-6125.

City web pages and forms do not always agree on the current registration fee, which is one more reason to let the roofer handle the filing and to confirm anything unclear with the city directly.

Four reasons Rowlett homeowners pick up the phone

The rebuild roof turns ten

Hundreds of roofs went on in the year after the 2015 tornado. Many owners now want to know how those shingles are holding up before the next spring season.

Open water to the east

With the lake along the east and south, storm winds reach shoreline streets without trees or buildings to break them. Loose ridge caps and lifted edges are the usual signs.

Second roofs on 1990s houses

Houses from the 1990s boom are often on their second roof, and some are due for a third. That brings up tear-off, decking and ventilation questions.

A clean paper trail

Rowlett buyers and insurers ask about storm history. A registered roofer, a permit and a written warranty make the next sale or claim easier.

What to ask a Rowlett roofer

  • Is your Rowlett contractor registration current today, and will you file the permit through the city's online system?
  • If my roof went on after the 2015 tornado, can you tell what shingle it is and whether the maker's warranty was registered?
  • On a lakeside lot, how will you fasten the edges and ridge to hold in strong wind?
  • If this is a claim, will you meet the adjuster, and will you explain any gap between your estimate and theirs without promising what the insurer will do?
  • Will your insurance carrier email me your liability certificate itself, rather than you handing me a copy?
  • Which parts of the job does your written workmanship warranty cover?

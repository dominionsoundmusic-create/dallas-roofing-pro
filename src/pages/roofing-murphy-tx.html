A young city with an old name

Roofing in Murphy, TX starts with a city that is much newer than its history. Settlers arrived in 1846 under the Peters Colony grants, and the town took William Murphy's name when the railroad came through in 1888. It incorporated in 1958. Then the suburbs reached it.

Census counts show Murphy with about 3,100 people in 2000 and about 21,000 by 2020, so most of its homes are recent. The President George Bush Turnpike opened in 2000 and made the city an easy commute to Plano and Richardson.

The result is a town where a large share of the roofs went on within a short span of years. Many of those first roofs are now old enough to be in the range, which is the window when North Texas homeowners start pricing replacements, especially after a few hail seasons.

What Murphy's re-roof rules say

From the City of Murphy's re-roofing permit requirements. Confirm the current version with the city before work starts.

RuleWhat it means for your roof
Remove the existing covering firstNo layering a new roof over the old one. Expect a full tear-off.
Minimum Class C coveringThe roof covering must carry at least a Class C fire rating.
Approved materialsComposition shingles (30-year), standing seam metal, terra-cotta tile, concrete tile or slate.
Muted colorsBright or unusual roof colors may not pass. Check with your HOA as well.
ProhibitedWood shingle, shake and metal roofing systems of the kind associated with agricultural or industrial buildings, and natural (galvanized or aluminum) metal roofs.
Permit feeSet by Murphy's Code of Ordinances, Article A9.000.

Registration, permits and inspections

  • Every contractor registers. Murphy's building permit application says all contractors and subcontractors must be registered with the city. Permits are not issued until they are.
  • What registration has asked for. The city's builder packet says photo ID, proof of liability insurance and any required state licenses are presented in person, and registration lasts 12 months. Texas does not license roofers, so for a roofing company the insurance proof is the part to ask about.
  • Permit on site. The permit must be posted at the job for inspection.
  • Inspection timing. Requests made by 2 p.m. are reviewed for scheduling the next business day, and inspectors do not inspect while work is still going on.

City Hall is at 206 N. Murphy Road (972-468-4000). The inspection line is 972-468-4050.

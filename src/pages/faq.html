Insurance and storm claims

Does homeowners insurance cover hail damage in Texas?

Most homeowners policies cover hail and wind, subject to a deductible and to how the policy values the roof (replacement cost or actual cash value). Some policies limit cosmetic damage or older roofs. Only your policy and your insurer can say what yours covers. How roof claims work in Texas.

Can the roofer handle my claim for me?

No. A roofer may document damage and walk the adjuster through it. Negotiating the claim is another matter: Texas keeps that for licensed public adjusters and forbids contractors from adjusting claims on homes they repair. The claim stays yours, unless you hire a public adjuster.

Will a hail claim raise my premium?

It can, depending on your insurer and your claims history. Ask your agent before filing, especially if the damage is close to your deductible.

Timing and the work itself

Do roofers work in the winter?

Yes. North Texas winters are mostly mild, so re-roofs go on through the cold months. Shingles seal best in warmth, so crews avoid freezing days and ice, and some hand-seal shingles in cold weather. Ask what the crew does differently on a cold day.

How late do roofers work?

Most crews start early to beat the heat and finish in the afternoon. Many cities limit construction noise to set hours, and those hours differ from town to town. Ask the roofer when the crew will arrive and leave.

How often is a roof replaced around here?

It depends on the material and the storms. Asphalt shingle roofs in North Texas often need replacing sooner than the label suggests because of hail and heat. See how long a roof lasts in North Texas.

Does a re-roof need a city permit here?

In many DFW cities, yes. Dallas and Plano, for example, require a permit for a re-roof. Rules differ by city, and each town page notes what that city's own site says.

Do roofers need a license in Texas?

Texas has never created a state roofing license. A 2025 bill to create one died without a House floor vote. Some cities register contractors, and manufacturers run their own installer programs. What to check instead.

When should I pay the roofer?

Keep the bulk of the money until the work is done. A materials deposit is normal; the last check waits for a finished, cleaned-up job, the invoice, any city sign-off, and the warranty paperwork.

What if a roofer knocks on my door after a storm?

You do not have to let anyone on your roof or sign anything. Ask for a local address, proof of insurance and a written estimate, and compare it with others.

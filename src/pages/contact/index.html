Ways to reach the line

Phone, any hour

{{ site.business.phone_display }}. An automated assistant answers day and night, every day of the year. Say you need a roofer, describe the problem, and name your town.

The form on this page

Best for work that can wait: an aging roof, a bid you want compared, or a commercial roof that needs a look before the next storm season.

Facebook

The line also has a Facebook page linked at the bottom of every page. For roof help, the phone gets you to a roofer faster than a message.

What to have ready

  • Your address or nearest cross streets, and the town (city limits matter for permits).
  • What you see: a stain, a drip, missing shingles, dented gutters, hail in the yard, a limb on the roof.
  • When it started, and the date of the storm if one caused it.
  • Whether you have already called your insurance company, and the claim number if you have one.
  • Roughly how old the roof is, if you know.

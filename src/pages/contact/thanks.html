What happens next

The roofing company that receives your request reads your note, then phones the number you gave. It will want to know a little more about the roof and set a time to see it. The visit, the estimate and any work are between you and that company; DFW Expert Roofers is only the line that connected you.

Keep your phone nearby, and answer calls from numbers you do not recognize for the next day or so, since the roofer may call from a business line.

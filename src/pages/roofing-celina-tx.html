The fastest-growing city of its size, roof by roof

Celina counted 16,739 people in the 2020 census. Census Bureau estimates then ranked it the fastest-growing U.S. city of 20,000 or more, first for 2020 to 2023 and again for 2024 to 2025, when it reached an estimated 64,427 residents.

Growth like that means a large share of Celina roofs are new, on homes in subdivisions such as Light Farms and Mustang Lakes near the Prosper line. New roofs still fail when a storm is strong enough, and Celina learned that in 2024.

For roofing in Celina TX, DFW Expert Roofers offers a free phone line. It is not a contractor. Your call is transferred to an independent, locally owned roofing company that serves Celina, and that company inspects and does the work.

May 25 and 26, 2024: one block, eight homes

Late on a Saturday night, a large storm crossed northern Collin County. On its southern edge, a smaller satellite tornado touched down in Celina. The National Weather Service survey rated it a high-end EF-3 with estimated winds of 165 mph, on a path about 0.71 miles long and up to 175 yards wide. The survey recorded no deaths or injuries.

Homes leveled

NBC DFW reported eight homes damaged on one Celina block, some of them leveled. Fox 4 reported multiple homes destroyed on Prairie Meadow Lane.

Firefighter row

Five of those eight homes belonged to Frisco firefighters, NBC DFW said. A family trapped in a pantry was dug out by neighbors.

Neighbors showed up

Close to 200 volunteers arrived the Monday morning after the storm to help clear debris, according to Fox 4.

Few storms are that violent, but the lesson applies to every storm: the hours after are when homeowners are most likely to sign with whoever arrives first. Keep the deductible law in mind and take time to compare.

Celina's roof permit and contractor registration

  1. The roofer registers. Every contractor working in the city must register through the MyGov portal before applying for permits. Insurance must list the City of Celina as certificate holder, and a valid Texas driver's license is part of the file.
  2. Registration expires. It lasts 365 days at most, so a roofer that worked in Celina two years ago may not be current today.
  3. The permit request goes in. A roof permit is required for roofing projects. The registered general contractor files it with a Residential Minor Permit Application. The fee is $100 per request.
  4. The deck question. The application asks whether the roof deck is being replaced, so the roofer should know the deck's condition before applying.
  5. No registration, no inspection. The city says it will not issue permits or perform inspections for anyone without a current registration.

Questions to ask before you sign in Celina

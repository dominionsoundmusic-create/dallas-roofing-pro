A small city on the lake's western arm

Roofing in Highland Village, TX happens in a city of about 16,000 people on the south side of the western branch of Lewisville Lake. The 2020 census counted 15,899 residents.

It started small. Residents voted 53 to 7 to incorporate on February 14, 1963, and the town had only 516 people in 1970 and 3,246 in 1980. Nearly all of its growth came after that, as the suburbs north of DFW Airport filled in. A Texas Senate resolution describes its beginnings as a rural farming community on the southern shore of the lake.

The city keeps a close eye on roofing work. It requires a permit to replace a roof and a registered roofer to pull it. DFW Expert Roofers is a free phone line, not a contractor: the call goes to an independent, locally owned roofing company that serves Highland Village, and that company handles the city side.

Highland Village's four roofing rules

1. A permit to replace the roof

The city's permit guide lists replacing a roof as work that needs a permit, and it is not valid until paid for, issued and placed on the job site.

2. A registered roofer

City ordinance requires roofing contractors to register, and the registration form asks for proof of current liability insurance. The form lists a yearly fee; confirm the current amount with the city.

3. A permit you can see from the street

The city asks that the permit be posted where it is clearly visible from outside. If you do not see one, ask the roofer for the number.

4. An inspection at the end

When the roof replacement is done, the homeowner or the contractor schedules an inspection. Do not make the final payment until it passes.

One more point from the city's FAQ: whoever pulls the permit, the owner is ultimately responsible for making sure it was obtained.

June 11, 2023 in southern Denton County

On the evening of June 11, 2023, storms dropped very large hail across the area west and south of the lake. NBC DFW gathered reader photos from neighboring Flower Mound and Argyle, and storm report logs list hail of roughly 3 inches in Highland Village that night. Hail that large can crack shingles, split vent caps and dent metal flashing.

If your roof rode through that storm or a later one without a close look, a roof inspection can tell you whether there is damage worth discussing with your insurer. Keep in mind that a roofer may point out damage, but only you and your insurer settle a claim.

Questions to ask a Highland Village roofer

  • Does Highland Village have your company on its current registration list?
  • Will you pull the permit, and where will it be posted at my house?
  • Who schedules the final inspection when the roof is done?
  • Can your insurance agent send me the same liability certificate you gave the city?
  • If you replace decking, how is it priced and photographed?
  • For a hail claim: what did you find, and can I have your photos for my insurer?

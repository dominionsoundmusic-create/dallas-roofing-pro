A permit town, not a handshake town

Roofing in Lewisville, TX runs through City Hall. The city's building permit list names re-roofs and re-decks, and every contractor has to be registered with Lewisville before it can get one.

That is stricter than some neighbors, where swapping shingles alone needs no permit. Here the dumpster in the driveway should come with a permit number. The city checks contractor registration when a permit is requested, and that registration expires unless renewed annually.

DFW Expert Roofers is a free phone line, not a contractor. When you call, an automated assistant takes the details and puts you through to an independent, locally owned roofing company that takes Lewisville calls. Ask that company the permit questions below; the line itself never pulls permits or touches a roof.

Whose name goes on a Lewisville roofing permit

The Building Services FAQ has a warning homeowners rarely hear: if you apply for and accept the permit for your contractor, you may be the one held responsible for correcting work that fails code.

  1. The roofer registers. All contractors must register with the city before receiving a building permit, and registrations renew annually.
  2. The roofer applies. Permits and registration both run through the MGO Connect portal. Let the registered company be the applicant.
  3. You see the permit. Ask for a copy before tear-off. If a quote says no permit is needed, ask the roofer to show you why.
  4. Decking counts too. The city lists re-decks next to re-roofs, so a job that turns into new sheathing is still covered.
  5. Questions go to the city. General permit questions: 972-219-3470.

Castle Hills: Lewisville rules since November 2021

For years Castle Hills sat outside city limits. The city says full annexation took effect on November 15, 2021, and that Lewisville Building Services took over the neighborhood's permits. Its Castle Hills page lists roof replacement, along with window replacement and air conditioning work, as jobs that need a permit.

If you bought or roofed a Castle Hills home before late 2021, the rules you remember may not be the rules now. Check that the roofer holds a current Lewisville registration, and check whether your neighborhood association wants to approve shingle color or style first.

Texas also limits how far an association can go on shingles. Under Property Code 202.011, an association generally cannot forbid hail-resistant or energy-saving shingles if they look like the ones it already permits. Ask the association for its current rules in writing before you choose a product.

Storms on record in Lewisville

March 23, 2016: the hail swath

CoreLogic data reported by Insurance Journal described hail of 1.5 to 2 inches falling along a path nearly 50 miles long, from near Lewisville to east of Wylie, across parts of Denton, Collin and Dallas counties.

March 4, 2025: wind on SH 121

A paint distribution warehouse on SH 121 lost part of its roof and a wall. Community Impact reported the National Weather Service in Fort Worth said the damage fit winds near 90 mph.

The lake next door

The Corps of Engineers finished the dam in 1954, the city says, turning the old reservoir into Lewisville Lake at 29,592 acres.

Houses from one fast decade

The city grew from about 9,200 people in 1970 to almost 25,000 by 1980, after DFW Airport opened to the south in 1974. Homes from that era are now on later roofs.

After a storm crosses Lewisville

The first hour matters more than the first estimate. Stay off the roof, keep people away from downed lines, and photograph damage from the yard and from inside. CoServ Electric runs lines around the lake and Oncor serves much of the area, so report a downed line to whichever utility serves your home, or call 911.

Then call your insurance company and ask what your policy covers, what your deductible is, and whether it wants its own adjuster to see the roof first. A roofer may point out damage, but Texas law bars it from negotiating your claim or covering your deductible.

For a commercial roof like the one torn open on SH 121, ask the roofer to list membrane, insulation and edge metal separately, so you can see what the wind took and what was already worn.

Six questions for a Lewisville roofer

  • Are you registered with the City of Lewisville this year, and under what company name?
  • Will your company be the permit applicant, and when will I see the permit?
  • If you replace decking, how do you price it and how will you show me the bad wood?
  • For Castle Hills: have you worked with my neighborhood association's approval process?
  • Can your insurance agent email me proof of liability coverage?
  • When will the shingle warranty be registered, and in whose name?

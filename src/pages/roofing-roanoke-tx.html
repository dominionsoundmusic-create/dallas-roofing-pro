The Unique Dining Capital, and its roofs

Roofing in Roanoke, TX serves a small city with a big name. The Texas Legislature dubbed Roanoke the Unique Dining Capital of Texas, and its restaurants cluster along Oak Street downtown.

Roanoke had 9,665 residents in the 2020 census and an estimated 10,798 by mid-2023. Most of the city lies in Denton County, with a small piece in Tarrant County. Newer subdivisions have filled in much of the land around the old town. Fairway Ranch, for example, was built on part of the 700-acre ranch that once belonged to golfer Byron Nelson, and listings there show homes built from about 2013 to 2019.

DFW Expert Roofers is a free phone line, not a roofing contractor. A call connects you with an independent, locally owned roofing company that takes Roanoke calls, and that company does all the roof work.

Roof repair in Roanoke: what the city says

Many Roanoke searches are about repair, not a whole new roof. The city's rule covers both.

  • Repairs count. Roanoke's Building Inspection page says a permit is required before alterations, additions, new construction or repairs to existing buildings and structures.
  • The codes are current. The city has adopted the 2021 International Codes, including the residential code, by Ordinance 2023-122.
  • Contractors register. The city's commercial permit packet says the general contractor must be registered with Roanoke before a permit is issued, at no fee. Ask whether that applies to your home job.
  • Who to call. City Hall is at 500 S. Oak Street. The inspection line is 817-491-2411, staffed Monday to Thursday 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Friday 8 a.m. to noon.

Because the page does not single out roofing, the honest answer to "do I need a permit for this?" is to ask the city with your address and the scope of work, and to have the roofer confirm what it filed.

Two Roanoke roofs, two conversations

A 2010s house in Fairway Ranch

A roof from 2013 to 2019 is probably still on its first set of shingles. Repairs after hail or wind are the likely call, and the original shingle warranty may still apply. Find your closing papers before the roofer visits.

An older Roanoke house

Older homes may have been re-roofed more than once. Ask the roofer to count the existing layers of roofing, what the decking looks like once the old shingles are off, and how the roofer prices extra work it finds.

Hail on May 27, 2024

Storm report listings show hail of about 2 inches about a mile from Roanoke on Memorial Day evening 2024, with 1.5 inch stones a few miles out. Hail that size can bruise shingles and crack vent caps, and the damage is often invisible from the yard. Photograph what you can from the ground, note the date, and call your insurer before any repair work starts.

One line for every roofing service in Dallas

The roofing companies you reach through this line handle the full range of residential and commercial roof work. DFW Expert Roofers itself does none of it; it answers your call and connects you. The pages below explain each kind of work so you can talk to the roofer with a clear idea of what you need.

The services fall into four groups. Most homeowners call about a repair (a leak, a few missing shingles, flashing that let go), about storm damage after hail or wind, about a replacement when the roof is at the end of its life, or about a commercial or low-slope roof on a business, church or garage.

What every service has in common

{{ img('dented-gutter-hail-marks.jpg', 'Close view of a gutter and downspout corner on a brick home below a shingle roof edge', 1200, 800) }}

Whatever the job, a few things should be the same. The roofer inspects before quoting. The quote is in writing and lists materials by brand and product line. The roofer can show you proof of liability insurance from its agent. If your city requires a permit, the roofer pulls it in its own name. And nobody asks you to sign on the spot or offers to cover your insurance deductible.

Many roofers inspect without charging; ask about that on the call, since policies differ from company to company.

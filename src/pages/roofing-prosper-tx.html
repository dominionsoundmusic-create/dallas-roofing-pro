A young town with a lot of young roofs

Prosper had fewer than 10,000 residents in the 2010 census and about 30,000 in 2020. The Town straddles western Collin County and eastern Denton County, and with the population tripling in ten years, a large share of its homes are recent builds.

Windsong Ranch alone was described in 2018 as a 2,030-acre master-planned development. Communities like it add new roofs a section at a time, picked by the builder and then governed by HOA rules for as long as the owner stays.

That does not make Prosper roofs safe from trouble. A roof only a few years old can still lose its granules to hail, and a builder's flashing detail can leak in a wind-driven rain. What the Town of Prosper checks on a re-roof

A registered contractor

All contractors must register with the Town. The fee ordinance lists an annual $100 registration fee.

A re-roof permit

Residential roof repair or replacement carries a $100 fee. The Town's monthly permit reports list many residential re-roofs.

Drip edge

The Town's re-roof sheet requires drip edge on every asphalt shingle roofing project.

An attic look at final

The building final includes the attic: vent penetrations through the roof and clearances around appliance vents.

The code edition

Work is checked against the 2021 International Residential Code and the Town's own amendments.

A clean jobsite

The final also confirms the roof is complete outside and that debris has been cleared away.

Inspection requests go in through the Citizens Self Service portal before the 7:00 AM cutoff on the day requested, and a failed inspection brings a reinspection fee that rises each time. A roofer who passes the first time saves everyone a day.

March 14, 2024: big hail on the county line

According to the National Weather Service in Fort Worth, as reported by Fox 4, the largest hail from the storms of March 14, 2024 fell in Denton and Collin counties, with stones of roughly 1.5 to 3 inches near places including Prosper and Frisco. Cars had cracked windshields and roofs took a beating.

Stones that size can bruise shingles even on a new roof, and the damage is not always visible from the driveway. A roofer can check the shingles and, from inside the attic, look for decking damage or daylight.

If the next storm brings hail to your street, stay off the roof and keep your notes simple: the date, photos from the yard, and a list of what you see inside.

Before a Prosper roofer starts work

  • Ask for proof the company is registered with the Town of Prosper this year.
  • Confirm who files the re-roof permit and how you will get the number.
  • Look for drip edge and vent work in the written scope.
  • Get the shingle brand, line and color in writing for your HOA.
  • Ask how the roofer prices damaged decking and whether you see photos first.
  • Request a liability insurance certificate from the roofer's insurer.

A college town that rewrote its roofing rules

Hiring a roofer in Denton, TX means dealing with one of the more detailed re-roof processes in Denton County. Since June 1, 2022, the city has required a permit for every residential roof or re-roof project, not just new construction.

Denton is the county seat and, with 139,869 residents in the 2020 census, the 20th largest city in Texas. Two universities shaped it: the school that became the University of North Texas opened in 1890, and Texas Woman's University followed in 1901. Around them and the courthouse square sit some of the oldest houses in the county, while newer neighborhoods spread out from the interstates.

That mix shows up on roofs. A house near the square may sit in a historic district with its own review. A rental near campus may have a landlord deciding when the roof gets replaced. A newer house on the edge of town may just need its hail claim sorted out. DFW Expert Roofers is a free phone line, not a contractor: it connects you with an independent, locally owned roofing company that takes Denton calls, and that company handles the permit.

What the Denton roof permit checks

The city's roofing page sets out more than a fee. These are the points a homeowner can see and ask about.

Permit before tear-off

The permit must be obtained before work starts, and the city counts tearing off the old roof as work. It should be posted where it can be seen from the street.

A registered contractor

The roofer registers with the city as a general contractor through eTRAKiT. First-time registration is done in person with photo ID, and it lasts one year.

Decking gets its own permit

If rotten or damaged decking is replaced, the city also requires a Residential Alteration Permit, and the decking is inspected before it is covered.

An attic look

Denton requires an attic interior inspection as part of the process. Plan for an inspector to need access to the attic hatch.

Drip edge on shingle roofs

Drip edge is required on asphalt shingle roofs. Ask the roofer to list it on the estimate so you know it is included.

A new material means review

Switching from shingles to metal, for example, triggers a plan review before approval. Solar panels pulled and reset need a licensed electrician named on the permit.

The October 2025 fee schedule lists the residential reroof permit at $50 and general contractor registration at $66. Development Services is at 401 N. Elm Street, (940) 349-8600. Confirm current fees with the city, since schedules change.

Houses in Oak-Hickory, West Oak and Bell Avenue

Denton has three local historic districts: Oak-Hickory, the West Oak Area and Bell Avenue. Inside them, and on any designated local landmark, Recorded Texas Historic Landmark or National Register landmark, the city requires a Certificate of Appropriateness before exterior repairs or alterations. Roofs are part of that. Historic Landmark Commission agendas show roof replacement requests, including a 2013 approval for a house on West Hickory Street and a 2014 request for a landmark house on Bolivar Street.

Beginning January 1, 2026, the city put certificate applications on a fixed submittal calendar. In practice that means a roof replacement in one of these districts cannot always start the week the contract is signed. If your house is in a district, tell the roofer at the first visit, ask whether the material, color or profile is changing, and check with the city's historic preservation staff before you choose shingles.

What older Denton houses can hide

Houses built many decades ago were not always framed or decked the way a modern shingle roof expects. Some older homes carry more than one layer of roofing, or decking that needs replacing once the old layers come off. Nobody can price that from the driveway. Ask how the roofer will handle it if the deck turns out to be bad, how replacement boards are priced, and whether you will get photos before they are covered. In Denton that decking also needs its own permit and inspection, so it should never be a surprise line on the final bill.

Storms on record in Denton

Denton sits in the part of North Texas where spring supercells regularly drop large hail. Two events stand out in the official record:

Six questions for a Denton roofer

  • Under what business name are you registered with the City of Denton, and is that registration current?
  • Will the permit be issued before tear-off, and where will you post it?
  • If decking needs replacing, will you add the Residential Alteration Permit and show me photos?
  • Is drip edge on this estimate?
  • Is my house in Oak-Hickory, West Oak or Bell Avenue, and who files the Certificate of Appropriateness?
  • For a rental: will the owner or the property manager sign the contract and receive the warranty paperwork?

From 1,350 people to one of the nation's fastest-growing cities

Melissa, in Collin County north of McKinney, had 1,350 residents in the 2000 census, 4,695 in 2010 and 13,901 in 2020. Census Bureau estimates for 2024 to 2025 placed it among the five fastest-growing U.S. cities with at least 20,000 people.

Growth that fast means many Melissa homes have roofs that are still on their first set of shingles. Those roofs will need repairs after storms and, in time, full replacement, and new residents often have no roofer they already know.

That is the gap DFW Expert Roofers fills. It is a free phone line, not a roofing company. The call goes to an independent, owner-run roofing company that takes calls in Melissa, and that company handles everything on the roof.

Melissa's contractor registration, step by step

  1. Register before working. The city requires anyone providing contractor services inside city limits to register with the City of Melissa.
  2. Apply online. Registration runs through the city's Camino portal, where contractors create an account and upload their documents.
  3. Provide the paperwork. A 2021 city notice lists a copy of the responsible party's driver's license, the contractor's liability insurance policy and any master licenses the trade requires.
  4. Renew every calendar year. Registration does not carry over from last year. A roofer registered in 2025 needs a fresh registration for work in 2026.
  5. Then the permit. The city issues a permit only after the contractor is registered, and every permit ends with a final inspection.

City Hall is at 3411 Barker Avenue and takes building questions at 972-838-2036.

Storm records for a young city

Melissa does not have a long, well documented storm history of its own; it was a town of about 1,350 people until recently, and the Weather Service summaries found for this page did not single it out. Collin County regularly sees spring hail and severe wind, so plan as if the next storm will reach your street.

After hail, look from the ground at gutters, downspouts and metal vents for fresh dents, check where downspouts empty for loose shingle granules, and look at ceilings for new stains. Note the date and take photos. Then have a roofer inspect before you decide anything about a claim.

Checklist before you hire in Melissa

  • Confirm the company is registered with the City of Melissa for this calendar year.
  • Ask who will apply for the permit and when the final inspection will happen.
  • Get the shingle brand, line and color in writing, along with your HOA's approval if you have one.
  • Ask how damaged decking is found, photographed and priced.
  • Have the roofer's insurer send you a current certificate of coverage.

Train and grain town, hail country

Roofing in Saginaw, TX gets a lot of attention after hail, and there is history behind that. On March 28, 2000, the storms that produced the downtown Fort Worth tornado also dropped hail that the National Weather Service says severely damaged roofs and cars in Lake Worth and Saginaw.

Saginaw itself grew up on the rail line north of Fort Worth. The city's own history describes settlers arriving in 1848, the railroad making Saginaw a stop on routes into Fort Worth, and the "train and grain" heritage that followed. It incorporated in 1949 and today is a home rule city in Tarrant County.

When a storm passes, expect two kinds of visitors: the roofers you call and the ones who come knocking. Saginaw has rules for the second group, covered below. DFW Expert Roofers belongs to neither. When a roofer knocks on a Saginaw door

Saginaw licenses door-to-door selling. These are the city's own conditions, and they give you an easy first check.

A city badge

Solicitors must hold a Saginaw solicitation permit and wear the permit badge. Ask to see it. No badge is a reason to close the door.

Set hours

Soliciting is allowed from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. April through October, and 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. November through March. A knock outside those hours breaks the city's rules.

Signs mean no

Permit holders must respect No Soliciting signs and stay out of the roadway and medians. The city says breaking the rules can cost a solicitor the license.

Who to ask

The City Secretary's Office handles solicitation permits and questions about them. Call the city if a knocker's badge or story does not add up.

Look the roofer up before you sign

A badge only means someone may knock. It says nothing about roofing. For that, Saginaw offers a second tool: a published Registered Contractor List with a roofing category, kept by the city alongside its contractor registration forms.

  1. Get the exact company name from the roofer's card or estimate.
  2. Find it on the city's Registered Contractor List under roofing.
  3. Call Building Inspections and Permits at 817-232-4640 to confirm, because the list is a snapshot.
  4. Ask who files the permit. Saginaw's permit forms include a roof category, and forms must be signed before the city accepts them.

The first hour after hail in Saginaw

Keep both feet on the ground, and give any fallen wire a wide berth. Photograph hail on the ground before it melts, then dented gutters, vents and any ceiling stain inside. Write down the date and time. Call your insurance company to ask how wind and hail claims work on your policy and what your deductible is, then have a roofer look. Many roofers inspect storm damage at no charge; ask about that on the call.

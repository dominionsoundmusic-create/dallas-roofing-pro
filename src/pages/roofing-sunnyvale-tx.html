Four hamlets and a one-acre rule

Roofing in Sunnyvale, TX looks different from roofing in the denser suburbs around it, and the reason goes back to 1953.

That year, four rural communities in eastern Dallas County, Long Creek, New Hope, Hattersville and Tripp, joined to form the Town of Sunnyvale. The name came from a contest at the Tripp-Long Creek school; the students' first choice, Sunnyville, was already taken. The new town allowed only single-family houses on one-acre lots, which set the pattern for much of the town. Sunnyvale had 2,693 residents in 2000 and 5,130 in 2010.

Where lots are big, there are often more trees close to houses, longer driveways, and often a barn, shop or carport with a roof of its own. A roofer quoting in Sunnyvale may be asked about all of them at once.

Sunnyvale re-roof permits and the roofing inspection

Apply online

The town requires every permit application to be completed online through its portal. Its residential fee schedule lists a roof and re-roof permit.

The inspector looks up close

Sunnyvale's inspection list has a roofing stage: underlayment, insulation, crickets, flashing and the finished roof.

Current codes and fees

The town builds to the 2021 International Codes with local amendments. A new fee ordinance took effect September 8, 2025, so ask for current amounts.

The town's fee list also carries a contractor registration line for general contractors. Ask your roofer whether it is set up with Sunnyvale and who will apply for the permit.

Where the 2015 tornado began

On the evening of December 26, 2015, a tornado formed in Sunnyvale and tracked northeast into Garland and Rowlett. In town it caused extensive damage to the Plantation RV park. By the time it crossed Interstate 30 in Garland it had reached EF-4 strength, and the National Weather Service in Fort Worth named it for all three places: the Sunnyvale-Garland-Rowlett tornado.

Storms like that are rare. Hail and straight-line wind come far more often, and on a one-acre lot the risk includes limbs from mature trees landing on the roof. After a storm, check the roof from the ground, photograph any damage, and call the insurer before permanent repairs.

Before hiring a roofer in Sunnyvale

  • Will you apply for the Town of Sunnyvale permit and tell me when the roofing inspection passes?
  • Where does my roof need crickets or new flashing, and is that in the written price?
  • Do you also quote barns, shops or carports, and in what material?
  • Which trees should I have trimmed back from the roof by a tree service before you start?
  • Who writes your liability policy, and will the insurer send me the certificate?

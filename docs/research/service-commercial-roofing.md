# Research: /commercial-roofing/ (searched Oct 14 2026)

- NRCA: inspections at least twice a year, preferably spring and fall, plus after severe weather (hail, tornado); NRCA does not recommend owners or property staff walk roofs without experience and proper equipment; hire an experienced roofing contractor for repairs.
  https://www.professionalroofing.net/Articles/Tech-Today--10-01-2013/2338
  https://www.professionalroofing.net/Articles/A-new-resource-for-consumers--11-01-2020/4787
- Membranes (WBDG / UFC 3-110-03): BUR = asphalt built-up plies; modified bitumen = SBS or APP polymer-modified asphalt, reinforced with fiberglass, polyester or both; EPDM single-ply; PVC must be modified with plasticizers; TPO and PVC are thermoplastic single plies (heat-welded seams).
  https://www.wbdg.org/api/documents/media/3436e098-6883-4c0c-80f6-b2bd14950736/file
- 2021 IECC C402.3: low-sloped roofs directly above cooled conditioned spaces in climate zones 0 to 3: 3-year aged solar reflectance 0.55 and 3-year aged thermal emittance 0.75, or 3-year aged SRI 64; exemptions incl. PV areas, vegetative roofs, equipment, shaded roofs, roofs ballasted with stone or pavers; CRRC-S100 test method.
  https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IECC2021P2/chapter-4-ce-commercial-energy-efficiency/IECC2021P2-CE-Ch04-SecC402.3
- NCTCOG 2021 IECC regional amendments cover the region in IECC climate zones 2 and 3. Dallas Building Inspection staff recommended adopting the 2021 IECC with NCTCOG amendments.
  https://www.nctcog.org/getmedia/614a5f3e-1ddb-451c-ba8e-e22316157f15/2021-IECC-Amendments-Draft.pdf?ext=.pdf
  https://dallascityhall.com:443/departments/sustainabledevelopment/buildinginspection/DCH%20documents/pdf/code_amendments/Draft_Memo_for_Dallas_I-Codes_IECC_LDSJ.pdf
- CRRC: ENERGY STAR roof products spec sunset June 1, 2022; CRRC rated products directory.
  https://coolroofs.org/news/energy-star-roofing-program-sunsets
- Dallas permits: re-roof permit for replacing large areas or the whole roof; minor repairs none (storm page). Commercial applications at Permit Center, Oak Cliff Municipal Center, 320 E. Jefferson Blvd., Room 118; registered contractor required on the application. Chapter 52 amendments (2015): reroof permit application lists debris disposal sites; final inspection needs proof of disposal at a City of Dallas landfill or transfer station.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx

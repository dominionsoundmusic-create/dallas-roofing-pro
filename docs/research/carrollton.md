# Carrollton research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Counties: Carrollton lies in parts of Dallas, Denton and Collin counties. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrollton,_Texas ; Handbook of Texas entry (TSHA, Joan Jenkins Perez) reposted at https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/dallas/towns-communities/TSHA/tsha-carrollton.htm
- Growth: population 1,610 (1950), 4,242 (1960), 13,855 (1970), 40,595 (1980, up 193 percent); almost three-quarters of the city's year-round housing units were built during the 1970s (Handbook of Texas, as written in that entry). 133,434 in 2020 census (Wikipedia).
- Re-roof permit (city Re-Roofing page): permits are required only when structural members supporting the roofing system are replaced (roof deck or sheathing); replacing the roof covering (shingles, underlayment, metal panels and similar) does not require a permit; replacement decking may be the same thickness as the existing decking. https://www.cityofcarrollton.com/departments/departments-a-f/building-inspection/my-home/re-roofing
- Contractor registration: all contractors, including the general contractor, must register and stay current with the city; permits are not issued until all contractors are registered; registration through CityServe, renewable annually, free, with a copy of government photo ID. https://www.cityofcarrollton.com/departments/departments-a-f/building-inspection/submittal-requirements ; https://www.cityofcarrollton.com/about-us/stay-connected/cityserve-permits-inspections
- Homeowner on homestead may do own work (state law), per city remodel page; Building Inspection phone 972-466-3225. https://www.cityofcarrollton.com/departments/departments-a-f/building-inspection/my-home/remodels-additions-to-single-family-dwellings
- June 6 2018 hailstorm (early morning): CBS News: damage trail from Carrollton to Coppell to Arlington; largest hail reports came from Coppell and Carrollton per NWS; Carrollton police confirmed significant property damage in the western part of the city, along Hebron Parkway and State Highway 121. Insurance Council of Texas preliminary: about 20,000 structures and 25,000 vehicles, about $425 million insured loss (metro-wide). Karen Clark & Co. later near $1 billion; most damage expected to roofs, windows, skylights and solar panels. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/severe-storms-baseball-size-hail-hit-north-texas/ ; https://www.businessinsurance.com/texas-hailstorm-insured-damage-estimated-at-1-billion/
- After the June 6 2018 storm, the city sent a release warning residents to be careful with roofers going door to door; BBB said contact your insurer before agreeing to a deal. https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/carrollton-says-beware-of-roofing-scams/235149/
- Neighborhood: Josey Ranch area near Josey Lane, with Josey Ranch Lake Library and Josey Ranch athletic complex. https://www.redfin.com/neighborhood/50098/TX/Carrollton/Josey-Ranch
- Electric: Oncor default (macro wording "Oncor or your local electric utility" left as is).

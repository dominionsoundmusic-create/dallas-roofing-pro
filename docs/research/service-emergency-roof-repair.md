# Research: /emergency-roof-repair/ (searched Oct 14 2026)

- Oncor outage line 888-313-4747 (Oncor outage FAQ). For a downed line Oncor says leave the area and call 911; 911 contacts Oncor; never approach or touch a downed line or anything touching it.
  https://www.oncor.com/us/en/home/faqs/faq-outages.html
- TDI storm recovery: make temporary repairs to prevent more damage; cover holes to keep rain out; keep a list of repairs and save receipts; photos before temporary repairs; do not make permanent repairs before the adjuster sees the damage; do not throw damaged items away until the adjuster says so. TDI FAQ: most policies cover the cost of necessary temporary repairs (ask the insurer).
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/recoverytips.html
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/after-hail-or-windstorms.html
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/when-are-water-damage-and-mold-covered-by-insurance.html
- City of Dallas storm damage page: no permit needed for temporary measures like tarps; minor roof repairs need no permit; replacing large areas or the whole roof needs a re-roof permit; structural damage to joists/rafters needs a building permit. Questions 214-948-4480.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx
- Tarp cost (cost sites, national): Angi average about $450, range $150 to $3,300; HomeAdvisor: emergency tarping runs 50% to 100% more than scheduled; HomeGuide: tarp lasts 60 to 90 days on average.
  https://www.angi.com/articles/cost-to-tarp-roof.htm  https://www.homeadvisor.com/cost/roofing/cost-to-tarp-a-roof/  https://homeguide.com/costs/roof-tarp-cost
- City of Dallas Home Improvement and Preservation Program (HIPP): run by Volunteers of America Texas since Feb 2026; application window Oct 5 to 16, 2026; forgivable loan up to $24,000 for necessary repairs incl. roofing; low and moderate income owners.
  https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/TXDALLAS/bulletins/42e292d
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/housing-neighborhood-revitalization/Pages/Repair-Programs.aspx
- Fort Worth Priority Repair Program (Neighborhood Services): June 2025 policy: repairs at no charge up to $25,000; leaking roofs covered subject to conditions; one whole roof replacement per lifetime of the home except major weather events; homestead; very low and low income; repairs limited to once every 12 months; 817-392-7548; long waits possible.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/neighborhoods/services/priority-repair
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/4/neighborhoods/documents/public-notice/prp-policies-and-procedures-june-2025.pdf
- USDA Section 504 (Texas page): loans to low and very-low income owner occupants who cannot get affordable credit elsewhere, max $40,000, 20 years, 1% fixed; grants to owners 62+ with very low income, max $10,000, to remove health and safety hazards; combined up to $50,000; grant repaid if sold within 3 years; rural area eligibility checked by address.
  https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/single-family-housing-programs/single-family-housing-repair-loans-grants-5

# Flower Mound research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Town (not city) incorporated February 25, 1961; reportedly to avoid annexation by Irving; counties Denton and Tarrant (mainly Denton); 2020 census 75,956. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flower_Mound,_Texas
- Permit: Town's Residential Permit Guidelines: to have a roof replaced, a permit must be obtained, and roof replacement or recovering must comply with the Town's currently adopted codes. https://www.flower-mound.com/residentialpermits ; Building info https://www.flower-mound.com/FMpermits
- Town HOA Resources Guide lists re-roofs among common permitted projects. https://flower-mound.com/DocumentCenter/View/35265/HOA-Resources-Packet
- Contractor registration: anyone performing work regulated by Chapter 14 of the Code of Ordinances must apply for required permits and register with the Town; registrations expire 1 year from application; Town publishes a registered contractors list; permits through eTRAKiT (contractor must already be registered). Building Inspections 972-874-6355. https://flower-mound.com/contractors ; https://etrakit.flower-mound.com/
- Trees: Town tree preservation ordinance goals include 35 percent canopy, protecting specimen trees; post oaks (Cross Timbers) a recurring topic; council approved tree removal permits (e.g., 117 specimen trees on Smith Tract, May 2025). https://communityimpact.com/flower-mound-highland-village-argyle/government/flower-mound-council-approves-tree-permit-conservation-district-zoning/ ; https://www.greensourcetexas.org/articles/alton-bowmans-30-year-fight-save-trees-flower-mound
- June 11, 2023 evening hail: NBC DFW reader photo gallery of large hail from Flower Mound and Argyle (2 to 3 inch hail shortly after 8 p.m. per a contributor). Spotter reports logged about 3-inch hail south of Bartonville and a social-media report of 4.25-inch hail near Cross Timbers Rd and Shiloh Rd in Flower Mound (StormerSite, aggregating NWS local storm reports). https://www.nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/your-pictures-of-large-hail-in-sunday-evening-storms-june-11-2023/3275335/ ; https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/flower_mound_texas/all
- March 23, 2016 hail swath near Lewisville to east of Wylie (CoreLogic via Insurance Journal); spotter report 1.25 in hail in Flower Mound that night (StormerSite). https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2016/07/25/420542.htm
- Electric: both Oncor and CoServ serve parts of Flower Mound. https://electricrates.org/texas/flower-mound/ ; CoServ service area includes parts of Denton and Tarrant counties.

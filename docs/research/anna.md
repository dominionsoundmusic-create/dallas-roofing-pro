# Anna research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-anna-tx/ . Target: roofing anna tx (10).

## Verified
- County: Collin. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna,_Texas
- 2020 census: 16,896 (Wikipedia). Census estimates for 2024 to 2025 put Anna in the top five fastest-growing US cities of 20,000+ (with Celina, Princeton, Melissa). https://celinaedc.com/about/news-press-releases/population-rebounds-for-many-cities-in-northeast-and-midwest
- History: Houston and Texas Central Railway passed through in 1873 (city history); Anna platted 1883 with a population of twenty; incorporated 1913. https://www.annatexas.gov/700/Anna-History ; Wikipedia.
- Roofing requirements (City of Anna Development Services, 120 W. 7th Street): all work involving new roofs and re-roofing must be permitted unless the repair is 3 squares or less; homeowner may do the work only on a homestead property with a homestead exemption on the appraisal district record; otherwise roofing permits only issued to a registered general contractor; application must describe all work, including decking or rafter replacement; if damaged decking is found after tear-off, contact the city to modify the permit; tear off and new underlayment required; contractor responsible for reconnecting gas and plumbing vents. https://annatexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2965/Roofing-Requirements ; FAQ: permit required for a reroof https://www.annatexas.gov/Faq.aspx?TID=65
- Contractor registration: general contractor registration $100; liability insurance of $500,000 with the City of Anna as certificate holder; valid one year; no construction permits without contractor registration (fee schedule). https://annatexas.gov/FormCenter/Planning-and-Development-4/Contractor-Registration-Application-non--241 ; https://www.annatexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/3964/Fee-Schedule-Building-Department
- Building Department: 120 W. 7th St, Ste 142; 972-924-2616; Mon to Fri 8 to 5 (from search summary of city pages).
- Door-to-door: Anna requires a permit for door-to-door sales. After a hailstorm, NBC DFW reported one homeowner solicited by roofers at least 30 times; police said they usually see about one solicitor application a month but received 70 in two days, all from roof and hail repair companies; police checking permits and shutting down those who do not comply. Article date not shown. https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/anna-storm-victims-door-to-door-repair-solicitors/3922828/
- Storms: May 2006, spotters reported tornadoes north and northeast of McKinney near Anna and Westminster (NWS). https://www.weather.gov/fwd/collintor06

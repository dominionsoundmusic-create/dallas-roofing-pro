## Life spans by material
- InterNACHI Standard Estimated Life Expectancy Chart: asphalt 3-tab 20 years; architectural 30; metal 40 to 80;
  clay or concrete 100+; wood 25. Figures assume regular maintenance and normal wear; guidelines only, not warranties.
  https://www.nachi.org/life-expectancy.htm
- InterNACHI "How to Perform Roof Inspections" (2012): table of average service lifespan of roof coverings, 3-tab 20,
  architectural 30, wood shakes and shingles 30, metal 40 to 80.
  https://www.nachi.org/documents2012/How_to_Perform_Roof_Inspections_SECOND_EDITION-revd-Oct-2012.pdf
- InterNACHI also publishes a separate hot-climate chart with much shorter shingle figures (3-tab 10 to 12, architectural
  15 to 20). NOT named on the page (it is for another state; site rule), only described as "hot-climate editions of
  such charts run shorter" without a citation. Decided: left out entirely to stay clean.
- NAHB / Bank of America Home Equity "Study of Life Expectancy of Home Components" (Feb 2007): telephone survey of
  manufacturers, trade groups, researchers; life depends on installation quality, maintenance, weather and climate,
  intensity of use; authors call findings general guidance. Roofing figures NOT verified (search result did not show
  them), so no NAHB number is used. https://standards.nsf.org/higherlogic/ws/public/download/348/NAHB%202007%20StudyUseful%20Life%20of%20Home%20Components.pdf

## Warranty versus life
- Professional Roofing (NRCA magazine) "30 years to life": stated warranty length combines non-prorated and prorated
  periods; "lifetime" warranty does not promise satisfactory performance. https://professionalroofing.net/articles/article/1913
- GAF Golden Pledge brochure: standard lifetime shingle warranty has 10 years non-prorated (materials and labor for
  manufacturing defects); after 10 years labor not included and materials prorated; "lifetime" = as long as the original
  owner owns the single-family home; top tier extends non-prorated to 50 years.
  https://www.gaf.com/en-us/document-library/documents/brochures-&-literature/golden-pledge-limited-warranty-brochure-reswt161b.pdf

## Heat, ventilation, weather (DFW)
- ARMA: ventilation keeps attic temperature closer to outside temperature and helps prevent premature shingle
  deterioration; removes moisture; balanced intake (low) and exhaust (high). https://www.asphaltroofing.org/why-ventilation-is-important/ ;
  https://www.asphaltroofing.org/attic-ventilation/
- IRC R806.2 1/150 net free vent area (from service-roof-replacement.md).
- NWS FWD: 20.2 days a year at 100 F or more (1991 to 2020), nearly all June to September; record 71 in 2011.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/d100info (from service-roof-repair.md)
- NWS FWD normals: about 37 in of rain a year, May wettest (~4.8 in). https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dfw_records_normals
- Hail: NWS severe = 1 inch; Wylie Apr 11 2016 about 80% of homes affected, over $300 million (NWS FWD); 1995 Mayfest.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/20160411 ; https://www.weather.gov/bmx/outreach_svr
- IBHS: sealant strength critical to wind resistance especially after hail; impact ratings. https://ibhs.org/hail/asphalt-shingle-durability/
- NRCA: check roofs twice a year (spring, fall) and after severe weather, owners from the ground only.
  https://www.nrca.net/news-events/press-room/storm-roof-repairs

## Insurance and older roofs
- TDI replacing your roof: check roof coverage at renewal; as roofs age some companies switch to ACV; roof in poor
  condition may not be covered. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/replacing-your-roof.html
- TDI RC vs ACV illustration: $10,000 roof, $200,000 home, $4,000 deductible (2% of $200,000). RC pays $6,000 at any
  age; ACV pays $4,500 (5-year roof), $3,000 (10-year), $0 (20-year).
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/home-insurance-policies-replacement-cost-or-actual-cash-value.html
- Roof payment schedules: some policies pay a sliding percentage of roof replacement cost that falls with roof age
  (credit union consumer article). https://www.texasbaycu.org/articles/roof-coverage-change-on-homeowner-renewals
  (Sample percentages not used.)
- TDI impact-resistant credits, UL 2218, form PC068; TDI Bulletin B-0030-98 cosmetic hail endorsement (shared notes).
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/bulletins/1998/b-0030-8.html
- Property Code 202.011 (shared notes). https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011

## Signs of an aging roof (ground and inside only)
- Amica (insurer) and Angi: granules in gutters (some loss normal on a brand-new roof), curling or cupping, cracked or
  brittle shingles, bald patches, daylight through the decking seen from the attic, clustered signs plus age point to
  replacement. https://www.amica.com/en/resources/home/maintenance/signs-new-roof.html ;
  https://www.angi.com/articles/7-warning-signs-you-need-new-roof.htm

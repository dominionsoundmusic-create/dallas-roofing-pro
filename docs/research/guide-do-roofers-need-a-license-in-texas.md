# Research notes: /do-roofers-need-a-license-in-texas/ (Oct 14 2026)

## State license
- Texas has no state roofing license. HB 3344 (89th Leg., 2025) would have had TDLR license residential re-roofing contractors; committee report exists (cleared House committee), no floor vote, died at session end. https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/analysis/html/HB03344H.htm (verified in WRITING-GUIDE.md)
- Voluntary RCAT "Licensed Roofing Contractor" program: private association credential, not state. Reported requirements (third-party sources, they conflict on details): principal at a Texas roofing firm about two years, general liability ($300,000 residential / $500,000 commercial reported), BBB standing, business/safety exam plus trade exam (70%), annual renewal with CE. Page says only: voluntary, run by a trade association, involves exams and proof of insurance; ask the association to confirm. https://getjobber.com/academy/roofing-license/texas/ ; https://app.labyrinthinc.com/agency/roofing-contractors-association-of-texas

## Deductible and public adjusting
- TDI: contractor may not waive, rebate, absorb a deductible; $1,000+ insurance-paid contract must carry deductible notice (Bus. & Com. Code 27.02); Insurance Code 4102.163 public adjuster rule. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/can-a-contractor-waive-my-deductible.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/Consumer/storms/roofing-and-insurance-know-the-law.html

## Business & Commerce Code Chapter 58 (disaster remediation contracts)
- Applies to disaster remediation contracts after a declared disaster when the contractor has NOT kept a physical business address for at least one year in the county where the property is or an adjacent county (58.002(b)). Where it applies: contractor may not require full or partial payment before work starts; partial payments must be reasonably proportionate to work done, including materials delivered; contract must carry a boldface Chapter 58 statement; violation is a DTPA deceptive act; waiver void. https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.58.htm ; https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._bus._&_com._code_section_58.002

## Property Code 53.259 bills-paid affidavit
- As a condition of final payment on a residential construction contract, the original contractor must sign and deliver an affidavit that everyone who supplied labor or materials was paid, or list who is owed. False affidavit: personal liability, criminal offense. https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_53.259

## Workers' comp
- Texas does not require most private employers to carry workers' comp (non-subscribers); non-subscribers must post notice and file notice with DWC; they can be sued by injured employees. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/WC/nonsubscriber.html

## Door-to-door
- AG (March 2026 alert after ice storm): in many instances a right to cancel a door-to-door contract up to three days after signing; seller must tell you; otherwise contract may be voided; check BBB; don't be rushed; report to Consumer Protection 800-621-0508 or online complaint. https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/news/releases/paxton-issues-consumer-alert-help-inform-texans-their-consumer-rights-following-last-weeks-ice
- AG Hurricane Laura advisory (2020) — not used (non-DFW title).
- McKinney: police page says a permit is required anytime someone solicits within the city; permit lasts six months (hours only from third-party source: left out). https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1400/Solicitors-Permit
- Frisco: city says it suspended the permit requirement for soliciting funds/handbills in Aug 2016 (left out: unclear for sales).

## City permits and registration (from teammates' verified town notes, URLs on the cities' own sites)
- Dallas: re-roof permit for replacing large areas or whole roof; registered contractor required on the application; homeowner exemption form. https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx
- Fort Worth: anyone getting a building permit must hold Contractor Registration (homestead owner exception); shingle-only replacement on 1/2-family home needs no permit, decking/structural replacement does. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/contractor-registration ; https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/residential-information
- Plano: permit required for all re-roofs; roof companies register as General Contractors ($100 annual); city publishes a list of active registered general contractors. https://www.plano.gov/220/Building-Inspections
- Arlington: roofing permits required since Nov 1 2011; roofing contractors must register to get a permit; registration searchable on ArlingtonPermits.com. https://www.arlingtontx.gov/City-Services/Permits
- Garland: permit for roof repairs valued $2,000+; contractor registers as General Contractor with insurance listing the city as certificate holder, minimum $300,000 general liability. https://www.garlandtx.gov/3186/Re-Roofing
- Grand Prairie: re-roof permit for any roof replacement, recover or repair; GC registration. https://www.gptx.org/Departments/Building-Inspections/Contractor-Registration-Forms/General-Contractor-Registration
- Allen: contractors register ($50/yr) to be added to a permit. https://www.cityofallen.org/departments/community_development/building_and_permitting/contractor_registration.php
- Richardson: all contractors register with Building Inspection ($100, one year); permit for more than 25% of roof. https://www.cor.net/departments/building-inspection/contractor-registration

## Warranty registration
- GAF: even an included warranty must be activated by contractor or homeowner within a set period after install; enhanced warranties usually need same-manufacturer components and an enrolled contractor; homeowner should get confirmation. https://www.gaf.com/en-us/blog/your-home/interested-in-a-roof-warranty-heres-what-to-know-281474980319534

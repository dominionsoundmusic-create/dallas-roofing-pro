# Lancaster, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing lancaster tx (10). Page: /roofing-lancaster-tx/

## County / city
- Incorporated city in Dallas County, about 15 minutes south of Dallas; 41,275 residents in 2020.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster,_Texas
- Settled in the 1840s (city site says first settlement September 1844 by Roderick Rawlins; markers say 1852,
  so no year is used on the page). Rival community Pleasant Run (stage stop, school, steam grist mill) faded
  after 1860. Historic Town Square was the center of nearly all business until after World War II; wooden
  buildings burned in 1877 and 1889, brick followed. Lancaster Historic Neighborhood District markers.
  Sources: https://tx-lancaster.civicplus.com/91/History-of-the-City ; https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=300844
- Electric: Oncor assumed (generic safety box, not separately verified).

## Permits and registration (City of Lancaster)
- Registered contractors apply for permits, check status and request inspections in the online portal;
  permit technicians (972) 218-1201 or (972) 218-1205.
  Source: https://www.lancaster-tx.com/258/Online-Permit-and-Inspection-Portal
- Fee schedule FY 2025-2026: Roofing Permit $100.00; building/general contractor registration $100 a year.
  Source: https://www.lancaster-tx.com/DocumentCenter/View/19266/Master-Fee-2025-2026
- Contractor registration form: Roofing Contractor is among registration types; registration valid one year;
  minimum $500,000 general liability, City named as certificate holder; notarized if someone else registers or
  by mail. Source: https://lancaster-tx.com/DocumentCenter/View/13079/Contractor-Registration?bidId=
- Development handbook: builders and subcontractors must be registered with the City; questions to the Building
  Division (972) 218-1200; City Hall 211 N. Henry Street. Permit copy posted on job site.
  Source: https://www.lancaster-tx.com/DocumentCenter/View/18286/Development-Handbook---Updated-08-19-2024

## Storm history
- April 25, 1994, 9:38 p.m.: one or more tornadoes through the heart of Lancaster; three deaths; marker says over
  200 homes and 26 businesses destroyed or severely damaged; Junior high, library, City Hall damaged. AP/Red Cross
  preliminary: 922 Lancaster homes affected, 173 destroyed, 192 major damage (counts differ, page uses marker).
  Sources: https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=300488 ; https://www.dallasobserver.com/?p=40491566
- April 3, 2012: NWS Fort Worth survey: EF-2 (NOT EF3, the EF3 that day was the Forney tornado in Kaufman County; the Royse City tornado was EF-2 (fc8 check)), about 130 mph,
  200 yards wide, 7.1 miles. Began east of I-35E and Parkerville Road, moved northeast, worst damage near
  Wintergreen Road, Telephone Road and North Dallas Road, crossed I-20 (trucking facility), lifted near Bonnie View
  Road and Memory Lane in Dallas. Source: https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012lancasterdallas ;
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012sum
- 2012 mayor interview: last bad tornado was 1994. Source: https://www.npr.org/2012/04/04/149982494/tornadoes-outside-dallas-indescribable-mayor-says

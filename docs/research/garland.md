## County and city
- City of Garland, mostly Dallas County; parts in Collin and Rockwall counties (site.json). Firewheel: majority in Dallas County, small portion in Collin County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewheel,_Garland,_Texas

## Growth and housing age
- Post-war boom: about 38,500 people by 1960, doubled to about 81,500 by 1970; city limits stretched east toward Lake Ray Hubbard through annexation. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garland,_Texas
- Firewheel Golf Park built 1983 (public course, city of Garland). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewheel_Golf_Park
- Firewheel area named after the golf park; neighborhoods developed principally from the late 1990s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewheel,_Garland,_Texas
- Firewheel Town Center: open-air regional mall, opened Oct 7 2005, NE corner of President George Bush Turnpike and SH 78. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewheel_Town_Center
- So: south and central Garland is largely 1950s to 1970s housing; Firewheel in the north is late 1990s onward.

## Permits and contractor registration (City of Garland)
- Re-Roofing page: permit needed for roof repairs valued at $2,000 or more; fee listed as $175 ($140 + $35 processing; confirm current). Must supply a copy of the executed contract or the insurance adjuster's scope. Up to two layers of shingles allowed (2015 IRC cited); a third layer needed means tear off and start over. Changing roof material needs a permit; metal roofing 26 gauge or better with manufacturer specifications. https://www.garlandtx.gov/3186/Re-Roofing
- Roofers register as General Contractors (category includes roofing contractors) with proof of insurance listing the City of Garland as certificate holder; minimum $300,000 general liability; owner's or principal's driver's license. Building Inspections Dept., 800 Main St, 972-205-2300. https://www.garlandtx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2638/Contractor-Registration-Application-PDF
- Building Inspection department page. https://www.garlandtx.gov/221/Building-Inspection

## Storm history
- Dec 26 2015 Sunnyvale-Garland-Rowlett tornado, rated EF-4 (Garland portion EF-4, Rowlett portion EF-3), about 13 miles long, up to 550 yards wide; NWS estimated peak 170 to 180 mph. Most deaths near I-30 and President George Bush Turnpike. NWS Fort Worth: highest-rated and deadliest December tornado in Dallas County; climatology lists 9 deaths in Garland and 1 in Rowlett.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dec26tornadoes ; https://www.weather.gov/media/fwd/dec26outbreak/products/pns.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/December_2015_North_American_storm_complex
- Hail: SPC/StormerSite reports list 1 inch hail in Garland May 6 2025 (aggregator; not used on page).

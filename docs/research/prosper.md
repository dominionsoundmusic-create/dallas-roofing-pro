# Prosper research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-prosper-tx/ . Target: roofing prosper tx (140).

## Verified
- County: Collin and Denton (western Collin, eastern Denton). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prosper,_Texas ; https://citypopulation.de/en/usa/places/texas/collin_denton/4859696__prosper/
- Population: 9,547 (2010, rebased census figure, citypopulation.de); 30,171 to 30,174 (2020). Page says "about 9,500" and "about 30,000".
- Town is "Town of Prosper" (prospertx.gov).
- Contractor registration: fee ordinance 2025-47 says all contractors must register with the Town; $100 registration, $100 annual renewal. Residential roof repair or replacement fee $100. Reinspection fees $50 first, rising in $25 steps. https://www.prospertx.gov/747/Building-Permit-Fees
- Permit Technicians: 972-346-3502, permits@prospertx.gov; registration and inspection requests via Citizens Self Service portal; inspection requests before 7:00 AM cutoff on the day. (Commercial inspection guidelines rev. 04-2025) https://prospertx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4649/Commercial-Inspection-Guidelines
- Re-roof document: drip edge required on all asphalt shingle roofing projects; building final includes interior attic inspection items (attic vent penetrations, appliance vent clearances), exterior verification of roof completion, compliance with 2021 IRC and Town rules, and jobsite debris cleanup. https://prospertx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1671/Re-Roof
- Monthly permit reports list many residential re-roof permits with a $100 fee. https://www.prospertx.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/657
- Electric: Town utility page lists both Oncor and CoServ Electric outage contacts; CoServ has a service center in Prosper. https://prospertx.gov/317/Utility-Service ; https://www.coserv.com/media/page/2
- Windsong Ranch: 2,030-acre master-planned development at 1001 Windsong Parkway South (2018 Varde press release); five-acre lagoon. https://varde.com/texas-real-estate-developers-and-global-alternative-investment-firm-varde-partners-acquire-windsong-ranch-in-prosper-texas
- Storms: March 14 2024, NWS Fort Worth placed the largest hail (about 1.5 to 3 inches) in Denton and Collin counties near places including Frisco and Prosper (Fox 4). https://www.fox4news.com/news/dallas-weather-hail-tornado-march-15 . March 24 2019 golf-ball hail at Dallas Parkway and University Drive came only from StormerSite (aggregator), not used. March 24 2019 Collin County hail generally (Dallas Morning News, allen.md) - not tied to Prosper by that source, not used.

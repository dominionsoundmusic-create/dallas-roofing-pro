# Cleburne, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing cleburne tx (50). Page: /roofing-cleburne-tx/

## County / city
- County seat of Johnson County; 31,352 residents at the 2020 census; county 179,927 in 2020.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleburne,_Texas ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnson_County,_Texas
- Founded near Buffalo Creek as Camp Henderson in 1862, incorporated 1871 and renamed for Patrick R. Cleburne.
  Lake Pat Cleburne, named for him too, supplies water to the city. Santa Fe railroad shops became the
  largest employer for about a century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleburne,_Texas

## Historic housing
- Cleburne Downtown Historic District (National Register): 89 contributing and 38 noncontributing buildings,
  period of significance 1877 to 1968. Source: https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/NR/pdfs/100002844/100002844.pdf
- Joiner-Long House National Register nomination: Prairie Avenue still lined with substantial homes from the
  late 19th and early 20th centuries. Source: https://atlas.thc.state.tx.us/NR/pdfs/03000558/03000558.pdf
  (Whether Prairie Avenue itself became a district: NOT verified, not claimed.)

## Permits / registration (City of Cleburne)
- Quick Guide to Residential Construction (Oct 7, 2025): a roofing permit is required for re-decking an
  existing roof and can be pulled by a contractor registered with the City of Cleburne; fee listed "Roofing
  (only decking)" $30 plus a $20 administrative fee per permit. All contractors must be registered with the
  city; registration $100. Permit valid 180 calendar days to first inspection.
  Source: https://cleburne.net/DocumentCenter/View/16123/Quick-Guide-to-Residential-Construction-Booklet
- FAQ: general contractors and all subcontractors must be registered, current and listed on the permit
  before issuance. Source: https://www.cleburne.net/DocumentCenter/View/54/FAQs
- Online portal cityworks.cleburne.net/BuildingPermits; inspection requests before 4 p.m. for next day.
  Building Inspections, City Hall, 10 N. Robinson Street, ground floor, Mon to Fri 8 to 5.
  Sources: https://www.cleburne.net/1343/Apply-for-a-Building-Permit ; http://www.cleburne.net/188/Building-Permits-and-Services
- Shingle-only re-roof without decking: rule NOT stated: page says ask the city.

## Storms
- May 15, 2013 about 9:30 p.m.: EF3 tornado, peak winds about 140 mph, path about 8.5 miles, about 1,060
  yards (0.6 mile) wide; worst damage just east of Lake Pat Cleburne; one of 16 North Texas tornadoes
  confirmed. CBS: hundreds of homes had roofs ripped off; about 600 families affected; mayor declared a local
  disaster. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_May_15%E2%80%9317,_2013 ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/nws-estimated-12-north-texas-tornadoes-some-ef-3-ef-4 ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/cleanup-continues-in-cleburne-from-mile-wide-tornado ;
  https://csmonitor.com/layout/set/amphtml/USA/USA-Update/2013/0516/Severe-Texas-tornadoes-hopscotch-through-rural-communities-killing-six
- Later storm (article references the 13th anniversary of the 2013 tornadoes, i.e. spring 2026): hail up to
  4 inches fell across most of Johnson County; over 1,000 homes lost power, about half in the Cleburne area;
  no injuries. Source: https://www.rockwallheraldbanner.com/storm-damage-reported-across-johnson-county-no-injuries-or-fatalities/article_c24c1129-f1b6-5f33-884e-b109d5eadb23.html

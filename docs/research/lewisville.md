# Lewisville research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Counties: located in Denton County with portions extending into Dallas County; 2020 census 111,822. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewisville,_Texas
- Growth (city history page): about 9,200 people in 1970, almost 25,000 by 1980 (census 9,264 and 24,273); DFW Airport opened 1974 and Lewisville and Flower Mound, north of the airport, grew fast. https://www.cityoflewisville.com/about-lewisville/our-history ; https://www.cityoflewisville.com/about-lewisville/our-history/timeline
- Lake: Corps of Engineers construction began 1948; completed 1954 (city timeline), expanding the Garza-Little Elm Reservoir into Lewisville Lake, 29,592 acres. https://www.cityoflewisville.com/about-lewisville/our-history/timeline
- Permits: city permit list includes "Re-roofs & re-decks". Castle Hills page lists roof replacement among work needing a permit. https://www.cityoflewisville.com/for-business/building-services/building-permits ; https://www.cityoflewisville.com/our-services/castle-hills-residents
- Contractor registration: all contractors must be registered before receiving a building permit; renewed annually; city checks state licenses and insurance through registration where applicable. https://metrics.cityoflewisville.com/departments/building-services
- FAQ: city advises homeowners not to apply for the permit on the contractor's behalf; a homeowner who accepts the permit may be held responsible for correcting non-code work. Permits and registration through MGO Connect; permit line 972-219-3470. https://www.cityoflewisville.com/for-business/building-services/faq
- Castle Hills: full annexation into Lewisville took effect November 15, 2021; Building Services inherited Castle Hills permits. https://www.cityoflewisville.com/our-services/castle-hills-residents
- March 4, 2025: Benjamin Moore distribution facility on SH 121 lost part of its roof and a wall; NWS Fort Worth said damage consistent with about 90 mph winds. https://beta2.communityimpact.com/dallas-fort-worth/lewisville-coppell/business/2025/03/07/lewisville-benjamin-moore-distribution-facility-receives-damage-from-storms
- March 23, 2016: CoreLogic via Insurance Journal: hail 1.5 to 2 inches in a swath nearly 50 miles long from near Lewisville to east of Wylie (Denton, Collin, Dallas counties). https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2016/07/25/420542.htm
- Electric: CoServ Electric has distribution lines around Lake Lewisville (PUC filings, docket 52667); the rest of the city default Oncor. Safety box worded "Oncor or CoServ, whichever serves your street". https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/Documents/52667_175_1373580.PDF

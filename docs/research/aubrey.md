# Aubrey research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- City in Denton County, nickname "Horse Country, U.S.A."; 2010 census 2,595, 2020 census 5,006. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aubrey,_Texas
- Aubrey ISD history: sandy, fertile land and moderate climate drew horse ranchers, leading some to call the area the "horse capital of Texas"; Ray Roberts Dam completed on the Elm Fork of the Trinity in 1986; district expects enrollment to double by 2030-31. https://aubreyisd.net/page/get-to-know-us
- Permits: the city's permit list includes a Trade Permit covering electrical, HVAC, plumbing and roofing. Residential application: general, plumbing, electrical, mechanical and OSSF contractor registration required at submittal (roofers not named). All applications and documents emailed to permitting@aubreytx.gov as one complete PDF; plan reviews typically 10 to 14 business days. Development Services page (dated Aug 17 2026) says the Building Permits office moved to 510 Rockhill Rd. Phone 940-440-9343 (from search summary of city page). https://www.aubreytx.gov/165/Building-Permits ; https://www.aubreytx.gov/329/Development-Services ; https://www.aubreytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1098/Residential-Building-Application-NEW-Codes
- Sandbrock Ranch: master-planned community with Aubrey addresses, about 800 acres, planned for about 2,600 homes; models opened (Denton Record-Chronicle); listing shows a home built 2020; students attend Denton ISD. https://dentonrc.com/business/sandbrock-ranch-in-aubrey-opens-first-three-model-homes/article_b23a7a21-c8d4-5ffb-afa9-366f8d201860.html ; https://www.builderonline.com/?p=8474
- Storm reports (StormerSite listing): 1.75 inch hail near Aubrey April 8, 2024; 2025 listings include "lots of tree damage" in Aubrey. https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/aubrey_texas/2024 ; https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/aubrey_texas/2025

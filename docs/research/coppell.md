# Coppell research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Counties: mainly Dallas County; a small area in the far north extends into Denton County (city expanded north into southern Denton County in the 1970s). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppell,_Texas ; Handbook of Texas repost https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/dallas/towns-communities/TSHA/tsha-coppell.htm
- Growth: 1,751 (1981) to 3,826 (1984) per TSHA; about 16,881 in 1990 (city history figure; census rebased 17,095); about 35,958 in 2000; 42,983 in 2020. Transformed from a small farming village into a suburb in the 1980s and 1990s, driven by DFW Airport; by 2000 almost all residentially zoned land was developed. (Wikipedia, TSHA)
- Permits: city Permits page lists roofing among permits whose requirements come from Building Inspections, 972-304-3500; contractor registration forms to inspect@coppelltx.gov. http://www.coppelltx.gov/residents/residential-services/permits
- Contractor registration: Code Sec. 15-1-2: any person or firm performing work that requires a permit must first register with the City of Coppell as a contractor; homeowner on own homestead may register for own work. https://coppelltx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5585/Article-15-1-Construction-Chapter
- City publishes monthly "Permits Issued by Work Class" reports listing residential reroof permits with scope descriptions (for example: tear-off, new underlayment, drip edge, flashing, architectural asphalt shingles). https://coppelltx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9930/Permits-Issued-All-Types---June-2026
- June 6, 2018 hail: CBS: some of the largest hail reports from Coppell and Carrollton (NWS); NBC DFW: a Coppell homeowner was told the roof, under three years old, had to be replaced along with siding and gutters; ICT expected up to 20,000 home claims and 25,000 auto claims; $425 million preliminary (Artemis); Karen Clark near $1 billion. https://nbcdfw.com/news/local/surprise-hail-storm-damages-homes-cars/76101 ; https://www.artemis.bm/news/2018/06/07/dallas-texas-hailstorm-estimated-to-cause-425m-insured-loss/ ; https://www.businessinsurance.com/texas-hailstorm-insured-damage-estimated-at-1-billion/ ; https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/severe-storms-baseball-size-hail-hit-north-texas/
- Electric: Oncor default.

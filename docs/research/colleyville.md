# Colleyville research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing companies colleyville tx (140/mo, SD 25). Supporting: roofing colleyville tx (50),
roofing colleyville (50, Q). County: Tarrant. Incorporated city.

## Verified
- Contractor registration (City of Colleyville, Contractor Registration page): registration and permitting are
  submitted and processed online through the city's CSS portal; registration is not accepted by email or mail.
  https://www.colleyville.com/government/departments-a-l/building-inspections/contractor-registration
- Reroofs are among the permit types the city tracks and publishes (Permits Issued page).
  https://www.colleyville.com/government/departments-a-l/building-inspections/permits-issued
- Virtual inspections: as of October 1, 2023 the Building Inspections department runs a Virtual Inspections
  program for HVAC replacements, water heater replacements, reroofs, under-slab repairs and irrigation
  installations; the page lists "Eligible Permit Types and Inspection Procedures" and a "Final Roof Inspection
  Waiver". Submittal guidelines effective April 1, 2025 link "Virtual Inspection Protocols" and a "Roof Final
  Inspection Waiver" document. Terms of the waiver NOT seen: page tells readers to ask.
  https://www.colleyville.com/government/departments-a-l/building-inspections
  https://www.colleyville.com/government/departments-a-l/building-inspections/submittal-guidelines-copy
- Building Inspections: 100 Main Street; department 817-503-1031 (forms questions); permit technician line
  817-503-1030. https://www.colleyville.com/government/departments-a-l/building-inspections/contacts
- History (City of Colleyville, History of Colleyville; city budget books): settled mid-1800s; named for Dr.
  Lilburn Howard Colley, who arrived in the 1880s; farmland known for melons, squash and tomatoes in the early
  1900s; incorporated January 1956 with under two square miles and about 250 residents; nearness to DFW Airport
  fueled residential development in the late 1970s and early 1980s; now over 25,000 residents.
  https://colleyville.com/colleyville/history-of-colleyville
  https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2025/Colleyville/files/basic-html/page12.html
- Electric: the city's Utility Services page lists outage reporting for both Oncor and Tri-County (Tri-County
  Electric Cooperative); territory split not mapped. Page tells readers to report downed lines to whichever
  serves their street, or 911. https://www.colleyville.com/residents/utilities-services
- Hail: SPC hail report log for May 27, 2024 includes ping-pong-ball hail reported in Colleyville, with larger
  reports in neighboring Bedford, Hurst and Euless the same night.
  https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/240527_rpts_hail.csv
- HOA: Texas Property Code 202.011 (verified earlier for the Rockwall page): an HOA may not prohibit or restrict
  shingles designed mainly for wind and hail resistance, better heating/cooling efficiency, or solar, if they
  resemble shingles used or authorized in the subdivision.
  https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011

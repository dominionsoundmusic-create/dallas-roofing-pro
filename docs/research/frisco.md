## Verified
- County: Collin and Denton (city lies in both). https://citypopulation.de/en/usa/texas/denton_collin/4827684__frisco/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frisco,_Texas
- Growth: about 33,700 (2000), about 117,000 (2010), 200,509 (2020 census, QuickFacts). citypopulation.de; https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/friscocitytexas/POP010220
- Contractor registration: all contractors who work in Frisco must be registered before permit issuance, with the Building Inspections Division, online through eTRAKiT; submit company and contact info, insurance info and pay fees; proof of general liability insurance must name the City of Frisco as certificate holder; registration gets an "R-Number" that expires the same day as the general liability insurance; status stays pending until documents arrive; contractors should verify status before applying for a permit. https://www.friscotexas.gov/1521/Contractor-Registration-Information ; https://www.friscotexas.gov/Faq.aspx?QID=451 ; https://www.friscotexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/874/Contractor-Registration-Application-PDF
- Residential permits: self-service permit with immediate access for a roof with the same material; other roof permits (changes) go through electronic review. https://www.friscotexas.gov/1696/Residential-Permits
- Storms:
  - April 3 2014: NWS Fort Worth report of golf ball hail that damaged a home's roof and broke two windows near Legacy and SH 121. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/storms040314
  - January 15 2017 (Sunday night): at least 30 Frisco homes damaged, from downed fences to roof damage and broken windows; windows out on an Avondale Drive home; chimney fell on a Blue Lake Drive home; no injuries in Frisco; damage from strong winds. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/storm-damages-more-than-30-homes-in-frisco/
  - May 2023: 1 to 2.5 inch hail in parts of Collin County incl. Frisco and Allen (WFAA, verified in allen.md).
  - March 14 2024 (Thursday): NWS Fort Worth said the largest hail (about 1.5 to 3 inches) fell in Denton and Collin counties near places including Frisco and Prosper; roofs banged up; NWS survey confirmed a brief EF-0 tornado near UNT Frisco, minor damage. https://www.fox4news.com/news/dallas-weather-hail-tornado-march-15 ; NBC DFW: EF-0 in Frisco, max winds 85 mph, path under a quarter mile, minor damage. https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/national-weather-service-confirms-ef-0-tornado-in-frisco-during-thursdays-storm/3490033/

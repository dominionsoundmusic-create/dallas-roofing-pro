# Corinth research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- City in Denton County, part of DFW. 2020 census 22,634 (Wikipedia) / 22,635 (Census QuickFacts base); 2010 19,935; 2023 estimate 23,707. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corinth,_Texas ; https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/corinthcitytexas
- Permits: a permit is required for any major construction in city limits; the city's forms page lists a Roof Permit Application. All new permits and plans go through the Civic Access portal starting March 27, 2025 (older permits stay in eTRAKiT). Inspections scheduled through Civic Access or by phone (940) 498-3273. Permits (940) 498-3207; inspections (940) 498-3270. https://www.cityofcorinth.com/building-services/page/permits ; https://www.cityofcorinth.com/forms ; https://cityofcorinth.com/building-services/page/contact-permits-and-inspections-staff
- Contractor registration: through Civic Access; create an account and email permits@cityofcorinth.com a color copy of driver's license, trade license if applicable, certificate of insurance if applicable; no fee; previously registered contractors must re-register in Civic Access to apply for a new permit. https://www.cityofcorinth.com/planning-development/page/contractor-registration
- 2023 fee ordinance (No. 23-10-19-42) lists Residential Re-roof at $0.04 per square foot (commercial same). https://www.cityofcorinth.com/sites/default/files/fileattachments/development_services/page/9961/ordinance_no._23-10-19-42_-_2023_fee_schedule_amendment.pdf
- City home repair grant program (Ordinance 23-12-07-46): contractors must give at least a one-year written warranty on general repairs and five years on roof repairs, and get all permits from Building Inspections before work. https://www.cityofcorinth.com/sites/default/files/fileattachments/neighborhood_services/page/24808/23-12-07-46.pdf
- Storm reports (StormerSite archive of NWS local storm reports): 2019 64 mph gust measured at the NCTC Corinth campus; 2025 considerable wind damage reported along FM 1385 into the Sutton Fields subdivision (trees and street signs down, construction trailers tipped). https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/corinth_texas/all
- EF-1 tornado (NWS: 85 to 95 mph, half-mile path, 50 yards wide) damaged a car wash near I-35 and Swisher Road in Hickory Creek and the Kensington Square shopping center (NBC DFW headline "in Corinth"). https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/storm-damages-car-wash-shopping-center/174056/ ; https://www.fox4news.com/news/storms-bring-tornado-high-winds-rain-to-north-texas
- March 14, 2024: NWS Fort Worth named this town among places with hail of about 1.5 to 3 inches. https://www.aol.com/live-weather-updates-storm-damage-004426788.html

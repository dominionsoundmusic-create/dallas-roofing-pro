# Highland Village research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- County: Denton. Incorporated February 14, 1963 (53 for, 7 against); 516 residents in 1970, 3,246 in 1980; 15,899 in the 2020 census; on the south side of the far western branch of Lewisville Lake. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highland_Village,_Texas
- Texas Senate resolution: city traces its roots to a small rural farming community on the southern shores of Lake Lewisville. https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/83R/billtext/pdf/SR00134F.pdf
- Permit: city "Do I need a permit for that?" lists replacing a roof among projects that require a permit. https://www.highlandvillage.org/159/Do-I-need-a-permit-for-that
- Registration: city notice/FAQ: ordinance requires roofing contractors to be registered with the City; when a permit is required the contractor must be registered and obtain the permit before work. Who pulls it: usually whoever performs the work, but the owner is ultimately responsible for making sure permits are secured. https://www.highlandvillage.org/faq.aspx?TID=24 ; https://www.highlandvillage.org/Faq.aspx?QID=111
- Permit posted where clearly visible from outside; after roof replacement, homeowner or contractor schedules an inspection; permits not valid until paid, issued and placed on site; online portal for registered contractors, new users register in person at the Building Inspections counter. https://www.highlandvillage.org/160/Permit-Application-Forms ; https://www.highlandvillage.org/1019/Online-Services
- Contractor registration form: $150 fee (reduced to $100 if renewed on or before Oct 31), proof of current liability insurance (or bond / builders risk). Form undated. https://highlandvillage.org/DocumentCenter/View/172/Contractor-Registration-Form
- Building Inspections: 1000 Highland Village Rd., (972) 899-5093.
- June 11, 2023 hail: StormerSite logs 2.75 to 3 inch hail in Highland Village that evening; NBC DFW gallery of large hail from Flower Mound and Argyle the same evening. https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/hickory_creek_texas/all (listing seen in results); https://www.nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/your-pictures-of-large-hail-in-sunday-evening-storms-june-11-2023/3275335/
- Highland Village Police relayed NWS severe thunderstorm warnings (Nixle) in 2017 to 2018 with hail and 60 to 65 mph gusts (not used on page).
- Electric: not verified (CoServ vs Oncor); default safety box wording used.

## Town
- City of Kaufman, county seat of Kaufman County; 2020 census population 6,797. County 145,310 in 2020; Forney is the county's largest city. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaufman,_Texas ; https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/kaufmancitytexas/POP010220
- County established 1848, named for David S. Kaufman (Republic of Texas and US Congress). 1956 modernist courthouse replaced the 1887 courthouse; the 1956 building closed in 2023. https://www.texasescapes.com/CentralTexasTownsNorth/Kaufman-Texas-Kaufman-County-Courthouse.htm ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaufman_County,_Texas

## Permits and registration (City of Kaufman)
- All contractors must be registered with the City of Kaufman before applying for a permit; roofing contractors listed; registration through MyGov; no registration fee. https://www.kaufmantx.org/2171/Building-Safety-Division
- City brochure: major repairs such as replacement of roofs, porches and foundations require permits. https://www.kaufmantx.org/DocumentCenter/View/854/Brochure-Building-Permit-Requirements
- Residential permit checklist asks for contractor name/phone, owner info, Kaufman County Appraisal District property ID, estimated project value. https://www.kaufmantx.org/DocumentCenter/View/2186/Residential-Permit-Submittal-Check-List
- Forms page with contractor registration application. https://www.kaufmantx.org/2214/Forms
- Office address conflicts across documents: left out.

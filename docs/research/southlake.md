## Verified
- Roof permit (City of Southlake, "Roof Replacement: What You Need to Know", June 2025; also Roof Replacement
  Guidelines): the IRC requires a building permit for roof replacement; permit submitted online; permit fee $100
  (fee not printed on page); contractor must register as a general contractor online ($100); permit card must be
  posted at the site in plain view from the road; on completion the contractor requests a final inspection in the
  online portal; inspections by photo or in person; photo requests need photos of the house address, exterior roof
  photos from ground level, and vent photos; permits expire after 180 days with no inspection or activity.
  Questions (817) 748-8236 or Southlake Serves.
  https://www.cityofsouthlake.com/DocumentCenter/View/44787/Roof-Replacement-June-2025
  https://www.cityofsouthlake.com/DocumentCenter/View/45681/Roof-Replacement-Guidelines
- Registration through the online permitting system; general contractors register annually.
  https://www.cityofsouthlake.com/111/Building-Inspections-Code-Enforcement
- City posts monthly permit reports listing permit number, address, value, contractor (e.g., April 2026 report).
  https://www.cityofsouthlake.com/DocumentCenter/View/46813/APR-2026-Monthly-Report
- History (Wikipedia; Southlake Historical Society): area settled in the 1840s; residents voted to incorporate
  September 25, 1956, partly over concern about annexation by Hurst; vote held at the 1919 Carroll School;
  original town 1.62 square miles; name chosen for the nearby lake (Grapevine Lake, completed 1952); Carroll ISD
  created 1959; area stayed rural until DFW Airport opened in the 1970s.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southlake,_Texas
  https://360westmagazine.com/southlake-history-exhibit-traces-citys-origins-and-growth/
- Electric: Tri-County Electric Cooperative serves Southlake members; Aug 2026 Mayor Shawn McCaskill filed a
  complaint with the PUC over repeat TCEC outages (eight outage days April to August); utility officials blamed tree
  branches contacting overhead lines in the Southlake and Keller service areas. TCEC outages: text OUTAGE to 25069
  or call 817-444-3201. Oncor also delivers in the area (outage line 888-313-4747): page says check your bill.
  https://hoodline.com/2026/08/southlake-mayor-files-state-complaint-over-repeat-power-outages/
  https://tcectexas.com/outage-center
- Hail (SPC logs): quarter-size hail 1 mile north of Southlake, April 14, 2011; quarter-size (1.00 in) mPING report
  1 N Southlake, May 27, 2024. https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/110414_rpts_raw_hail.csv
  https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/240527_rpts_hail.csv
- Dec 13 2022 Grapevine EF-1 tornado lifted at the Grapevine Lake shore (see grapevine.md); not in Southlake.
- HOA: Texas Property Code 202.011 (see colleyville.md).

# Research notes: /roof-replacement/ (roof replacement in dallas), Oct 14 2026

- City of Dallas: large areas or a whole roof need a re-roof permit; minor repairs do not; structural members need a building permit; a registered contractor must be on the application; homeowner exemption form for owners doing their own work; 214-948-4480.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx
- IRC R908.3: roof replacement includes removing existing roof coverings down to the deck. R908.3.1.1: a recover (overlay) is not allowed over a roof with two or more layers, over water-soaked or deteriorated roofing, or over slate, clay, cement or asbestos-cement tile.
  https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IRC2021P3/chapter-9-roof-assemblies/IRC2021P3-Pt03-Ch09-SecR908.3.1 ; https://up.codes/s/roof-re-cover
- IRC R806.2: attic net free vent area at least 1/150 of the vented area; a reduced 1/300 ratio only under listed conditions.
  https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IRC2021P3/chapter-8-roof-ceiling-construction/IRC2021P3-Pt03-Ch08-SecR806.2
- IRC R905.2.8.5: drip edge required at eaves and rakes of shingle roofs, at least 1/4 inch below sheathing, fastened 12 inches on center max; underlayment over drip edge at eaves, under at rakes.
  https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2024V2.0/chapter-9-roof-assemblies
- TDI: insurers credit roofing rated under UL 2218; Class 4 gets the largest credit, each insurer sets its own amount and paperwork; labeling must show class, maker, year, brand; contractor completes TDI form PC068 after installation.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/forms/pcpersonal/pc068roofinstal.pdf
- Texas Property Code 202.011 (HB 362, 2011): an HOA may not enforce a rule that bars an owner who may already install shingles from installing shingles designed to be wind and hail resistant, more energy efficient, or to generate solar power, if they resemble the shingles used or allowed in the subdivision, are more durable than and equal or better in quality than those, and match the look of the surrounding property.
  https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011 ; https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/82r/billtext/html/hb00362f.htm
- TDI deductible law and HB 3344 (shared notes).

# Fort Worth research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Page: /roofing-companies-fort-worth-tx/ (existing URL). Target: roofing fort worth tx (1600).

## Verified
- City roofing FAQ (Development Services): shingle-only replacement on one/two-family home needs no permit, but there is a limit on the number of shingle layers; replacing any decking, lathing, sheathing, rafters or ridge boards needs a building permit; a roof that met code when built still complies and sound sheathing need not be replaced; no new decking layer over an existing layer; 3/8 in plywood or OSB still allowed on 24 in rafters when span rating is 24/0; local amendment: no ice barrier required (no history of ice damming); drip edge and crickets required when re-roofing if missing. FAQ cites the 2021 IRC.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/2/development-services/documents/resources-applications-forms-videos/r/roofing-faq.pdf
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/residential-information
- Contractor registration: anyone obtaining a building, mechanical, plumbing or electrical permit must hold a valid Contractor Registration with Development Services; exception is a homeowner on own homestead. Application with government ID and any trade licenses.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/contractor-registration
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/17/development-services/documents/resources-applications-forms-videos/c/contractor-registration-application.pdf
- Historic districts: Fairmount/Southside guidelines: original tile and slate roofs to be maintained; metal roofs require a Certificate of Appropriateness; standing seam allowed on secondary slopes; R-panel and rib-panel prohibited. Historic roofing guidelines require a COA for exterior work needing a building permit. Carver Heights has its own historic design guidelines.
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/development-services/documents/all-preservation-and-design/historic/historic-districts/fairmount/section7-roofing.pdf
  https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/development-services/documents/all-preservation-and-design/historic/historic-districts/carver-heights/historic-carver-heights-guidelines-english.pdf
- March 28, 2000: F2 tornado touched down about 4 miles west of downtown, tracked east along West 7th Street, heavily damaged the Cash America building, broke thousands of downtown windows; 2 deaths. Same storm system: hail severely damaged roofs and cars in Lake Worth and Saginaw.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/march2000ftwarltor ; https://www.spc.noaa.gov/faq/tornado/downtown.html
- May 5, 1995 Mayfest storm: high-precipitation supercell, struck the Mayfest festival around 7:10 p.m., softball-size hail, 70 mph gusts, rain up to 2.25 in in 15 min; NWS case study: nearly $2 billion property loss; first single thunderstorm over $1 billion. Death toll differs by source (NWS case study 20, WFAA 16), most from flooding: do not state a count.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/mayfest15
- Alliance: AllianceTexas developed since 1989 with Fort Worth Alliance Airport (industrial airport); residential subdivisions followed.
  https://www.alliancetexas.com/Portals/0/Images/AllianceTexas/resources/facts/AllianceTexas%20Economic%20Impact%20Report_Full%20Report.pdf
- Electric: Oncor serves most of Fort Worth (use default safety box wording "Oncor or your local electric utility").

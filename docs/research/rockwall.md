## County
- City of Rockwall is the seat of Rockwall County, the smallest Texas county by area (147 sq mi per county site and state marker). Bounded on the west by Lake Ray Hubbard (East Fork of the Trinity). 2020 census 107,819; county grew 7.94% 2020 to 2021 (third fastest in Texas then, per Stacker via CultureMap). https://rockwallcountytexas.com/255/History ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockwall_County,_Texas ; https://dallas.culturemap.com/news/city-life/05-16-22-rockwall-richest-county-in-texas-stacker

## Permits and registration (City of Rockwall)
- A new roof requires a building permit from the City of Rockwall first, whether owner or contractor applies. Contractor registrations and permits submitted through the city's CityWorks public portal; inspections scheduled there. Permit technicians (972) 771-7709; 385 S. Goliad Street. https://www.rockwall.com/buildinginspections.asp
- General contractor registration $100, expires annually (fee schedule). https://rockwall.com/documents/BI/PermitFeeSchedule.pdf
- Permit application: contractors caught working without a permit have registrations revoked. https://ww.rockwall.com/documents/BI/PermitApplication.pdf
- City publishes monthly permits-issued reports listing roofing permits. https://m.rockwall.com/Documents/BI/Reports/Permits/Permits%20List%202024.pdf

## HOA
- Texas Property Code 202.011: POA may not prohibit or restrict shingles designed mainly to resist wind and hail, to provide heating/cooling efficiency beyond customary composite shingles, or to generate solar energy, if they resemble shingles used or authorized in the subdivision (and other conditions). https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011

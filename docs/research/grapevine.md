## Verified
- Re-roof permit (City of Grapevine, Residential Re-Roofing Requirements sheet, rev. 1-2024): a permit is required
  for re-roofing; permit fee $42.00 on that sheet (not printed on page; fees change); work follows Chapter 9 of the
  2021 IRC, re-roofing Section R908; asphalt shingles may NOT be installed over existing roof coverings; roof vents
  must be properly connected and flashed under the permit; inspection request line (817) 410-3010.
  https://grapevinetexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/376/Residential-ReRoofing-Requirements
  "When Is a Permit Required" lists re-roofing (with windows and siding). 
  https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/353/When-Is-A-Permit-Required
- Applicant and owner are responsible for obtaining inspections; failing to obtain and pass them lets the permit
  expire (residential permit requirements).
  https://grapevinetexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/385/Residential-Permit-Requirements
- Contractor registration: roofing contractors are registered as general contractors; registration required before
  performing work regulated by the building code; owner-occupants exempt; register via MyGov portal or at Building
  Services, 200 S. Main Street; Building Services 817-410-3165. (Fee $75 per city page; not printed.)
  https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/139/Contractor-Registration
  https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/1746/Building-Services
- Historic districts: exterior alterations to properties in historic districts and landmarks need a Certificate of
  Appropriateness before work; simple, routine changes approved by staff within a few days; bigger projects go to
  the Historic Preservation Commission, which meets monthly; a CA does not replace the building permit; design
  guidelines include roof guidance on replacing historic roofing materials, favoring repair and matching
  replacement. Historic Preservation office 817-410-3197.
  https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/284/Historic-Preservation
  https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/285/Certificate-of-Appropriateness
  https://grapevinetexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/137/DesignGuidelines
- History: oldest settlement in Tarrant County, established 1844 as Grape Vine Prairie (Wikipedia); October 1843
  treaty meeting at Grape Vine Springs opened the area to homesteaders; Cotton Belt rail line 1888 made it an
  agricultural trade center (city budget history). Incorporated 1907 (Wikipedia).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapevine,_Texas
  https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2025/Grapevine/files/basic-html/page258.html
- Grapevine Lake: built by the US Army Corps of Engineers under the River and Harbors Act of 1945 for Trinity River
  flood control; completed 1952 (Community Impact, 2012).
  https://communityimpact.com/grapevine-colleyville-southlake/news/grapevine-lake/
- Dec 13, 2022 tornadoes: NWS confirmed two EF-1 tornadoes in Grapevine: one about 110 mph from near Sam's Club to
  southwest of the Gaylord Texan, lifting at the lakeshore; a short one about 100 mph at Grapevine Mills. Homes
  damaged along Lucas Drive near Northwest Highway (roofs, trees). Mayor William D. Tate: five minor injuries, no
  deaths. City counted over 40 structures affected.
  https://communityimpact.com/grapevine-colleyville-southlake/weather/2-tornadoes-confirmed-in-dec-13-storm-grapevine-mayor-thankful-for-no-loss-of-life/
  https://communityimpact.com/grapevine-colleyville-southlake/weather/city-still-recovering-after-2-tornados-damage-grapevine/
  https://nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/16-tornadoes-confirmed-in-tuesday-morning-storms-in-north-texas/3148179

# Weatherford, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing weatherford texas (480). Page: /roofing-weatherford-tx/

## County / city
- Incorporated city, county seat of Parker County. 30,854 residents at the 2020 census; Parker County 148,222.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weatherford,_Texas ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_County,_Texas
- Parker County created 1855, organized the next year. Courthouse built 1885. First railroad reached
  Weatherford in 1880 (Britannica). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_County,_Texas ;
  https://www.britannica.com/place/Weatherford

## Permits / registration (City of Weatherford, weatherfordtx.gov)
- All contractors doing work within city limits must be registered; registration needs the form, proof of
  general liability coverage of at least $300,000 and a photo ID. Source: https://weatherfordtx.gov/3683/Residential-Contractor-Registration
  (also https://weatherfordtx.gov/3653/Contractor-Licensing)
- Residential page lists "Roof Repair or Replace" among the top 10 common permits; permits applied for
  online (permits.weatherfordtx.gov); plan review usually 2 to 3 business days; questions (817) 598-4282 x 2
  or permitapplications@weatherfordtx.gov; many lenders will not finance improvements without proof of final
  inspection. Sources: https://weatherfordtx.gov/3657/Residential ; https://weatherfordtx.gov/3688/Residential-Permit-Process
- Monthly "permits issued by type" reports list "Roof Repair or Replace Permit" entries.
  Source: https://weatherfordtx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/27433/12-DECEMBER-2025
- Fees seen in logs ($125 / $375 / $750) NOT used: not an official schedule.

## Electric
- City of Weatherford runs an electric utility with its own Outage Center (report outages by text OUT to
  817-598-4225 or call 817-598-4257). Source: https://weatherfordtx.gov/3438/Outage-Center
- 2017 CBS Texas: a Tri-County Electric Co-op line fell onto a City of Weatherford line on a windy day,
  about 1,500 homes lost power. Shows both providers have lines in the area.
  Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/freak-power-outage-leaves-some-homeowners-with-unexpected-expenses

## Storms
- Windstorm of Thursday, March 2, 2023 (date confirmed by fact-check fc7: city release quoted by the Weatherford Democrat, WISD closed March 3; about 10,000 city electric customers out): copper paneling torn off the
  City Hall roof, trees uprooted, billboards north of downtown blown over, residents displaced from two
  apartment complexes, Weatherford ISD canceled classes; NWS meteorologist believed straight-line winds,
  no tornado confirmed; Parker County Fire Marshal said five or six locations reported 80 to 90 mph winds.
  Sources: https://www.aol.com/tornado-believed-hit-weatherford-no-022707096.html ;
  https://aol.com/did-tornado-tear-weatherford-national-175208319.html
- Separate CBS piece: suspected tornado tore off roofs, split trees, leveled fences in a Weatherford
  neighborhood (date not shown). Source: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/suspected-tornado-tears-off-roofs-splits-trees-levels-fences-in-weatherford-neighborhood
- Stormersite hail archive (spotter reports) mentions golf-ball hail in Weatherford in 2025 and 2008: NOT
  used (aggregator, unverified).

## Housing
- Weatherford has a National Register historic district near the courthouse square and Cherry Park, with
  Victorian, 1930s cottage and Tudor homes (real estate listings only). Used only as "older homes near the
  square", general.

# Roanoke research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- City mostly in Denton County with a small part in Tarrant County. 2020 census 9,665; Census estimate 10,798 (July 1, 2023). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roanoke,_Texas ; https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/roanokecitytexas
- "Unique Dining Capital of Texas": designated by the Texas Legislature (2009 concurrent resolution per Popik; Community Impact says the House bestowed it). Restaurants concentrated downtown on Oak Street; 39 restaurants at designation, more than 65 by 2019. https://communityimpact.com/keller-roanoke-northeast-fort-worth/city-county/new-food-hall-joining-unique-dining-capital-of-texas/ ; https://barrypopik.com/blog/unique_dining_capital_of_texas_roanoke_nickname
- Permits: Building Inspection page says permits are required before alterations, additions, new construction or repairs to existing buildings and structures; 2021 International Codes adopted incl. Residential Code (Ordinance 2023-122). City Hall 500 S. Oak Street; 817-491-2411 (inspection requests); Mon to Thu 8 to 5:30, Fri 8 to noon. https://roanoketexas.com/146/Building-Inspection---Code-Enforcement
- Commercial permit packet (2021): general contractor must be registered with Roanoke before the permit is issued; no registration fee. https://roanoketexas.com/DocumentCenter/View/3508/2021-Commercial-Building-Permit-Application-Packet
- Fairway Ranch: built on part of the 700-acre ranch once owned by golfer Byron Nelson, near historic downtown; listings show homes built about 2013 to 2019. https://www.davidweekleyhomes.com/news/article/fairway-ranch-from-david-weekley-homes-offers-move-in-ready-homes ; https://spencer.roveridx.com/tx/roanoke/fairway-ranch-ph-1/944-highpoint-way/
- Hail: StormerSite listings show 2 inch hail about 1 mile from Roanoke on May 27, 2024, and a 1.5 inch report about 3 miles away the same evening. https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/roanoke_texas/all

## Could not verify / left out

- Electric provider: third-party tracker lists Oncor; not official. Default safety wording.
- Hawkins Ranch, Roanoke Oaks: not verified, not named.

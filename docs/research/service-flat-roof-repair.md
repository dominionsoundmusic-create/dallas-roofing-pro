# Research: /flat-roof-repair/ (searched Oct 14 2026)

- Ponding: NRCA treats water remaining more than 48 hours after rain (in drying conditions) as ponding; short-term standing water is considered acceptable.
  https://www.professionalroofing.net/articles/article/2109
  https://www.constructionspecifier.com/better-drainage-better-low-slope-commercial-roof-systems/
- IBC 2021 "positive roof drainage": accounts for deck deflection under loads plus added slope so the roof drains within 48 hours of precipitation.
  https://www.constructionspecifier.com/roof-drainage-tapered-insulation-systems/
- 2021 IRC R905.11.1 (modified bitumen) and R905.13.1 (thermoplastic single-ply): design slope not less than 1/4 unit in 12 (2 percent). R908.1: reroofing need not meet the minimum slope for roofs that provide positive roof drainage.
  https://up.codes/viewer/connecticut/irc-2021/chapter/9/roof-assemblies
- OSHA: falls are the leading cause of work-related deaths among residential construction workers; 389 of 1,034 construction deaths in 2024 were falls to a lower level (BLS via OSHA stand-down page); fall protection at 6 feet in construction.
  https://www.osha.gov/residential-fall-protection/guidance
  https://www.osha.gov/stop-falls-stand-down
- Cost (national cost sites): HomeGuide flat roof replacement about $3 to $11 per sq ft ($4,000 to $16,800 total); asphalt tear-off and replace about $3 to $5 per sq ft (3-tab), $4 to $6 (architectural).
  https://homeguide.com/costs/flat-roof-replacement-cost  https://homeguide.com/costs/average-cost-to-tear-off-and-replace-roof

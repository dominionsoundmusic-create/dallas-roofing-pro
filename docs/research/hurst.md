## Verified
- Permits and contractors (City of Hurst Building Inspections): Hurst no longer requires annual
  contractor registration; contractors register through EACH permit application, supplying a copy of a
  US-issued ID, a trade license if applicable, and a copy of general liability insurance. Permits are
  applied for through the city's online Citizen Self-Service portal (hursttx.gov/css); accounts are to
  use the company name and company email. The city's permit resources list a "Roof Contractors" page
  and a "Certificate of Final Roof Installation/Inspection" document. A homeowner may pull permits
  without a contractor only if living in the structure and personally doing the work. Building
  Inspections phone 817-788-7088.
  https://www.hursttx.gov/about-us/departments/community-development/building-inspections/building-permit-information
  https://www.hursttx.gov/about-us/departments/community-development/building-inspections/frequently-asked-questions
  https://www.hursttx.gov/about-us/departments/community-development/building-inspections/applications-permits
- Conflict: a separate Public Works permit fee page still lists a $50 annual contractor registration.
  Page tells readers to confirm with Building Inspections; the $50 figure is not printed.
  https://www.hursttx.gov/about-us/departments/public-works/engineering-construction/engineering-forms-regulations-and-fees/permit-fees
- History (City of Hurst library, Hurst History): Bell helicopter plant opened 1951 and touched off a
  growth boom; Hurst incorporated September 25, 1952 with about 2,700 residents; about 5,700 five years
  later; passed 20,000 by the mid-1960s; North East Mall opened 1972, the largest mall in Tarrant County
  until the 1990s.
  https://www.hursttx.gov/about-us/departments/library/hurst-history
- 1953 Star-Telegram photo caption: new homes going up in Hurst from industrial growth after the Bell
  plant; land values up 200 to 1,000 percent. (UTA Star-Telegram 1950s collection)
  https://mavmatrix.uta.edu/specialcollections_startelegram1950s/28565
- 1955 aerial photo looking south from Pipeline Road shows Highway 183, Brown Trail, Bell Helicopter.
  https://texashistory.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metapth27993/metadata/
- Electric: Oncor (default wording, nothing found otherwise).

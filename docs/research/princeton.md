## Verified
- County: Collin. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_Texas
- Population: 17,027 (2020 census); 28,027 (Census estimate July 1 2023). https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/princetoncitytexas/PST045223 ; Census estimates for 2024 to 2025 put Princeton in the top five fastest-growing US cities of 20,000+. https://celinaedc.com/about/news-press-releases/population-rebounds-for-many-cities-in-northeast-and-midwest
- Roof permits: city monthly "Permits Issued by Type" reports list "Building Permit - Roof" re-roof permits (numbers like BLDR-2025-xxxxx), many with a $90.00 fee and $0 valuation; one entry describes felt replacement, ice and water shield and new vents. https://princetontx.gov/Archive/ViewFile/Item/874 ; https://princetontx.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/958
- Contractor registration: city FAQ lists general contractors among the trades that must register with the City of Princeton; FAQ gives $75; a city contractor list from early 2025 shows $100 for general contractor registration (conflict, so page gives no amount). https://princetontx.gov/m/faq?cat=17 ; https://princetontx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4417/CONTRACTORS-JAN-2025--APRIL-2025-1
- Development Services: 2000 E. Princeton Dr, (972) 736-2416; Building Inspection Division 972-736-6169 (from city pages per search summary).
- Tornado April 3 2014: NWS Fort Worth: brief EF-0 tornado near Princeton, estimated max winds 85 mph, path about 1.2 miles; some structures in the path had roof damage with loss of shingles and roof decking. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/storms040314

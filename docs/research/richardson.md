# Richardson research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-company-richardson-tx/ . Target: roofing company richardson (260).

## Verified
- County: Dallas and Collin.
- Permit: needed for replacing, recovering or repairing more than 25% of the roof area (FAQ says 25% or more); inspections after roof deck installed (if applicable) and at final; permit approved and posted at property before work. https://www.cor.net/departments/building-inspection/residential/permit-types/roof-repair-replacement
- Contractor registration: all contractors must register with Building Inspection before any work; $100, valid one year; city advises checking Registered Contractors List and online permit search; staff confirm by phone. https://www.cor.net/departments/building-inspection/contractor-registration ; https://www.cor.net/departments/building-inspection/online-permits
- City storm-cleanup guidance notes Texas does not require roofing contractors to be licensed or carry insurance. https://www.cor.net/departments/building-inspection/residential/storm-cleanup
- Building Inspection: 972-744-4180, 2360 Campbell Creek Blvd., Suite 550.
- Oct 20 2019 tornado: NWS survey EF3, peak 140 mph, 15.76 mi path, max width 1,300 yd, began 3 ENE Irving 8:58 pm, ended 2 SE Richardson 9:30 pm; no deaths or injuries reported. https://www.weather.gov/media/fwd/torclimo/PNS/PNS_October_20_2019.pdf
- Richardson impact (Community Impact, Oct 28 2019): 3,117 properties evaluated, 671 damaged to varying degrees, 12 destroyed; 54 homes and 196 apartment units uninhabitable; mayor signed disaster declaration; city waived building permit fees for tornado repair work. https://communityimpact.com/news/2019/10/28/richardson-mayor-signs-disaster-declaration-after-hundreds-of-properties-damaged-by-oct-20-tornado/ ; debris: over 50,000 cu yd cleared https://communityimpact.com/dallas-fort-worth/richardson/2019/11/11/crews-in-richardson-clear-over-50000-cubic-yards-of-debris-from-areas-impacted-by-oct-20-tornado/
- Dallas Morning News one year later: families still recovering and working with insurers. https://www.dallasnews.com/news/2020/10/05/one-year-later-richardson-families-still-recovering-from-the-tornado-that-tore-through-the-city/
- Housing: Canyon Creek homes mostly built late 1960s through the 1970s, mostly ranch style (D Magazine) https://www.dmagazine.com/?p=827 ; Greenwood Hills midcentury (CandysDirt) https://candysdirt.com/?p=92987 ; Richardson Heights 1950s-60s ranch (realtor page, used only as "older neighborhood" with the D Magazine/CandysDirt support).
- Low-slope rule: IRC R905.2.2: asphalt shingles only on slopes 2:12 or greater; 2:12 to 4:12 need double underlayment (well established code; kept from old page). https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IRC2015NY/chapter-9-roof-assemblies/IRC2015-Pt03-Ch09-SecR905.2.2 (verified in search: 2:12 minimum, double underlayment 2:12 to 4:12)
- Electric: Oncor.

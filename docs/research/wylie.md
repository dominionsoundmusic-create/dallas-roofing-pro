## Verified
- County: Collin, Dallas and Rockwall.
- Permit: Building Inspection lists "Roofing replacement (residential and commercial)" among projects requiring permits; residential turnaround 5 to 10 business days; 972-516-6100 option 3; permits@wylietexas.gov for registration and permitting. https://www.wylietexas.gov/departments/building_inspection/projects_requiring_permits.php ; https://www.wylietexas.gov/departments/building_inspection/index.php
- April 11 2016 hailstorm: NWS Fort Worth event page: 80% of homes affected, over $300 million damage in Wylie. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/20160411 . CBS Texas: mayor Eric Hogue said about 80% of homes, roughly 12,000, damaged https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/hailstorm-damaged-80-percent-of-wylie-homes/ . FOX 4: baseball-size hail https://www.fox4news.com/news/wylie-area-pounded-by-baseball-sized-hail . NBC DFW: roofing experts found baseball-size or larger stones penetrated wood decking on many Wylie roofs; overlays reduced resistance https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/Roofing-Experts-Study-North-Texas-Hail-Damage-378486875.html ; homeowner still waiting nearly nine months later https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/wylie-hail-storm-victim-still-waiting-for-home-repairs/35480/ . WFAA one year later: https://www.wfaa.com/article/news/one-year-ago-hail-storm-slammed-wylie/287-430227386 . CoreLogic: Wylie event losses $246.8 million (Insurance Journal) https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2016/07/25/420542.htm
- Growth: 15,132 (2000), 41,427 (2010), 57,526 (2020); 5 miles south of Lake Lavon, between Lavon Lake and Lake Ray Hubbard; Lavon Dam and NTMWD offices spurred growth by 1960; a 1993 tornado ravaged downtown. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wylie,_Texas
- Electric: Oncor and Farmers Electric Cooperative both serve parts of Wylie (poweroutage.us lists Farmers Electric Coop; city asked residents to report Oncor outages). https://poweroutage.us/area/city/wylie-tx ; https://local.nixle.com/alert/5102762/ .

## Permits and registration (City of Forney)
- Contractors must be registered with the city before they can pull a permit; all contractors completing work in city limits must register; registration online through MyGov; $30 fee per license; registrations valid for the calendar year only (Jan to Dec). https://www.forneytx.gov/756/Contractors-Corner
- Building permits must be obtained before making improvements or alterations; applied for online through MyGov. https://www.forneytx.gov/138/Building-Inspection ; https://www.forneytx.gov/619/Building-Applications-Residential
- Residential permit application: all contractors listed must be registered before permit issued; permit void if work not started within 180 days. https://forneytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/32/Building-Permit-Application---Residential-Project-PDF
- Master fee schedule includes a re-roofing line (amount unreadable: not stated).

## Storm history
- April 3 2012: NWS Fort Worth survey: tornado stayed in Kaufman County, touched down near Forney southwest of US 80; EF-3, est. 150 mph, max width about 150 yards, path about 8 miles. Worst damage in Diamond Creek subdivision northeast of Forney: two homes destroyed, several heavily damaged; Crosby Elementary roof damaged; a vehicle carried about 300 yards. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012forney
- Wikipedia: no deaths, seven injured. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forney,_Texas

## Electric
- Forney EDC: three suppliers: Oncor (largest; downtown, north of US 80, Gateway), Trinity Valley Electric Cooperative (southern Forney near I-20 and around Forney High School), Farmers Electric Cooperative. https://forneytexasedc.org/site-selection/infrastructure

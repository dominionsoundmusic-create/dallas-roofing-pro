# Haltom City research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing haltom city tx (10). County: Tarrant.

## Verified
- Contractor registration: all contractors must register with the city to receive permits; homeowners on their own homestead-exempt property (TAD) with matching driver license are exempt. Registration asks for driver license and a certificate of insurance naming the city as certificate holder; the contractor form has a GC/Roof category and an annual fee.
  https://haltomcitytx.com/Faq.aspx?QID=85 ; https://www.haltomcitytx.com/DocumentCenter/View/343/Contractors-Registration-PDF
- Residential Roofing Permit form exists; asks shingle type, felt type and decking thickness; lists a higher fee for working without a permit; permit invalid if work not started within 180 days.
  https://www.haltomcitytx.com/DocumentCenter/View/340/Residential-Roofing-Permit-PDF
- Applications through the city's MyGov online portal; Planning and Inspections FAQ; Planning & Community Development 817-222-7730; City Hall 4801 Haltom Rd.
  https://haltomcitytx.com/280/Planning-Inspection-FAQs ; https://www.haltomcitytx.com/278/Planning-Inspections---Forms-and-Permits
- History: Haltom Village founded 1932, named for G. W. Haltom, Fort Worth jeweler; growth from Meadow Oaks development and highways; businesses from old Birdville moved south. Incorporation date differs by source (1944 city budget vs 1949): left out.
  https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2024/Haltom%20City/files/basic-html/page186.html
- The city relays NWS severe thunderstorm warnings to residents via Nixle (Aug 26 2026 warning: 60 mph gusts, quarter-size hail, roof damage possible). https://nixle.us/HKNLM
- Electric: Oncor (default wording).

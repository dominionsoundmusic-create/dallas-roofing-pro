# Arlington, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target keyword: roofing arlington tx (880). Page: /roofing-arlington-tx/ (existing URL kept).

## County / city
- Incorporated city in Tarrant County. Electric: Oncor (deregulated area; Oncor owns the wires).

## Permits and contractor registration (City of Arlington)
- Building permits required for replacement of roofing materials/coverings effective November 1, 2011.
  Source: https://www.arlingtontx.gov/cms/One.aspx?portalId=14481146&pageId=14989339 (Permitting & Inspections)
- Replacing roof covering where the area replaced is 10 percent or less of roof area is listed as work that
  does not need a permit; city 2016 article: repairs greater than 10% of the roof surface require a permit.
  Sources: https://www.arlingtontx.gov/files/assets/city/v/2/code-compliance/documents/get-involved-code-compliance/do-i-need-a-permit.pdf ;
  https://www.arlingtontx.gov/news/my_arlington_t_x/news_archive/2016_archived_news/march_2016/who_is_on_your_roof_tips_to_pick_a_roofing
- Roofing contractors must be registered with the City of Arlington to get a building permit; anyone hired
  under contract to do construction must be registered. Registration searchable on ArlingtonPermits.com.
  Source: https://www.arlingtontx.gov/city_hall/departments/planning_development_services/information_center/tips_for_choosing_a_contractor
- Permits via ArlingtonPermits.com (apply, pay, schedule inspections). Inspections requested before 7 a.m.
  or scheduled next business day. Planning & Development Services 817-459-6502; One Start Center, City Hall,
  101 W. Abram Street, second floor. Source: Permitting & Inspections page (above) and https://www.arlingtontx.gov/City-Services/Permits
- Public permit search: https://ap.arlingtontx.gov/AP/sfjsp?interviewID=PublicSearch&btnclk=search

## City's own roofer advice (March 2016, after the March 17 2016 Tarrant County hailstorm)
- Check registration and when the contractor last pulled an Arlington permit; ask for written list of recent
  jobs, confirm via city permit records and Tarrant Appraisal District; several written estimates; check with
  insurer before signing; beware cash or large upfront payments (partial payment for delivered materials is
  normal); contract should list name and address, scope, payment schedule, completion date, debris removal,
  warranty; lien releases; deductible offers may be insurance fraud.
  Source: 2016 "Who is on Your Roof?" article (above).

## Storm history
- April 3, 2012: NWS Fort Worth rated the Kennedale-Arlington tornado EF-2, max winds about 135 mph, path
  about 4.6 miles, max width 150 yards. Moved north from I-20, EF-2 damage to homes on Haverford Dr. and
  Thorncliffe Dr., crossed Chaperito Trail (Green Oaks Nursing and Rehabilitation Center damaged), ended just
  east of Lake Arlington along Waterview Drive. Survey April 4, 2012.
  Source: https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012kennedalearlington ; outbreak summary https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012sum
- June 6, 2018 early-morning hail: Arlington named among hardest-hit cities with Coppell and Carrollton;
  Insurance Council of Texas preliminary estimate about $425 million insured loss, up to 20,000 homes and
  25,000 vehicles (metro-wide, not Arlington alone). Karen Clark & Co later near $1 billion (includes other
  areas). Sources: https://www.artemis.bm/news/2018/06/07/dallas-texas-hailstorm-estimated-to-cause-425m-insured-loss/ ;
  https://www.fox26houston.com/news/large-hail-causes-damage-in-parts-of-north-texas (label without city name)
- March 17, 2016 hail in Tarrant County prompted the city's roofer warning (source: 2016 city article).

  1990. Older homes near Downtown and East Arlington; newer homes in Viridian and parts of south Arlington.

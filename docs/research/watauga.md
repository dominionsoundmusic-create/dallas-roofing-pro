# Watauga research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing watauga tx (10). County: Tarrant.

## Verified
- Incorporated 1958; Tarrant Utility Company deeded a lot in April 1958 for the fire station and City Hall; grew rapidly in the late 1970s and early 1980s; population about 7,050 in 1976 and about 20,000 by 1990.
  https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2024/Watauga/files/basic-html/page22.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watauga,_Texas
- Name from a Cherokee word whose exact meaning has been lost (Wikipedia). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watauga,_Texas
- Permits: Public Works action center handles contractor registration and renewal, online permit submittals, status and inspection requests (817-514-5806); permits in person at Public Works, 7800 Virgil Anthony Sr. Blvd. City says a homeowner hiring a contractor for structural and other work should make sure the contractor has a permit with the city. https://www.cowtx.org/1147/Permits
- City monthly permit reports list residential roof permits issued to roofing contractors (2021 to 2023 reports).
  https://www.cowtx.org/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/1753 ; https://www.cowtx.org/Archive/ViewFile/Item/1843
- Electric: Oncor (default wording).

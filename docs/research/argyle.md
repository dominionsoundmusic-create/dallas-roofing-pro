# Argyle research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Town in Denton County, about 6 miles southwest of Denton. 2020 census 4,403; Census estimate 5,859 (July 1, 2023). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argyle,_Texas ; https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/argylecitytexas
- Permits: town says permits are required prior to new construction, addition, alteration or repair of existing buildings and structures. Permit FAQ list of common permit projects (accessory structures, fences, HVAC, pools, etc.) does not mention roofing; the town says to call 940-464-7273 with specific questions. Applications through iWorQ portal (argyletx.portal.iworq.net). Permits office 308 Denton Street, Argyle 76226, Mon to Fri 8 to 4:30. https://www.argyletx.com/156/Permits ; https://argyletx.com/Faq.aspx?QID=68
- Contractor registration: all contractors must register with the Town before performing services within Town limits; yearly; $125 initial, $50 renewal; registration application, trade license (or driver's license if not a trade), certificate of insurance with at least $500,000 coverage. Homeowners need not register but must follow codes. https://www.argyletx.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=97 ; https://www.argyletx.com/303/Permit-Fees
- Hail: March 26, 2017 storm (about five minutes) damaged roofs, windows and cars in Argyle; many roofs needed full replacement; student cars at Argyle High undriveable (The Talon News, Argyle High student paper). https://thetalonnews.com/2017/04/hail-storm-brings-costly-damage/
- June 11, 2023: NBC DFW reader photos of large hail from Flower Mound and Argyle. https://www.nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/your-pictures-of-large-hail-in-sunday-evening-storms-june-11-2023/3275335/ (gallery seen in Highland Village research)
- StormerSite storm report listings: 2.5 inch hail near Argyle March 14, 2024; 2 inch hail April 1, 2024 (crowd-sourced/LSR listings). https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/argyle_texas/2019 (archive)
- Housing mix: listings show horse acreage (stalls, arenas, pasture) on roads such as Gold Hill Road and Hilltop Road, and master-planned communities Harvest (by Hillwood) and Canyon Falls with Argyle addresses; one Hilltop Road listing with an Argyle address is in Denton ISD, and some Harvest homes are in Northwest ISD, so an Argyle address does not settle which jurisdiction you are in. https://www.redfin.com/city/1023/TX/Argyle/pending-listings ; https://aaronlayman.com/tx/argyle/harvest/1833-climbing-court/

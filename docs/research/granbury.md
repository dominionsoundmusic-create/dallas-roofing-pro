## County / city
- Incorporated city; county seat of Hood County; 10,958 residents at the 2020 census; principal city of the
  Granbury micropolitan area. Hood County had 61,598 people in 2020.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granbury,_Texas ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hood_County,_Texas
- Began as a courthouse on a square; many buildings on the square are registered historic landmarks,
  including the Granbury Opera House. Named for Confederate General Hiram B. Granbury.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granbury,_Texas
- A dam on the Brazos River in 1969 formed Lake Granbury, a long, narrow lake that runs through the city.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granbury,_Texas

## Permits / registration (City of Granbury Building Inspections)
- Only contractors and subcontractors registered with the City of Granbury can obtain permits;
  unregistered firms submit a contractor registration application to Building Inspections.
  Source: http://granbury.org/67/Building-Permits-Inspections
- FAQ: the general contractor and all subcontractors must be registered, current and listed on the permit
  before it is issued. Source: https://www.granbury.org/Faq.aspx?QID=78
- Residential Construction Guidelines (rev. 09/25): contractors must register with the city before
  submitting permits. Source: https://www.granbury.org/DocumentCenter/View/11901/Residential-Construction-Guidelines
- City monthly permit reports list residential and commercial re-roof permits as a permit type
  (e.g. https://granbury.org/DocumentCenter/View/12059). Whether EVERY re-roof needs one was NOT confirmed:
  page tells readers to ask the city / roofer.

## Storm history
- May 15, 2013: EF4 tornado struck the southeast part of Granbury in the evening, 6 deaths, all in the
  Rancho Brazos subdivision, about $143 million damage there; NWS Fort Worth estimated winds near 180 mph
  (preliminary range 166 to 200 mph in AP / CBS reporting). Strongest tornado of the outbreak.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Granbury_tornado ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tornado_outbreak_of_May_15%E2%80%9317,_2013 ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/officials-north-texas-tornado-likely-had-200-mph-winds
- Same night: EF3 near Cleburne (Johnson County), about 140 mph, 8.5-mile path; NWS Fort Worth confirmed
  16 tornadoes by May 17. Sources: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/nws-estimated-12-north-texas-tornadoes-some-ef-3-ef-4 ;
  https://earthsky.org/?p=171479

## Electric provider
- The City of Granbury operates its own electric distribution utility in the ERCOT region (city manager
  affidavit in a 2023 PUC filing). Source: https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/Documents/53385_889_1275958.PDF

# Red Oak, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing red oak tx (20). Page: /roofing-red-oak-tx/

## County / city
- City in northern Ellis County, about twenty miles south of Dallas. Census: 4,301 (2000), 10,769 (2010), 14,222 (2020);
  Census estimate 18,624 for July 1, 2023. Early settlers James E. Patton and family in the 1840s; first called Possum
  Trot; named Red Oak in 1849; Missouri, Kansas and Texas Railroad reached Red Oak in 1884.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Oak,_Texas ; https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/redoakcitytexas/PST045223
- Electric: not verified (generic safety box wording kept).

## Permits and registration (City of Red Oak Development Services)
- Permits and Inspections Division takes applications and issues permits; the city inspects all work requiring a permit with
  in-house and contracted inspectors; adopted codes include the 2024 International Residential Code.
  Source: https://www.redoaktx.org/309/Permits-Inspections
- Any firm, business, individual or organization obtaining a permit in Red Oak must have a valid Contractor Registration on
  file with Development Services, signed by an official of the business; no fee; account via the Community Core portal;
  licensed trades renew annually. Permit technician (469) 218-7725.
  Source: https://www.redoaktx.org/342/Contractor-Registration
- Residential permit application (7-1-24): building department 101 S. Live Oak St., (972) 576-3414; permit void if work not
  started within 180 days; all permits require final inspection.
  Source: https://www.redoaktx.org/DocumentCenter/View/7972/Residential-Permit-Application-7-1-24-FINAL
- Residential Building Permit page: checklist for additions asks for foundation and roofing plans.
  Source: https://www.redoaktx.org/318/Residential-Building-Permit
- No city page found that states whether a re-roof alone needs a permit: page says so and tells reader to ask.

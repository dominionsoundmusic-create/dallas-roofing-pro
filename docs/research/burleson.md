## County / city
- Incorporated city in Johnson and Tarrant counties. 2010: 36,690. 2020: Census QuickFacts base 47,643
  (Wikipedia infobox 47,641). 2024 estimate 57,625. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burleson,_Texas ;
  https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/burlesoncitytexas/PST045223
- Grew around a depot on the Missouri-Kansas-Texas ("Katy") Railroad after the Fort Worth to Alvarado
  segment was laid out in 1880. Source: Wikipedia above.
- NOTE: Burleson County is a different place (near College Station). Not mentioned on the page.

## Permits / registration (City of Burleson, burlesontx.com)
- "What requires a permit?" list includes "Roof Decking ONLY: residential reroof where decking will be
  replaced"; the list is not all-inclusive. Source: https://www.burlesontx.com/87/Building-Permits-Inspections
- FAQ: city requires registration for electricians, mechanical, plumbing, irrigation, backflow testers and
  fire prevention contractors; roofers are not named. Registrations expire December 31.
  Sources: https://www.burlesontx.com/m/faq?cat=54 ; https://www.burlesontx.com/DocumentCenter/View/512/Contractor-Registration
- Adopted 2021 International codes Oct 2, 2023; enforced from Nov 2, 2023. Source: https://www.burlesontx.com/Faq.aspx?QID=315
- Inspections online or 817-426-9636 by 8 p.m. the day before; Permits and Inspections 817-426-9632,
  bldgpermits@burlesontx.com. Sources: https://www.burlesontx.com/776/Request-an-Inspection ;
  https://www.burlesontx.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=313

## Neighborhoods / growth
- Chisholm Summit: the city calls it the first master-planned community proposed for Burleson, about
  3,066 residential units at build out; homes zoned to Joshua ISD. Source: https://burlesontx.com/2309/Chisholm-Summit
- 2021 Cleburne Times-Review: roughly 900 acres near the Chisholm Trail Parkway on the western border.
  Tallgrass: more than 600 acres, about 4,000 units, at the Chisholm Trail Parkway / FM 1902 interchange.
  Sources: https://www.cleburnetimesreview.com/news/1-2-billion-master-planned-development-106-acre-professional-office-park-headed-to-burleson/article_dcc7157a-c86b-11eb-bb12-2bd1b43f3997.html ;
  https://www.cleburnetimesreview.com/news/1b-community-planned-in-burleson/article_96bf6950-1ed4-4352-a90d-010b2dbaf698.html
- Old Town area: homes from the 1960s to 1990s (real estate blog, not authoritative): stated only as "older
  homes near Old Town" in general terms.

## Electric

  Cooperatives co-op map lists it with Burleson). Formed 2000 from Johnson County and Erath County co-ops

## Claim deadlines (Insurance Code ch. 542)
- [S] TDI blog: company has 15 business days to acknowledge the claim; 15 business days after getting what it needs to
  say whether it will pay; rejection reasons in writing; may extend 45 days if it says why; pays within 5 business days
  of agreeing; TDI can extend deadlines 15 more days after a weather catastrophe; if deadlines missed, policyholder may be
  able to collect the claim, interest and attorney's fees. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/blog/insurance-claim-deadlines.html
  (Note: the 2021 Consumer Bill of Rights says "15 days" for acknowledgment; page follows the TDI blog wording and says
  "about 15 days".)
- [H] Statutes 542.056 and 542.059. https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._ins._code_section_542.056 ;
  https://codes.findlaw.com/tx/insurance-code/ins-sect-542-059/
- [S] TDI getting your claim paid page. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/getting-your-insurance-claim-paid.html
  [H] mortgage company usually on the check; insurer might deny if permanent repairs are made before it sees the damage.

## Policy valuation
- [H] Replacement cost vs actual cash value; some companies switch older roofs to ACV at renewal; a roof in poor
  condition may not be covered; some policies set a one-year deadline to file. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/replacing-your-roof.html
- [H] RC claims usually paid in two checks: first minus depreciation and deductible, second (withheld depreciation)
  after the insurer gets the bill for the finished work; repairs usually must be done within a set period.
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/home-insurance-policies-replacement-cost-or-actual-cash-value.html
- [H] Percentage deductibles: convert to dollars; deductible applies to each claim. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/deductibles.html
  (Page uses its own arithmetic example, labeled as arithmetic.)
- [H] TDI Bulletin B-0030-98: optional endorsement excluding cosmetic hail damage on policies receiving an
  impact-resistant roof credit. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/bulletins/1998/b-0030-8.html
- [H] Impact-resistant roof discounts set company by company. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html
- [H] Appraisal provision in many policies; denial reason in writing. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/consumer/storms/home-damaged-faq.html

## Texas law on contractors
- [G][H] Bus. & Com. Code 27.02: contractor may not waive, rebate, absorb a deductible or pad an estimate; contracts of
  $1,000+ paid with insurance proceeds must carry the deductible notice; Ins. Code ch. 707: policyholder pays the
  deductible, insurer may ask for proof; penalty up to $2,000 fine and up to six months in jail (TDI).
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/can-a-contractor-waive-my-deductible.html ;
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/Consumer/storms/roofing-and-insurance-know-the-law.html ;
  https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/state-law-cracks-down-on-roof-scams.html
- [G][H] Ins. Code 4102.163: contractor may not act as a public adjuster or advertise to adjust claims on a property it
  serves or may serve; it may give estimates and discuss them and technical information with the insurer or adjuster.
  Public adjusters are licensed by TDI. https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/public-adjusters.html
- [T] Bus. & Com. Code ch. 58 (disaster remediation after a declared disaster; contractor without a physical address
  for a year in the county or an adjacent county may not require payment before work starts; partial payments
  proportionate). https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.58.htm
- [G] AG consumer line 800-621-0508. [H] TDI help line 800-252-3439 (coordinator verified in the Houston build).

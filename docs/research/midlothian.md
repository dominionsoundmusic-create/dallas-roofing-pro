## County / city
- Incorporated; northwest Ellis County, about 25 miles southwest of Dallas. 2020 census 35,125; Texas Almanac
  2021 estimate 37,053. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midlothian,_Texas ; https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/midlothian-tx
- Three cement plants and a steel mill; "Cement Capital of Texas" nickname tied to 1959 cement plant.
  Source: Wikipedia (above). Google data center campus (Candy's Dirt 2024) not used.
- Electric: Oncor serves most; not separately verified per address (generic safety box).

## Permits and registration (City of Midlothian Building Inspections)
- Building Permits page lists Roofing among projects needing a permit; permits must be obtained prior to making
  improvements or alterations. Source: https://www.midlothian.tx.us/101/Building-Permits
- Separate Roof Permit Application on the Permit Applications page. https://www.midlothian.tx.us/1004/Permit-Applications
- Contractors must be registered with the City to obtain a permit; no fee; register in person, email, fax or MyGov;
  bring picture ID, insurance and appropriate license. Help line 972-775-7134 (8 a.m. to 5 p.m.).
  Building Inspections, 215 N. 8th Street, (972) 775-7161.
  Sources: https://www.midlothian.tx.us/74/Building-Permits-Inspections ; https://www.midlothian.tx.us/FAQ.aspx?QID=75

## Storm history
- October 20, 2019: NWS Fort Worth survey, EF-1, peak winds about 100 mph, path about 2.8 miles, began near
  Highway 67 and 9th Street on the north side of Midlothian and moved east; several church and retail buildings
  had blown-out windows and roof damage. Same night as the Dallas tornadoes.
  Source: https://www.weather.gov/media/fwd/torclimo/PNS/PNS_October_20_2019.pdf
- April 4, 2022: NWS survey, EF-0, peak winds about 85 mph, path about 1.4 miles, along E Wyatt Road near Ward
  Road; roof damage to homes and tree damage.
  Source: https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/202204072136-KFWD-NOUS44-PNSFWD

# Little Elm research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Town in Denton County along the northern and eastern shores of Lewisville Lake. Census: 3,646 (2000), 25,898 (2010), 46,453 (2020); Census estimate 58,496 (July 1, 2023). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Elm,_Texas ; https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/littleelmcitytexas
- 66 miles of Lewisville Lake shoreline (Little Elm EDC 2023 Fast Facts). https://littleelmedc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023-Fast-Facts.pdf
- KERA (June 17, 2019): Little Elm's population skyrocketed after investing in lake living. https://www.keranews.org/texas-news/2019-06-17/little-elm-saw-its-population-skyrocket-after-investing-in-lake-living
- Permits ("little elm roofing permit" keyword): Building Safety, a division of Development Services, reviews, issues and inspects permits. The town's adopted building code language: any owner, authorized agent or contractor who wants to alter or repair a building must first apply and obtain the required permit. No reroof-specific page found. Permits through the MyGov portal. Building Safety: 100 W. Eldorado Pkwy, 214-975-0456, permits@littleelm.org. https://www.littleelm.gov/86/Building-Permits ; https://www.littleelm.gov/82/Building-Permits-Safety ; https://www.littleelm.gov/1428/Development-Portal
- Contractor registration: MyGov handles building permits, contractor registrations and annual renewals. Residential Building Packet (revised Jan 13, 2020) lists a $100 registration fee and requires subcontractors to register before certain permits. https://www.littleelm.gov/DocumentCenter/View/14541/Residential-Building-Packet_Revised_-1-13-2020 ; https://littleelm.org/9/Town-HallOnline
- March 14, 2024: NBC DFW hail photo gallery includes images captioned hail in Little Elm; StormerSite lists 1.5 to 2.5 inch hail reports within a few miles that evening (one 2.5 inch report located at El Dorado Pkwy and FM 423 in Frisco). https://www.nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/photos-large-hail-falls-as-severe-storms-roll-through-north-texas-on-march-14-2024/3489258/ ; https://www.stormersite.com/hail_reports/little_elm_texas/2024
- Electric: comparison sites say Little Elm is in Oncor's delivery area (retail choice); not confirmed by Oncor itself, so default safety wording (Oncor or your local utility) used.

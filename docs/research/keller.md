## Verified
- Contractor registration (City of Keller registration requirements form; Building Services fee schedule):
  $50 per trade per year (electrical, mechanical and plumbing exempt); registration expires one year from the
  registration date; form asks for a copy of a state license (where applicable) and proof of liability insurance
  with a $300,000 minimum. Fee not printed on page except as "a yearly fee"; the $300,000 minimum IS used.
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/home/showdocument?id=15426
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/finance/fee-schedule/building-services-fee-schedule
- Permits: Building Services at Keller Town Hall, 1100 Bear Creek Parkway; 817-743-4110,
  buildingservices@cityofkeller.com; permits through the online portal (cityofkeller.com/css).
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/business/building-construction-services
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/building-services/contact-us
- Building inspections FAQ: a permit is required to construct, enlarge, alter, repair, move, demolish or change the
  occupancy of a building; doing the work yourself does not exempt you; work without a needed permit is subject to
  fines and penalties. Construction tips: contractors should obtain permits; many repair or replacement jobs need
  one (example: windows). Roofing is NOT named on pages seen: page tells readers to ask Building Services.
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/community-development/building-inspections/frequently-asked-questions
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/community-development/building-inspections/construction-tips
- History (City of Keller history page; Wikipedia; Community Impact): settlers in the early 1850s; first called
  Athol; renamed for John C. Keller, a railroad foreman, at the urging of a railroad official; Texas and Pacific
  Railway; US 377 between Fort Worth and Denton; incorporated November 16, 1955 (70th anniversary in 2025); under
  4,000 residents when DFW Airport opened in 1974; 13,683 in 1990; 45,776 in the 2020 census; 11 park sites on
  300+ acres and 26+ miles of trails.
  https://www.cityofkeller.com/about-us/history
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keller,_Texas
  https://communityimpact.com/keller-roanoke-northeast-fort-worth/government/city-of-keller-celebrates-70-year-anniversary/
- Hail: SPC storm reports for March 30, 2009 list 2.75-inch (baseball) hail at Keller: "NWS MET reports baseball
  hail at home. Damage to skylight." https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/090330_rpts.html
  March 8, 2016 SPC reports include Keller wind damage entries (fence damage, Bradley Ct).
  https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/160308_rpts.html
- Electric: utility officials blamed tree branches on overhead lines for 2026 outages in the Southlake and Keller
  service areas of Tri-County Electric Cooperative (Hoodline, Aug 2026). TCEC outage line 817-444-3201 / text
  OUTAGE to 25069. https://hoodline.com/2026/08/southlake-mayor-files-state-complaint-over-repeat-power-outages/
  https://tcectexas.com/outage-center
- HOA: Texas Property Code 202.011 (see colleyville.md).

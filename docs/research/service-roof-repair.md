# Research notes: /roof-repair/ (roof repair in dallas), Oct 14 2026

Verified (search results from the primary source):
- City of Dallas storm damage repair info: minor repairs to roofing or roof vents need no permit; temporary tarps need no permit; replacing large areas or the whole roof needs a re-roof permit; replacing structural members (joists, rafters, wall sections) needs a building permit; a registered contractor must be on the application; homeowners doing their own work file a Homeowner's Exemption from Contractor Registration form; questions 214-948-4480.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx
- NWS Fort Worth: DFW averages 20.2 days a year at 100 F or above (1991 to 2020 normals), nearly all June to September; record 71 in 2011.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/d100info
- NRCA: inspect roofs at least twice a year, spring and fall, plus after severe weather; owners should look only from the ground.
  https://www.nrca.net/news-events/press-room/storm-roof-repairs ; https://www.nrca.net/roofingguidelines/pdf?id=169193&amp=&k=2173279
- TDI deductible and public adjuster rules (shared, per WRITING-GUIDE): https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/can-a-contractor-waive-my-deductible.html

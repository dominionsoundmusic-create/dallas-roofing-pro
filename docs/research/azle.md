# Azle, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing azle tx (110). Page: /roofing-azle-tx/

## County / city
- City west of Fort Worth in Parker and Tarrant counties; 13,369 at the 2020 census (Census table on
  citypopulation.de: 13,362; 2024 estimate 15,046). Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azle,_Texas ;
  https://www.citypopulation.de/en/usa/texas/tarrant_parker/4805168__azle
- First recorded settlement 1846 (Dr. James Azle Steward); post office 1881 as O'Bar, renamed Azle 1883 at
  Steward's request. Farming, then dairy in the early 1900s (milk to Fort Worth creameries). Population 800
  in 1940, 2,696 in 1960, 5,822 in 1980, 9,600 by 2000. Source: Wikipedia above.

## Eagle Mountain Lake
- Formed by a dam on the West Fork of the Trinity River, east of Azle; owned and operated by the Tarrant
  Regional Water District. Construction January 23, 1930 to October 24, 1932; impoundment began February 28,
  1934. TWDB 2018 survey: about 9,246 acres at conservation pool.
  Sources: https://www.twdb.texas.gov/surfacewater/rivers/reservoirs/eagle_mountain/index.asp ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle_Mountain_Lake

## Permits / registration (City of Azle, cityofazle.org)
- Roofing page: residential shingle replacement does not require a permit; replacing the entire roof decking
  goes through a Miscellaneous Permit application; commercial roofs start with an email to
  permits@cityofazle.org with the address and project description. Source: https://www.cityofazle.org/701/Roofing
- Contractors & Registration: no general contractor registration; mechanical, electrical, plumbing
  contractors register (no fee); backflow $100. Source: https://www.cityofazle.org/736/Contractors-Registration
- Permit desk 817-444-2541 ext. 102, permits@cityofazle.org. Source: https://www.cityofazle.org/154/Permits

## Electric
- Tri-County Electric Cooperative has an office at 600 NW Parkway, Azle (main office in Aledo); report
  outages by texting OUTAGE to 25069 or calling 817-444-3201; serves more than 110,000 members in 16 North
  Texas counties. Sources: https://tcectexas.com/contact-us ; https://tcectexas.com/outage-center

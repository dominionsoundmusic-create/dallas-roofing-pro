## Growth and housing age
- Population 1,696 (1950), 27,526 (1960), 55,131 (1970): post-war boom fed by returning veterans and Dallas housing shortage. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesquite,_Texas ; https://www.visitmesquitetx.com/p/articles1/mesquite-texas-from-sleepy-train-depot-to-bustling-midcentury-suburb
- Town East Mall opened 1971; I-635 (LBJ) completed by about 1970 linking Mesquite with Garland and Balch Springs. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesquite,_Texas
- Mesquite Championship Rodeo opened as a professional rodeo in 1958; Legislature recognized Mesquite as Rodeo Capital of Texas. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesquite_Championship_Rodeo

## Permits and contractor licensing (City of Mesquite)
- "A professional license is required before you can obtain a building permit." License obtained through the city's Citizen Self Service (CSS) portal. Only a licensed contractor or a homeowner who owns, lives in and homesteads the home (Dallas Central Appraisal District) may get permits through CSS. https://www.cityofmesquite.com/faq.aspx?TID=26
- When is a permit required: new construction, repairs, remodels, windows, exterior doors and more; cosmetic work (paint, carpet, cabinets) does not. https://www.cityofmesquite.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=457
- Building Inspection: 972-216-6212, Mon to Fri 8 to 5. https://www.cityofmesquite.com/354/Building-Inspection
- City's 2024 rental inspection checklist lists Roofs with the permit pulled by the general contractor, homeowner or property manager and inspections scheduled in the CSS portal. https://www.cityofmesquite.com/DocumentCenter/View/27554/Rental-Inspection-Checklist

## Storm history
- March 9 2019, about 7:35 a.m.: NWS-confirmed EF-0 tornado (75 to 85 mph) from the Allen Drive / Cedar Crest area east over I-635 and south Belt Line Road, lifting near Wilkinson and Paza. Roofs and fences damaged on Allen Drive, one home made uninhabitable, part of a church roof blown off with metal landing on I-635; no injuries reported.

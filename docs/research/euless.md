# Euless research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing euless tx (140/mo, SD 10). County: Tarrant. Incorporated city. Electric: Oncor (default;
nothing found otherwise).

## Verified
- Permits: the City of Euless Building Construction and Permitting page says the city does NOT require a
  permit for the replacement of residential roofing materials (residential re-roof); the contractor must
  follow the 2021 International Residential Code. Commercial roofing is listed among projects that do
  need a permit. The page links a Contractor Registration Application (also on the Development Forms
  page). The city is moving permitting and planning submittals to a new Cityworks self-service portal.
  Building permit line 817-685-1630.
  https://www.eulesstx.gov/departments/planning-and-economic-development/building-construction-and-permitting
  https://www.eulesstx.gov/departments/planning-and-economic-development/development-forms
- April 3, 2012 (NWS Fort Worth): tornado outbreak across North Texas; hail was a major hazard across the
  Metroplex; about 110 airplanes at DFW International Airport were damaged by hail and taken out of
  service. EF-2 tornadoes in Kennedale/Arlington and Lancaster/Dallas.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012dfwairport
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april32012sum
- DFW Airport: its southwestern portion lies inside Euless city limits (Wikipedia).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euless,_Texas
- Name: Elisha Adam Euless, Tennessee native, came to Texas in 1867, later Tarrant County sheriff (Wikipedia).
- Schools: Texas Historical Commission marker: Bell Helicopter and Fort Worth Municipal Airport growth led to
  a 1955 merger of the Euless and Hurst school districts; Bedford joined in 1958, forming
  Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD. https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5507015815
- May 27, 2024 (SPC storm report log, file 240527, FWD office): baseball-size (2.75 in) hail 2 SSW of Euless
  ("Baseball size hail euless"); hen egg (2.00 in) 2 SW Euless; ping pong ball (1.50 in) 3 S Euless; 2.75 in
  2 SE of Hurst. https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/240527_rpts_hail.csv

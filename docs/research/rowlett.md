## County and setting
- Dallas and Rockwall counties; bordered east and south by Lake Ray Hubbard, west by Rowlett Creek. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowlett,_Texas
- Lake Ray Hubbard completed 1971; building boom followed. Census: 1,015 (1960), 2,243 (1970), 7,522 (1980), 23,260 (1990), 44,503 (2000), 56,199 (2010), 62,535 (2020). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowlett,_Texas
- Legislature resolutions describe "30 miles of beautiful Lake Ray Hubbard shoreline". https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/86R/billtext/html/SR00266F.htm

## Permits and registration (City of Rowlett)
- Permit required when installing a new roof. https://www.rowletttx.gov/933/Residential ; https://rowletttx.gov/101/Building-Safety
- "It is mandatory that a contractor register with the city before beginning any work." Roofers fall under the general contractor category (Home Builder, Roofer, Pool, Fencing, Sign). https://rowletttx.gov/Faq.aspx?TID=27 ; https://www.rowletttx.gov/2233/Contractor-Registration
- Fee conflict between form ($100) and page (no annual fee): page states no fee amount.
- Permit applications and contractor registrations through the city's online system (MyGov). https://www.rowletttx.gov/1815/Online-Permitting
- Building Inspections 5702 Rowlett Road, 972-412-6125. https://www.rowletttx.gov/Directory/Home/DepartmentListing?DID=62

## Storm history
- Dec 26 2015 EF-4 tornado (Sunnyvale-Garland-Rowlett); Rowlett portion rated EF-3; path through southeast Rowlett. NWS lists 1 death in Rowlett. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dec26tornadoes ; https://www.weather.gov/media/fwd/dec26outbreak/products/pns.pdf
- CBS DFW one year later: 1,296 homes in Rowlett damaged or destroyed; nearly 1,170 rebuilt or under construction; Harbor Drive among hardest hit. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/rowlett-one-year-after-the-ef-4-tornado
- WFAA eight years later: mayor said only four slabs remained. https://www.wfaa.com/article/news/local/eight-years-later-remembering-the-tragedy-i-30-bridge-tornado-rowlett-garland/287-21d958ac-10c2-4308-90ce-efecd9e8a994
- Oct 20 2019: NWS found EF1 tornado damage on Hickox Road (Rowlett) near PGBT, est. 100 mph. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/tornadoes-october2019

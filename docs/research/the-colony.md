# The Colony research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- History (city): Fox and Jacobs, a Dallas developer, bought about 2,500 acres on the eastern shore of Lake Lewisville (city says 1973; Taylor Morrison history says 1969 and construction began 1972); first families moved in October 1974; by January 1977 over 3,500 residents voted to incorporate; name honors the Peters Colony. https://www.thecolonytx.gov/692/City-History ; https://blog.taylormorrison.com/2018/04/a-brief-history-of-the-colony-texas/
- County: Denton County. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Colony,_Texas
- Permit: as of Oct. 1, 2025 the city requires a permit for roof replacement, siding replacement and foundation repair (Building Inspections page). Re-roof requirements sheet: permit required for sheathing and/or roof replacements; built to 2024 IRC (R902, R903, R904, R905, R908); photos of installed underlayment showing all sides and proper lapping may be submitted online; final inspection verifies materials and code compliance, including attic check (vent piping through the roof, rafters in position, water heater and A/C exhaust vents reinstalled); a waiver stating the same is required. https://www.thecolonytx.gov/283/Building-InspectionsPermits ; https://www.thecolonytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/13585/Residential-Re-Roof-Requirements
- Fee: residential general permit application lists a Re-Roof fee of $75 for single family. https://www.thecolonytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/10008/Residential-General-Permit-Application
- Contractor registration: all contractors working in the city must register; Roofing Contractor is a registration type; $75; valid one year from date paid; driver's license copy; permits not issued if registration or state license not current; homeowners acting as own GC use a Homeowner Registration Form, no fee. https://www.thecolonytx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/613/Contractor-Registration-Form-PDF ; https://thecolonytx.gov/293/Contractors
- City phone on re-roof sheet: 972-624-3100.
- Tornado: NWS confirmed a brief EF-0 tornado in The Colony on a Tuesday; minimal damage from the tornado itself; most damage from straight-line winds; CBS: roofs torn up, fences down, sheds destroyed, Red Cross helped house several families. https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/brief-ef-0-tornado-confirmed-in-the-colony/2037744/ ; https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/colony-residents-brace-for-rains-days-after-tornado-hit
- Electric: Oncor named by most sources (Choose Texas Power); default safety box wording used.

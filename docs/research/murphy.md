# Murphy research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-murphy-tx/ . Target: roofing murphy tx (10).

## Verified
- County: Collin.
- Re-roof requirements sheet: permit application; existing roof coverings must be removed before a new covering (no overlays); minimum Class C roof covering; approved materials include composition (30-year), standing seam metal, terra-cotta tile, concrete tile or slate in muted colors; wood shingle, shake, and metal systems of the agricultural or industrial kind are prohibited; fees per Code of Ordinances Article A9.000. https://murphytx.org/DocumentCenter/View/4924/Re-roofing-Permit-Requirements
- Contractor registration: all contractors and subcontractors must be registered with the City of Murphy; permits cannot be issued until all contractors are registered. https://www.murphytx.org/DocumentCenter/View/25/Building-Permit-Application ; https://www.murphytx.org/116/Permits
- Inspections: inspection line 972-468-4050; requests by 2 pm reviewed for next business day; permit must be posted on site; no inspections while work is in progress. https://www.murphytx.org/2018/Request-Inspection ; City Hall 206 N. Murphy Road, 972-468-4000; customer service 972-468-4100.
- History/growth: settlers 1846 (Peters Colony); renamed for William Murphy when railroad arrived 1888; incorporated 1958; population 3,099 (2000) to 21,013 (2020); President George Bush Turnpike opened 2000. https://murphytx.org/99/History ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murphy,_Texas
- Storms: WFAA reported homeowners in Murphy and Wylie could wait weeks for roof work during a run of hailstorms (article undated in results; consistent with spring 2016). https://www.wfaa.com/article/news/local/collin-county/hail-storm-damage-could-top-1-billion/287-131769497 . The CoreLogic 2016 swath ran from near Lewisville to east of Wylie (does not name Murphy).
- Electric: Oncor (no co-op found for Murphy).

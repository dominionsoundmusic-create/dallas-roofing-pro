# Waxahachie, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing waxahachie tx (260). Page: /roofing-waxahachie-tx/

## County / city
- County seat of Ellis County; 2020 census 41,140; city planning estimate 53,000 as of June 1, 2026.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waxahachie,_Texas ; https://www.waxahachietx.gov/618/Demographics
- Nickname "Gingerbread City"; many Victorian and Gingerbread-style homes from the late 1800s/early 1900s boom;
  Richardsonian Romanesque Ellis County Courthouse.
  Sources: https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/waxahachie-tx ; https://www.waxahachietx.gov/242/About-Waxahachie
- West End Historic District: National Register 1986, 77 acres, Classical Revival and Queen Anne buildings.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_End_Historic_District_(Waxahachie,_Texas)
- City website moved to waxahachietx.gov. Electric: Oncor serves the city (generic box used).

## Permits / registration / historic review
- City adopted 2021 International codes and 2020 NEC (effective June 1, 2024). Permits through the CSS portal
  (apply, pay, schedule inspections). Permitting page lists a Residential Remodel Permit Submittal Guide and a
  Contractor Registration form. Building Inspection 469-309-4020.
  Sources: https://www.waxahachietx.gov/294/Permitting-Applications ; https://www.waxahachietx.gov/289/Building-Inspection ;
  https://www.waxahachietx.gov/508/CSS-Portal
- Historic: Certificate of Appropriateness (Sec. 24-56) required for alteration, restoration or new construction
  in a historic overlay district, National Register properties and designated landmarks; Heritage Preservation
  Commission. Source: https://www.waxahachietx.gov/363/Certificate-of-Appropriateness
- Tax incentive guidance lists roof replacement among routine maintenance that does not change historic
  appearance; routine residential maintenance does not require a COA; work without prior COA approval disqualifies
  a property from the Historic Property Tax Incentive Program for that year.
  Source: https://www.waxahachietx.gov/366/Historic-Tax-Incentives
- Accessory structure guide quotes 2021 IRC: asphalt shingles only on slopes 2:12 or greater; double underlayment
  for 2:12 to 4:12. Source: https://www.waxahachietx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/2303/Residential-Accessory-Structure-Submittal-Guide

## Storm history
- Saturday October 13, 2018: NWS confirmed a high-end EF-0 tornado touched down at the new Waxahachie High
  School (opened that August); shattered windows, busted door, bus overturned, light poles down; ~25 theater
  students inside, no injuries; NOAA Storm Events: just southwest of the school, max winds 85 mph.
  Sources: https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/nws-confirms-tornado-waxahachie-high-school/ ;
  https://www.fox4news.com/news/saturday-storms-damage-new-waxahachie-high-school ;
  https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/stormevents/eventdetails.jsp?id=788306

# Denton research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- County seat of Denton County; city population 139,869 (2020), 20th most populous city in Texas; county 906,422 (2020). University of North Texas founded 1890 (renamed UNT 1988), Texas Woman's University 1901. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton,_Texas ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denton_County,_Texas
- Roof permit: since June 1, 2022, the city requires a roof permit for all residential roof or re-roof projects. Replacing decking also needs a Residential Alteration Permit. Contractor must be registered with the City of Denton as a general contractor; permit obtained before work starts, including tear-off. Permit posted visible from the street; attic interior inspection required; drip edge required on asphalt shingle roofs; changing roof material requires plan review; solar panels removed and reinstalled need a licensed electrician on the permit. Inspections: roof decking (if applicable) and roof final. https://www.cityofdenton.com/672/Roofing
- Registration through eTRAKiT, reviewed by Building Safety before account activation; first-time registration in person with government photo ID; expires one year from issue. https://www.cityofdenton.com/668/Contractor-Registration
- Fee schedule dated 10/08/2025: residential reroof permit $50; general contractor registration $66. Development Services, 401 N. Elm St., (940) 349-8600. https://www.cityofdenton.com/DocumentCenter/View/3590/Permit-and-Fee-Schedule-PDF
- Historic: three local historic districts, Oak-Hickory, West Oak Area, Bell Avenue. A Certificate of Appropriateness is needed for exterior repairs and alterations in those districts and for local landmarks, Recorded Texas Historic Landmarks and National Register landmarks. From Jan 1 2026 COA applications follow a submittal calendar. Past Historic Landmark Commission agendas include roof replacement COAs (2013 W Hickory St in Oak-Hickory; 2014 a landmark on Bolivar St). https://www.cityofdenton.com/250/Certificate-of-Appropriateness
- April 3, 2014: NWS Fort Worth event page lists 4.25 inch hail in Denton and an 82 mph gust at Denton Municipal Airport. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/storms040314
- April 30, 2019: NWS Fort Worth confirmed two EF-1 tornadoes in Denton County; the Denton tornado had max winds of 90 mph and first touched down near the Texas Woman's University campus; another EF-1 east of Justin. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/nws-confirms-2-ef1-tornados-denton-county-tuesdays-storms ; City press release on the April 30, 2019 storm event: https://lfpubweb.cityofdenton.com/PublicWeblink/4/edoc/18186/050119%20Press%20Release%20-%20April%2030,%202019%20Storm%20Event%20(May%202019).pdf
- Electric: Denton Municipal Electric (city utility). Report outages and downed poles or wires to city dispatch (940) 349-7000; Oncor serves other parts of Denton County. https://www.cityofdenton.com/378/Utility-Outages

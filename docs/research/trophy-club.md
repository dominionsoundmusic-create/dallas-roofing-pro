# Trophy Club research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- Town mainly in Denton County with a very small part in Tarrant County; west of DFW Airport, south of the western edge of Grapevine Lake, along SH 114. 2020 census 13,688. Established in the 1970s as one of the earliest planned communities in Texas, built around the only golf course designed by Ben Hogan. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trophy_Club,_Texas
- Incorporated January 19, 1985 (title company community guide). https://www.independencetitle.com/wp-content/uploads/pdfs/DiscoverTrophyClub.pdf
- Permits: combined trade permit form covers re-roofs (with plumbing, electrical, fences, etc.); submit to permitting@trophyclub.org; Community Development 682-237-2917; Town Hall 1 Trophy Wood Drive. https://www.trophyclub.org/187/Permitting
- Fee schedule: re-roofing permit $85. https://www.trophyclub.org/DocumentCenter/View/113/Permitting-Fee-Schedule-PDF
- Contractor registration: by email form with driver's license copy, license copy where a trade has one, and proof of insurance naming the town; good one year; no fee. Permitting page describes registration through the online portal; pages differ on method. https://www.trophyclub.org/188/Contractor-Registration
- March 14, 2024: NWS Fort Worth said the largest hailstones, about 1.5 to 3 inches, fell in Denton and Collin counties, including near Argyle, Bartonville, Corinth, Double Oak, Frisco, Highland Village, Lake Dallas, Lincoln Park, Prosper, Roanoke and Trophy Club. NBC DFW gallery includes hail in Trophy Club. https://www.aol.com/live-weather-updates-storm-damage-004426788.html ; https://www.nbcdfw.com/weather/weather-connection/photos-large-hail-falls-as-severe-storms-roll-through-north-texas-on-march-14-2024/3489258/
- Lightning: CBS News Texas reported five Trophy Club homes caught fire from lightning strikes on a Sunday; two had roofs and second floors destroyed, one serious damage, two minor; no injuries. https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/lightning-strikes-five-homes-in-trophy-club

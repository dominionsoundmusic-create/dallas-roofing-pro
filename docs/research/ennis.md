# Ennis, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing ennis tx (30). Page: /roofing-ennis-tx/ (small town: shorter page)

## County / city
- Incorporated city in eastern Ellis County; 2020 census 20,159 (2023 estimate 23,686). One of the largest
  Czech communities in Texas; National Polka Festival every Memorial Day weekend (founding year differs by source:
  not used); Bluebonnet Trails each spring.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennis,_Texas ; https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/ennis-tx ;
  https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/online/articles/xfn03
- Downtown historic district: about seven to eight blocks, more than 80 railroad-era buildings (CBS Texas).
  Ennis joined the Texas Main Street Program in January 2015 (Wikipedia).
- Electric: Oncor serves the city area generally; generic box used.

## Permits and registration (City of Ennis Planning, Development and Inspections)
- Building permit required before constructing, altering, enlarging, moving, demolishing or repairing any
  residential or commercial structure in city limits. Roofing not named; ask the city.
  Source: https://www.ennistx.gov/departments/PlanningDevelopment/DoesMyProjectRequireaPermit
- All work must be done by a licensed trade contractor or general contractor registered with the city, or by
  the homeowner under a homestead permit. Contractor Registration application on the city's site.
  Source: https://www.ennistx.gov/departments/PlanningDevelopment/ContractorsRegistration
- Since June 5, 2024 all building permit applications through the online portal (OpenGov); permit holder
  schedules inspections through the portal; call 972-875-6442 before beginning work.
  Source: https://www.ennistx.gov/departments/PlanningDevelopment/Permits
- FY2022 fee schedule listed a roofing miscellaneous charge; outdated, not used.

## Storm history
- May 15, 2013 (Wednesday night): NWS rated the Ennis tornado EF-1, peak wind about 90 mph, on the ground about
  six miles, started west of I-45 and crossed the highway south of Ennis Avenue; downtown hardest hit including
  historic district buildings (roof and structural damage); city officials: 25 residential and 40 commercial
  structures severely damaged; no deaths, one injury from flying glass. Afterward demolition permit applications
  came in; the city offered incentives to buyers who would restore damaged buildings.
  Sources: https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/downtown-ennis-damaged-in-storm/2075356 ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/storm-leaves-dozens-of-ennis-structures-severely-damaged ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/ennis-downtown-historic-district-prospering-after-2013-tornado
- March 4, 2004: NWS Fort Worth survey of an F1 tornado path into Ennis: unroofed a mobile home, overturned
  semi trailers in southern Ennis, removed most of the roof of a warehouse/office building along I-45.
  Source: https://www.weather.gov/fwd/tor030404

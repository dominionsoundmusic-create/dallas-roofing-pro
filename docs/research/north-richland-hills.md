# North Richland Hills research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing north richland hills tx (50). County: Tarrant.

## Verified
- Contractor registration: every contractor doing work that requires permits and inspections must be registered with the city; online via NRH E-Portal; no registration fee listed. https://www.nrhtx.com/FAQ.aspx?QID=204 ; https://www.nrhtx.com/105/Fee-Schedule
- Building Inspections FAQ (newer version): one and two family dwellings do not need a permit to replace shingles only; permits required for re-roofs that include repair or replacement of decking; work must still meet IRC Chapter 9. Older FAQ text said all re-roofing must be permitted unless 3 squares or less: versions conflict, so the page tells readers to confirm. Roofs with two or more layers must be completely removed; underlayment must be replaced when re-shingling; if damaged decking or rafters are found after tear-off, the permit must be modified; permits valid 30 days after issue; work may not start until permit is issued and displayed. Homestead owners may do own work (homeowner affidavit); other roofing permits only to a registered contractor. All commercial re-roofs need a permit; removed insulation generally replaced to meet the energy code.
  https://www.nrhtx.com/554/FAQs ; https://www.nrhtx.com/1310/Permit-Information
- Dec 13, 2022: NWS-rated EF-1 tornado (peak about 90 mph) with about a 1.5 mile damage path through North Richland Hills, developing near Sayers Lane west of Davis Blvd; police said about 20 homes and businesses damaged; police described a path from around Smithfield/Starnes northeast toward Precinct Line/North Tarrant.

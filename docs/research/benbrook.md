## Benbrook Lake
- Dam on the Clear Fork of the Trinity River; flood control, authorized after repeated flooding (Rivers and
  Harbors Act of 1945). Built by the Corps of Engineers (Galveston District), operated by the Fort Worth
  District. Construction began May 27, 1947, completed December 1950; impoundment began September 29, 1952.
  Sources: https://www.twdb.texas.gov/surfacewater/rivers/reservoirs/benbrook/index.asp ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benbrook_Lake

## Permits / registration (City of Benbrook)
- All work must be performed by a contractor registered with the City of Benbrook, unless a homestead
  exemption is on file with the appraisal district (FAQ). Registration: state license copy where relevant,
  owner's driver's license, proof of at least $300,000 liability insurance; expires one year after
  application. Sources: https://www.benbrook-tx.gov/Faq.aspx?QID=61 ; https://www.benbrook-tx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5080
- Permit FAQ: a building permit is required for any new structure, addition or remodeling; re-roof not
  spelled out. A city permit log has a roof repair/replacement category. Page says ask the department.
- Benbrook's local amendments to the 2012 IBC: materials and methods for recovering or replacing a roof
  covering must comply with Chapter 15. Source: https://benbrook-tx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/880/2012-International-Building-Code-Local-Amendments
- Permits and Inspections: Benbrook City Hall, 911 Winscott Road; 817-249-6064; inspection request line
  817-249-6000, one day ahead. Sources: https://www.benbrook-tx.gov/200/Building-Permits-and-Inspections ;
  https://www.benbrook-tx.gov/656/Building-Inspection

## Storms
- March 2016: NWS confirmed an EF-0 tornado near the Benbrook Lake marina, about 85 mph winds; it tore up
  the dry dock area and pushed boats onto each other; one of five tornadoes in North Texas in 24 hours.
  Sources: https://www.keranews.org/news/2016-03-09/latest-ef-0-tornado-confirmed-in-benbrook-bringing-total-to-five-tornadoes-in-24-hours ;
  https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/ef-0-tornado-confirmed-in-benbrook/2037825
- WFAA: storm stripped the roof of a church on the Benbrook / Fort Worth border, homes and cars damaged in
  southwest Tarrant County (date not shown in results). Source: https://www.wfaa.com/article/news/local/homes-cars-roofs-ravaged-by-storms-in-southwest-tarrant-county/287-426605130

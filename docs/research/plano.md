# Plano research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-contractors-plano-tx/ . Target: plano tx roofing companies (720).

## Verified
- County: Collin and Denton (site.json; Plano straddles both).
- Permit: Plano's roof permit page says a roof permit is required on all re-roof projects (verified in WRITING-GUIDE.md: https://www.plano.gov/220/Building-Inspections). An older city re-roof sheet (2015 IRC era) says work not exceeding 25% of the roof does not need a permit: CONFLICT, so the page says "all re-roofs" per the current page and tells readers to confirm. https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/0569d14b-21e6-4de5-b30b-910fc33fdfd6
- Final inspection: attic check of vent piping through the roof, rafters in position, water heater and furnace exhaust vents reinstalled properly (city roof permit page and older sheet). Old sheet: unpackaged shingles ("seconds") not allowed; check deed restrictions/HOA. Same civicplus sheet.
- Contractor registration: Plano Building Inspections registration lists roof companies under the General Contractor category, $100 annual fee, driver's license; contractors must be registered before doing any work. https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/tx-plano/5259a213-2b04-4a12-95e1-9babd95fd81f?cache=1800 ; registration form https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/tx-plano/5b6abca4-aa3e-42da-9672-37a09a2d77c3?cache=1800 ; city publishes a registered general contractors roster https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/tx-plano/deec6af1-0b02-4b70-88ee-e68f065dfcbe/active-general-contractors.pdf
- Building Inspections: 1520 K Avenue (suite numbers differ between sources: 140 vs 220, so page gives street only); 972-941-7140 listed on registration doc. https://www.plano.gov/217/Building-Inspections
- June 13 2012: NWS Fort Worth: storms pummeled parts of Dallas, Collin and Fannin counties with baseball-size hail, numerous cars and houses severely damaged, estimated $900 million, one of the costliest hailstorms in Texas. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/june132012
- March 23 2016: NBC DFW: Plano took the brunt; hundreds of homes damaged. https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/hail-damages-hundreds-of-plano-homes/102420/ . CoreLogic (Insurance Journal, July 2016): Plano event losses $35.8 million, 263,440 homes in the Plano storm footprint; hail 1.5 to 2 in across a swath of nearly 50 miles from near Lewisville to east of Wylie. https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2016/07/25/420542.htm
- Historic districts: Plano has locally designated districts including Haggard Park and Downtown (Planning). https://www.plano.gov/locally-designated-districts
- Electric: Oncor (default).

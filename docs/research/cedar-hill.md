# Cedar Hill, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing cedar hill tx (30). Page: /roofing-cedar-hill-tx/

## County / city
- Mostly Dallas County; a small southern section of the city lies in Ellis County. 2020 census 49,148.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedar_Hill,_Texas
- High ground on the escarpment (White Rock / Balcones escarpment). Elevation figures differ: Wikipedia 791 ft; city nature
  page says high point 880 ft above sea level and that the city sits at the northern edge of the Balcones Escarpment.
  Page says "about 800 feet and more", citing city page for 880. Sources: https://cedarhilltx.com/3212/Nature-Open-Space ;
  Wikipedia above.
- Dallas County Cedar Mountain Preserve (west side of FM 1382 between Northwood University and Cedar Hill State Park), over
  700 ft, some of the highest points in Dallas County. Source: https://www.dallascounty.org/departments/plandev/openspaces/locations/18-cedar-mountain.php
- Cedar Hill State Park is on Joe Pool Lake; state acquired it 1982, opened 1991. Source: https://tpwd.texas.gov/state-parks/cedar-hill/history
- Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center adjacent to Cedar Mountain; Cedar Ridge Preserve managed by Audubon Dallas (Dallas County page).
- Best Southwest Partnership founding city (1986). Source: https://bestsouthwest.org/about-us/area-overview
- Electric: Oncor assumed (generic safety box).

## Permits and registration (City of Cedar Hill Building Inspections)
- A person or company acting as general contractor must register with the City; $100 fee for general contractors.
  State-licensed trades register annually with proof of license. Building Inspections, Government Center,
  285 Uptown Boulevard; weekdays 8 to 5; 972-291-5100 ext. 1090. Source: https://www.cedarhilltx.com/383/Contractor-Registration
- Inspections requested through the online portal, email or 972-291-5100 ext. 1093, before 4 p.m. the day before.
  Source: https://www.cedarhilltx.com/384/Permit-Process
- Monthly "permits issued by type" reports list residential reroof permits (many each month; fees around $100 in
  2025 to 2026 reports: fee NOT stated on page as an official fee). Source: https://cedarhilltx.com/DocumentCenter/View/47016/2026-April-Permit-Issue-Report?bidId=

## Storm history
- 1856 tornado hit the town, killing nine people and destroying most of its buildings (Wikipedia). Dallas Daily Herald
  May 10 1856 account reprinted by Dallas Pioneer, struck about an hour after sunrise.
  Sources: Wikipedia above; https://dallaspioneer.org/the-april-29-1856-cedar-hill-tornado/

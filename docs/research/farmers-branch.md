# Farmers Branch research notes (Oct 14 2026)

## Verified
- County: Dallas County. 1960 census 13,441 residents, a 1,369 percent increase over 1950 (rapid 1950s growth). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farmers_Branch,_Texas
- Midcentury modern homes are a recognized part of the city's housing (D Magazine, Sept 2018, "The story of how midcentury modern came to Farmers Branch"). https://www.dmagazine.com/publications/d-magazine/2018/september/the-story-of-how-midcentury-modern-came-to-farmers-branch/
- Demo/Rebuild program: residential incentive offering grants to residents and builders to demolish and rebuild homes in a city with aging housing stock and little land; reported terms (2019-era) up to $5,000 upfront plus seven years of city taxes paid back; terms may have changed. https://candysdirt.com/?p=136242 ; https://dallasnews.com/news/2014/01/31/farmers-branch-moves-forward-with-revitalization-program
- Building Inspection Division administers building, zoning and property maintenance ordinances; permits via Citizen Self Service portal. https://www.farmersbranchtx.gov/190/Building-Inspections-Permits ; https://www.farmersbranchtx.gov/806/Building-Permit-Forms
- May 24, 2015 EF-1 tornado: began in Dallas near Walnut Hill and Marsh, crossed LBJ Freeway at Josey Lane into Farmers Branch, where an apartment complex had roof damage; 100 to 105 mph, 5.1-mile path, no injuries. https://www.iweathernet.com/dallas-fort-worth/tornadoes-confirmed-dallas-irving-sunday-may-24-2015 ; https://www.fox4news.com/news/ef-1-tornadoes-hit-irving-farmers-branch-overnight
- June 16, 2019 (Sunday) EF-0: NWS confirmed Tuesday June 18 a brief EF-0 near Mustang Trails Park at 3:45 p.m., about 85 mph; Villa Creek apartments roofs damaged; 14 families displaced; no injuries. https://www.keranews.org/news/2019-06-18/weather-service-confirms-4-tornadoes-struck-on-sunday ; https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/families-displaced-tornado-storm-damages-farmers-branch-apartment ; https://www.wfaa.com/article/weather/4th-tornado-from-sundays-storms-confirmed-in-farmers-branch-national-weather-service-says/287-cb625a28-83fa-4a99-9f8f-3849fc1f82f9
- Electric: Oncor default (generic safety box).

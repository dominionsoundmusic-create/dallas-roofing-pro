## County / city
- Incorporated city spanning Dallas and Tarrant counties with a small part in Ellis County; 1990 census split
  81,527 Dallas, 18,086 Tarrant, rest Ellis. Source: TSHA text via https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/dallas/towns-communities/TSHA/tsha-grand-prairie.htm
- A portion of Joe Pool Lake is in southern Grand Prairie (same source). USACE: Joe Pool Lake lies partly in
  Grand Prairie, Dallas, Cedar Hill, Mansfield and Midlothian; touches Tarrant, Dallas, Ellis counties; Loyd
  Park and Lynn Creek Park among developed parks (archived page ~2007).
  Source: https://www.swf-wc.usace.army.mil/joepool/Information/Directions.shtml
- Electric: Oncor service area (generic safety box used).

## Permits and registration (City of Grand Prairie)
- Residential re-roof permit required for any roof replacement/recovering or repair. Contractor must hold a
  General Contractor registration with the city; applicant needs a CSS portal account to file online. Online
  applications typically processed in about 5 to 10 business days.
  Source: https://www.gptx.org/Departments/Building-Inspections/Residential-Information/Residential-Re-Roof
- General Contractor registration: $75 fee, company info, application, authorized signature, driver's license
  copy; online or at City Hall. Building Inspections 972-237-8230.
  Source: https://www.gptx.org/Departments/Building-Inspections/Contractor-Registration-Forms/General-Contractor-Registration
- Re-roof guideline sheet: inspections follow 2021 International Residential Code; application asks for a brief
  description of work, square footage and valuation; example description: tear off existing shingles, check for
  rot or damage, replace underlayment, flashing, drip edge and vent boots; attach manufacturer specifications.
  IRC rules quoted: recover not allowed where the existing roof has two or more applications of roof covering;
  existing flashings, edgings, outlets, vents that are rusted, damaged or deteriorated must be replaced.
  Source: https://www.gptx.org/files/sharedassets/public/v/2/departments/building-inspections/documents/residential-reroof-requirements.pdf
- Commercial re-roof permit page exists: https://www.gptx.org/Departments/Building-Inspections/Commercial-Information/Commercial-Re-Roof

## Storm history
- March 16, 2023: NWS Fort Worth survey confirmed an EF-1 tornado that formed in the extreme northern part of
  Grand Prairie in a distribution facility area near West Trinity Boulevard and Trinity Ridge Drive, peak wind
  about 105 mph; a section of roofing peeled off a warehouse; then moved into Irving (apartment building damaged).
  Source: NWS PNS text https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/202303180002-KFWD-NOUS44-PNSFWD ;
  https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/ef-1-tornado-confirmed-in-irving-during-thursday-storms/3217161/
- January 2017: tornado confirmed near Grand Prairie airport; doors ripped off a hangar, no injuries.
  Source: https://cbsnews.com/texas/news/powerful-winds-cause-damage-to-hangar-at-grand-prairie-airport

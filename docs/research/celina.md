# Celina research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-celina-tx/ . Target: roofing celina tx (50).

## Verified
- County: Collin and Denton (site.json value; Wikipedia lists both). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celina,_Texas
- Growth: 2020 census 16,739; Census estimates rank Celina the fastest-growing US city of 20,000+ for 2020 to 2023 (grew more than 143%, about 17,800 to about 43,300) and again for 2024 to 2025 (up 24.6% to 64,427), with Princeton, Melissa and Anna also in the top five for 2024 to 2025. https://celinaedc.com/about/news-press-releases/population-rebounds-for-many-cities-in-northeast-and-midwest ; https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/americans-are-flocking-texas-fastest-growing-us-cities-where-rcna163340
- Roof permit: required for roofing projects; general contractor must be registered with the city; submit with a Residential Minor Permit Application; $100 per permit request; the application has a Roof Replacement checkbox and asks whether the deck is being replaced. https://www.celina-tx.gov/1204/Building-Services ; https://www.celina-tx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5689/Residential-Permit-Application
- Contractor registration: all contractors must register before applying for permits, through the MyGov portal (Collaborator account); documents include a valid Texas driver's license and insurance listing the City of Celina as certificate holder; registration expires at the state trade license date or 365 days, whichever first; no permits issued or inspections performed for anyone without current registration. https://www.celina-tx.gov/1985/Contractor-Registration ; https://celina-tx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5672/Contractor-Registration-21
- Tornado: night of May 25 to 26, 2024 (Saturday overnight). NWS rated a satellite tornado on the south edge of a larger storm in northern Collin County a high-end EF-3, estimated 165 mph, path 0.71 miles, up to 175 yards wide, no deaths or injuries recorded. NBC DFW: eight homes damaged on one Celina block, five belonged to Frisco firefighters, some leveled. Fox 4: multiple homes on Prairie Meadow Lane destroyed; close to 200 volunteers came the Monday after. https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/202405300201-KFWD-NOUS44-PNSFWD ; https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/local/tornado-damages-8-frisco-homes/3551483/ ; https://www.fox4news.com/news/frisco-firefighters-celina-tornado
- Electric: City of Celina power outage page lists CoServ, GCEC (Grayson-Collin Electric Cooperative) and Oncor; CoServ outages by texting OUT to (855) 938-3496. https://www.celina-tx.gov/1974/19744/
- Communities: Light Farms (Republic Property Group, over 1,070 acres, between Celina and Prosper) per HAR listing; Mustang Lakes on the Celina and Prosper border per real estate guides. Named only. https://www.har.com/light farms/masterplan_1144/

## County / city
- Dallas County. Residents incorporated a 225-acre city on August 2, 1947, to head off annexation by Dallas; first
  mayor D. L. Hopkins Jr. Rapid growth in the 1950s and 1960s. Home-rule charter with council-manager government after
  population passed 5,000 (1962). Postwar Army landing field became Duncanville Air Force Station (Nike-Hercules HQ).
  Sources: https://atlas.thc.texas.gov/Details/5113006680 ; https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=154591 ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duncanville,_Texas
- Census: 841 (1950), 3,774 (1960), 14,105 (1970), 27,781 (1980). Source: https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/dallas/towns-communities/duncanville.htm
  (another source gives 9,385 for 1960, conflict: page uses only 1950 and 1980 figures).
- Best Southwest Partnership founding city (1986). Source: https://bestsouthwest.org/about-us/area-overview
- Electric: Oncor assumed (generic safety box).

## Permits and registration (City of Duncanville Permit & Inspection Services)
- Contractors must register with the city before working; new contractors register through the Citizen Access Portal
  and receive a login; permit applications through the portal; paper at City Hall, 203 E. Wheatland Road;
  (972) 780-5000, Mon to Fri 8 to 5. Sources: https://www.duncanvilletx.gov/residents/household_services/permit_and_inspection_services ;
  https://www.duncanvilletx.gov/residents/household_services/permit_and_inspection_services/permits_and_applications/what_is_the_duncanville_citizen_access_portal_
- Permit needed to repair, alter or replace building systems; one or more inspections before complete; permits valid
  six months from issue; if homeowner pulls a permit for a contractor's work, the homeowner becomes responsible; city
  recommends the contractor apply in its own name. City advice: insist on evidence of a current state license (roofing is
  not on its list of state-licensed trades). Codes: 2021 IRC.
  Sources: https://www.duncanvilletx.gov/residents/household_services/permit_and_inspection_services/building_inspections ;
  https://www.duncanvilletx.gov/residents/household_services/permit_and_inspection_services/permits_and_applications
- City weekly permit report lists "Reroof Permit" as a permit type (reroof permits issued routinely).
- Fee schedule effective 10/01/2024 lists residential shingles $150 (garbled; city says fees since updated): NOT used.

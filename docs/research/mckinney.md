# McKinney research notes (Oct 14 2026)
Page: /roofing-companies-mckinney-tx/ . Target: roofing companies mckinney tx (390).

## Verified
- County: Collin (county seat). Electric: not stated per neighborhood; page uses the default Oncor-or-local-utility wording.
- Roof permit packet (Building Inspections): permits required for all roofing projects; flat $200 permit fee; drip edge mandatory on asphalt shingle roofs; damaged or deteriorated vents and flashing replaced; existing layers removed under certain conditions; building final checks shingle and flashing installation per manufacturer, plumbing and gas vents reattached and flashed, debris hauled away. Packet notes Texas does not license roofers. The packet copy found references the 2021 IRC and looks several years old, so the page tells readers to confirm the current fee. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/DocumentCenter/View/7376/Roof-Packet
- Building Inspections page lists the roof permit packet among residential resources. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/243/Building-Inspections
- Contractor registration: licensed contractors must register before a permit is issued, through CSS (Citizen Self Service); a general contractor is not required to register separately; the applicant, contractor and every subcontractor on a permit need their own CSS account; a Signature Verification Form is required per project. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/257/Contractor-Registration
- Solicitor's permit required for door-to-door sales (verified by coordinator). https://www.mckinneytexas.org/1400/Solicitors-Permit
- Historic Overlay District: a Certificate of Appropriateness covers changes in materials on any property visible from the public right-of-way; in-kind replacement (cedar shingles with cedar shingles) needs no permission, but the city advises checking with the Historic Preservation Office first; COA application has a Roof Materials section; no fee for COA review; staff can approve projects that meet the Secretary of the Interior's Standards, others go to the Historic Preservation Advisory Board; work without a COA can bring fines or misdemeanor charges. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/faq.aspx?TID=50 ; https://mckinneytexas.org/DocumentCenter/View/17/COA-Application-Packet
- McKinney Residential Historic District on the National Register, roughly bounded by W. Lamar, N. Benge, W. Louisiana and N. Oak, period of significance 1880 to 1937; THC nomination says the L-plan house was the most popular type in McKinney in the 1880s and 1890s. https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/87001744
- Stonebridge Ranch Community Association: pre-approved shingle list gives automatic approval once a Modification Application is submitted; a shingle not on the list (even matching the old roof) needs committee approval; some villages stricter; some villages have a sub-association approval after SRCA; skipping the application can bring violations or fines. https://stonebridgeranch.com/modifications
- Storms (NWS Fort Worth): April 9 to 10 2008, an EF-1 tornado touched down near Custer and Hedgcoxe in Allen and lifted near US 75 and SH 121 in McKinney; damage at Collin County Regional Airport in McKinney appeared to come from downburst winds. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/april09102008 . April 3 2014: 1.75-inch hail at the sheriff's office at Bloomdale Road and US 75; ping pong to golf ball size hail in Craig Ranch; brief EF-0 near Princeton. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/storms040314 . May 2006: spotters reported tornadoes north and northeast of McKinney near Anna and Westminster. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/collintor06
- CBS News Texas: a McKinney neighborhood near SH 121 and Stacy Road hit hard by late-March hail, with repair signs and roof patches still visible and claims still open (article year not shown; page does not give a year). https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/homeowners-prepare-for-another-round-of-possible-severe-storms-in-north-texas

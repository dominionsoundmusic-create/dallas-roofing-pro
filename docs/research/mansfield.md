## County / city
- Incorporated; mainly southeastern Tarrant County with sections in northeastern Johnson and northwestern Ellis
  counties; about 15 miles southeast of Fort Worth. Origins in a place called Walnut Creek.
  Source: https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/mansfield-tx
- 2020 census population 72,602; Mansfield ISD covers the Tarrant and Johnson parts, Ellis part zoned to
  Midlothian ISD. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield,_Texas
- Electric: Oncor serves most of the area (generic safety box wording used).

## Permits (City of Mansfield Building Safety)
- FAQ "Do I need a permit to re-roof?": residential permits required for re-roofing that involves decking or
  structural members; replacing shingles only on one- and two-family homes needs no permit. All commercial
  re-roofs need a permit; removing roof insulation on commercial triggers energy code calculation. Re-roofing
  must meet code even without a permit: flashing and drip edges required; old shingles must be fully removed
  before new ones go on. Source: https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/Faq.aspx?QID=383
- Registration list (Permits & Registration page) names electrical, irrigation, mechanical, plumbing and
  third-party energy contractors; roofers not named. Building Safety 817-276-4220, permits@mansfieldtexas.gov,
  1200 E. Broad St. Submittals online (MyGov). Inspection requests by 4 p.m. inspected next business day.
  Sources: https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/173/Permits-Registration ; https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/171/Permit-Applications-Forms
- City says it cannot recommend contractors; suggests checking how long a contractor has operated locally,
  references, BBB complaints. Source: city FAQ (above, remodeling FAQ list https://www.mansfieldtexas.gov/m/faq)

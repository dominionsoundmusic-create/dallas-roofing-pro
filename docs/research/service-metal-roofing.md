# Research: /metal-roofing/ (Oct 14 2026)

My own searches ran out after one metal query (the shared budget of 200 WebSearch calls was used up). The page uses only facts verified this session, either by me or in other writers' notes (cited there):
- ENERGY STAR roof products specification sunset June 1, 2022; the Cool Roof Rating Council (CRRC-1) directory is the replacement. https://coolroofs.org/news/energy-star-roofing-program-sunsets
- UL 2218 Class 4: a 2-inch steel ball dropped twice on the same spot; IBHS has rated shingles with lab-made hailstones since 2019 (service-hail-damage-roof-repair.md). https://ibhs.org/ibhs-news-releases/ibhs-releases-its-most-expansive-impact-resistant-shingle-ratings-to-date/ ; https://ibhs.org/hail/asphalt-shingle-durability/
- TDI: insurers credit roofing rated under UL 2218, with Class 4 earning the largest credit; each insurer sets its own amount; the label shows class, maker, year and brand; the contractor completes form PC068 (service-roof-replacement notes). https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html ; https://www.tdi.texas.gov/forms/pcpersonal/pc068roofinstal.pdf
- Texas Property Code 202.011 covers SHINGLES (impact-resistant, energy-efficient, solar) that resemble the approved ones; the page says it is written about shingles and does not apply it to metal. https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_202.011
- Angi Dallas metal roofing: average about $12,474, range $4,118 to $20,829. https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-does-metal-roofing-cost/tx/dallas
- JLC Cost vs. Value 2025: metal roofing job cost $44,505 in the West South Central region, $51,865 national, about 50% recouped. https://www.jlconline.com/cost-vs-value/2025/west-south-central/
- Garland: metal roofing 26 gauge or better to manufacturer specs; a change of roof material needs a permit. https://www.garlandtx.gov/3186/Re-Roofing
- Fort Worth Fairmount historic guidelines: metal roofs need a Certificate of Appropriateness; standing seam allowed on secondary slopes; R-panel and rib panel prohibited. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/development-services/documents/all-preservation-and-design/historic/historic-districts/fairmount/section7-roofing.pdf

Left out (not verified): FM 4473, noise data, lifespan figures, insurer cosmetic-damage exclusions (the page only tells readers to ask the insurer), discount percentages, gauge norms beyond Garland.

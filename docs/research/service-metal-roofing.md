# Research: /metal-roofing/ (INCOMPLETE: shared search budget ran out Oct 14 2026)

Verified so far:
- ENERGY STAR roof products specification sunset June 1, 2022; Cool Roof Rating Council (CRRC-1) rated products directory is the main replacement. https://coolroofs.org/news/energy-star-roofing-program-sunsets
- 2021 IECC C402.3 cool-roof values apply to low-slope commercial roofs in climate zones 0 to 3 (see service-commercial-roofing.md); not a residential rule.
- From other writers' notes: Garland re-roof page requires metal roofing 26 gauge or better installed to manufacturer specs (https://www.garlandtx.gov/3186/Re-Roofing); Fort Worth Fairmount historic guidelines: metal roofs need a Certificate of Appropriateness, standing seam allowed on secondary slopes, R-panel and rib panel prohibited.

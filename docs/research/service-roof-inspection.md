# Research: /roof-inspection/ (Oct 14 2026)

- TREC licenses real estate inspectors (license type page) https://www.trec.texas.gov/license-type/real-estate-inspector?page=1
- TREC standards (535.228): inspector inspects roof covering; may depart from walking the roof if it cannot be reached safely or walking could damage the covering, and should say so in the report; drones allowed but must be used competently (TREC guidance in search results). TREC roof FAQ example https://www.trec.texas.gov/does-trec-consider-roof-roofing-felt-ending-short-drip-edge-and-rake-not-overlapped-over-drip-edge

- Texas has no state roofing license; HB 3344 (2025) died (WRITING-GUIDE, TDI/capitol links).

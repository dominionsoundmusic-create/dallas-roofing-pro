## County / city
- Incorporated city, mainly in southern Tarrant County; a 5.8-acre piece extends into Johnson County.
  County front matter: "Tarrant and Johnson". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowley,_Texas
- 2020 census 18,070, up 40.8% from 12,838 in 2010; 1960 population 583. Census QuickFacts July 1 2024
  estimate 20,483. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crowley,_Texas ;
  https://census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/crowleycitytexas,springcdptexas,corpuschristicitytexas/POP060210
- Farming around Deer Creek from about 1848; settlement moved about a mile west when the Gulf, Colorado and
  Santa Fe Railway arrived; named for S. H. Crowley, the railroad's master of transportation. Incorporation
  election February 3, 1951; council changed town to city September 3, 1972. Source: Wikipedia above.

## Permits / registration (City of Crowley)
- "When Do I Need a Permit?" lists roof repairs of 50 square feet or less among work that does not need a
  permit. Source: https://www.ci.crowley.tx.us/cd/page/when-do-i-need-permit
- FAQ: any contractor doing work that requires a permit must register with the city (state license if
  applicable, driver's license, insurance copy). Source: https://ci.crowley.tx.us/cd/faq/contractor-registration
- Permits through the city's MyGov online system (submit plans, register as a contractor). Permits office
  817-297-2201 ext. 3000, permits@ci.crowley.tx.us; inspection requests ext. 3020; 201 E. Main Street.
  Most permits require inspection. Sources: https://www.ci.crowley.tx.us/cd/page/building-permits-inspections ;
  https://www.ci.crowley.tx.us/media/10751

## Town
- Town of Sunnyvale, Dallas County. Incorporated Feb 26 1953 by joining four rural communities: Long Creek, New Hope, Hattersville and Tripp. Name chosen through a school contest (Tripp-Long Creek school; "Sunnyville" was taken). 1953 incorporation allowed only single-family homes on 1-acre lots. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunnyvale,_Texas ; https://www.sunnyvaletx.gov/63/History
- Population 2,693 (2000), 5,130 (2010). https://sites.rootsweb.com/~dalcotx/towns-communities/sunnyvale.htm (2020 count not verified: left out)

## Permits
- All permit applications completed online through the town's portal. https://www.townofsunnyvale.org/71/Building-Permits-and-Inspections ; https://www.townofsunnyvale.org/642/Permits
- Fee schedule had a "Roof/Re-roof" residential line (so re-roofs are permitted work); fee schedule replaced by Ordinance 2025-15 effective Sept 8 2025: amounts not stated on page. https://townofsunnyvale.org/622/Adopted-Codes
- Inspection list includes roofing stage: underlayment, insulation, crickets, flashing, roofing complete. https://townofsunnyvale.org/DocumentCenter/View/6992/Building-Inspections
- Building-related fees list a general contractor registration line. https://www.townofsunnyvale.org/DocumentCenter/View/4390
- Town adopted 2021 International Codes. https://townofsunnyvale.org/622/Adopted-Codes
- Office (972) 203-4188 (not put on page).

## Storm history
- Dec 26 2015: the EF-4 Sunnyvale-Garland-Rowlett tornado (NWS name) began in Sunnyvale; Wikipedia: extensive damage to the Plantation RV park before moving into Garland and Rowlett. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dec26tornadoes ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunnyvale,_Texas

# Research notes: /roof-leak-repair/ (roof leak repair dallas), Oct 14 2026

- NWS Fort Worth normals 1991 to 2020: DFW about 37 inches of precipitation a year; May wettest (about 4.8 in), October second (about 4.4 in); wet spring and fall, drier summer and winter; annual totals have ranged from under 20 to over 50 inches.
  https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dfw_records_normals ; https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dfw_narrative
- EPA: dry water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to help prevent mold; hidden areas like ceilings and attics count too; drying in time is not a promise mold did not start.
  https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home ; https://www.epa.gov/mold/mold-course-chapter-9
- Residential mechanical code (M1411.3.1 / M1411.9.1 depending on edition): an auxiliary or secondary drain pan, separate overflow line, or a water-level shutoff switch is required for cooling coils where a clogged condensate line would damage the building; attic units are the common case. Auxiliary pan drains discharge to a conspicuous point so occupants notice.
  https://up.codes/s/auxiliary-and-secondary-drain-systems
- City of Dallas: minor roof repairs and temporary tarps need no permit.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx
- NRCA: inspect only from the ground after storms.
  https://www.nrca.net/news-events/press-room/storm-roof-repairs

# Bedford research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing bedford tx (10/mo, SD 34). County: Tarrant. Incorporated city. Electric: Oncor (default).

## Verified
- Permits (City of Bedford, "When is a Residential Building Permit Required?"): a permit is required for ALL
  roofing replacement or repairs; it must be issued before work starts and kept on the job site until final
  inspection; the city performs a final roof inspection; a remodel permit is also needed if framing or roof
  pitch changes. The page says contractors must be registered with the City of Bedford (it also mentions
  state licensing; Texas has no state roofing license, so the page tells readers to ask the city what it
  checks). https://bedfordtx.gov/177/When-is-a-Building-Permit-Required
- Registration through the online permit portal (bedfordtx.portal.opengov.com); renewable annually; homeowners
  on their own homestead exempt. https://bedfordtx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/135/Contractor-Registration-Information
  https://bedfordtx.gov/185/Permits-Applications-and-Information
- City publishes lists of registered general contractors (2024 list found).
  https://bedfordtx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/9744/2024-Registered-General-Contractors-Sorted-Alphabetically
- Building Permits and Inspections: 1805 L. Don Dodson Drive, Building C, 817-952-2140; call to check whether a
  contractor is registered. https://bedfordtx.gov/176/Building-Inspections
- History (City of Bedford FY23-24 budget, Tarrant County city budgets): settlers arrived about 1860; Bedford
  Boys Ranch set up on a 253-acre tract in 1949; city incorporated January 22, 1953 with about 475 people on
  about two square miles.
  https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2024/Bedford/files/basic-html/page9.html
- Business View Magazine (2016 Bedford brochure): more than 10,000 residents by 1970; 1970s brought industrial
  park, shopping centers and the completion of DFW Airport nearby; about 20,800 in 1980 and about 44,000 in
  1990; Boys Ranch became the biggest park in 1974.
  https://businessviewmagazine.com/brochures/march-2016/bedford/files/assets/basic-html/page3.html
- Bedford Citizen (Nov 2015, "Housing in Bedford: a look back at 1955"): ranch and Cape styles favored in the
  1950s. https://thebedfordcitizen.org/2015/11/housing-in-bedford-a-look-back-at-1955/
- Hail (SPC storm report logs, preliminary reports):
  May 27, 2024 (file 240527): baseball-size hail 1 N Bedford; hen egg (2.00 in) 1 SW Bedford; golf ball reports.
  https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/240527_rpts_hail.csv
  Aug 1, 2021: golf ball hail near Forest Ridge Drive and Highway 183, southwest Bedford; quarter size near
  Bedford Road and Highway 121. https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/210801_rpts.html

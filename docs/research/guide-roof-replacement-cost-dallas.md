## Permits
- [S] City of Dallas storm repair page: shingle and deck damage: roofing permit over the counter or online; replacing
  large areas or a whole roof needs a re-roof permit; structural members (joists, rafters) need a building permit;
  minor repairs to roofing or roof vents need no permit; tarps need no permit; Oak Cliff Municipal Center, 320 E.
  Jefferson Blvd., Room 118; registered contractors may apply online. Fee schedule page exists.
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/Pages/storm_damage_repair_info.aspx ;
  https://dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/buildinginspection/pages/fees.aspx
- [T] Fort Worth: shingle-only replacement on a one or two-family home needs no permit; decking/structural does;
  permit holders must hold Contractor Registration. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/permits/residential-information
- [T] Plano: permit for all re-roofs. https://www.plano.gov/220/Building-Inspections

## Texas sales tax (Comptroller)
- [S] Labor to repair or remodel residential real property is not taxable. Re-roofing a house is repair, not a "new
  improvement". Lump-sum contract: contractor pays tax on materials when it buys them and does not charge the customer
  tax. Separated contract: contractor buys materials with a resale certificate and collects sales tax (state and local,
  by job site) on the separately stated materials charge; materials charge at least the contractor's cost. Separate
  invoices alone do not turn a lump-sum contract into a separated one.
  https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-116.php ; https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/publications/94-157.php
- Not verified, left out: the exact state and local tax rates; commercial (nonresidential) repair taxability detail.

## Financing and paying
- [S] FTC: never agree to contractor-arranged financing without shopping and comparing; scam where papers signed
  mid-job turn out to be a home equity loan; never sign a document with blanks or that you have not read; no final
  payment until work is done; Cooling-Off Rule: 3 business days to cancel when signed away from the seller's place of
  business. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-home-improvement-scam
- [T] Property Code 53.259: final payment on a residential construction contract conditioned on a bills-paid affidavit.
  https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._prop._code_section_53.259

## County / city
- Incorporated 1949 (petition with 42 voters to the Dallas County judge Feb 17 1949; election March 2 passed 50 to 2).
  Area first settled 1847; post office named DeSoto in 1881. Dallas County. 2020 census 56,145.
  Sources: https://www.desototexas.gov/292/History ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeSoto,_Texas
- Best Southwest Partnership formed 1986 by DeSoto, Cedar Hill, Duncanville and Lancaster; now 11 city partners.
  Source: https://bestsouthwest.org/about-us/area-overview
- Electric: Oncor assumed (generic safety box).

## Permits and registration (City of DeSoto Building Inspections)
- All contractors must register annually; since Sept 5 2025 registration goes through the Customer Self-Service
  (CSS) Portal; Roofing Contractor fee $25; normal turnaround 1 to 2 business days (city notes delays).
  Source: https://www.desototexas.gov/476/Registration
- Paper application (may be dated): $1,000,000 general liability naming the city as certificate holder; valid one
  year. Building Inspections 972-230-9610. Source: https://desototexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1055/Contractor-Registration-Application-PDF?bidId=
- Roofing listed among work needing a permit; apply online; contractor must have active registration approved before
  applying. Sources: https://www.desototexas.gov/478/When-is-a-Permit-Required ; https://www.desototexas.gov/474/Permit-Application-Submission
- Inspection required before work is concealed and at completion; request online by 4 p.m. for next-day inspection.
  Source: https://www.desototexas.gov/462/Building-Inspections
- Old city ordinance required 30-year limited warranty shingles; effective Sept 1 2019 Texas HB 2439 (building
  material restrictions) supersedes it. Building Official 972-230-9621.
  Source: https://www.desototexas.gov/m/faq?cat=21#question-418
- Permit fee: fee page garbled in results ($110?), NOT used; page tells readers to check the Permit Fees page.
  https://www.desototexas.gov/475/Permit-Fees

## Storm history
- Feb 20 2018 (early Tuesday, about 4:45 a.m. per police): EF-0, NWS peak wind estimate 85 mph, path only a couple of
  blocks; city said about 30 homes had roof damage near South Westmoreland Road and Eagle Drive; a church and a car
  wash on Belt Line Road damaged; no injuries.
  Sources: https://www.fox4news.com/news/ef-0-tornadoes-damage-homes-in-desoto-johnson-county ;
  https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/api/1/nwstext/201802210028-KFWD-NOUS44-PNSFWD ;
  https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/texas/news/tornado-damages-homes-desoto

# Haslet research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing haslet tx (10). County: Tarrant and Denton (per site.json; Wikipedia).

## Verified
- Permits: the city's permit list includes replacing a roof among improvements that need a permit. Contractors must be registered with the City of Haslet before any permit is issued, renewed annually; documents: state licenses when applicable, photo ID, proof of insurance of at least $1,000,000 with the City of Haslet named as certificate holder. Building services at 1090 Schoolhouse Road, Suite 300. Online Permit Portal.
  https://www.haslet.org/181/Permits-and-Contractor-Registrations ; https://www.haslet.org/219/Permits-and-Inspections ; https://www.haslet.org/FormCenter/BUILDING-PERMITS-5/Contractor-Registration-Application-40
- History: settled around 1880; community formed after the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe Railway came through in 1883; population 1,952 in the 2020 census; distribution centers in town; AllianceTexas (since 1989, Fort Worth Alliance Airport) drove growth in the area.
  https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Haslet,_TX ; https://www.alliancetexas.com/Portals/0/Images/AllianceTexas/resources/facts/AllianceTexas%20Economic%20Impact%20Report_Full%20Report.pdf
- NWS Fort Worth significant snow and ice events page: Haslet reported 14.4 inches in an 1893 storm. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/dsnows
- Electric: Tri-County Electric Cooperative lists Haslet in its service area; a City of Haslet PUC filing shows some city facilities on Oncor and one on Tri-County Electric Coop, so both serve parts of town. utility= "Oncor or Tri-County Electric Cooperative, whichever serves your address".
  https://tcectexas.com/about-us ; https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/Documents/52299_659_1163277.PDF

# Saginaw research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing saginaw tx (10). County: Tarrant. (Do not confuse with Saginaw, Michigan.)

## Verified
- March 28, 2000: NWS Fort Worth review of the downtown Fort Worth tornado storm says hail severely damaged roofs and automobiles in Lake Worth and Saginaw. https://www.weather.gov/fwd/march2000ftwarltor
- Building Inspections & Permits: 301 S. Saginaw Blvd., Saginaw TX 76179; 817-232-4640; permits@saginawtx.org; forms emailed, signatures required. Permit categories list a Roof item. https://www.ci.saginaw.tx.us/departments/building_inspections___permits/index.php ; https://www.ci.saginaw.tx.us/departments/building_inspections___permits/permits___forms.php
- City has contractor registration forms and publishes a Registered Contractor List with a roofing category (updated Feb 2026). https://www.ci.saginaw.tx.us/departments/building_inspections___permits/registered_contractors.php ; https://www.ci.saginaw.tx.us/economic_development/doing_business_in_saginaw/registered_contractor_list.php
- Solicitation permit: door-to-door solicitors need a city permit and must wear the permit badge, obey No Soliciting signs; hours 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. April to October and 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. November to March; City Secretary's Office handles it. https://www.SaginawTX.org/business/solicitors_permit.php
- History: settled 1848 by David Thurmond family; named 1882 by Jarvis J. Green after Saginaw Street in Pontiac, Michigan; railroad stop on routes to Fort Worth; "train and grain" heritage; incorporated 1949. https://citybudgets.tarrantcounty.com/flipbook/CityBudgets/2021/Saginaw/files/basic-html/page253.html
- Electric: default wording ("Oncor or your local electric utility").

# Seagoville, TX research notes (Oct 14 2026)

Target: roofing seagoville tx (10). Page: /roofing-seagoville-tx/

## County / city
- City in Dallas County; a small portion extends into Kaufman County. 2020 census 18,446 people, 4,283 households.
  First called Seago, laid out in 1876 by T. K. Seago; the Post Office changed the name to Seagoville in 1910 to avoid
  confusion with another Texas town named Sego. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seagoville,_Texas (via search
  summary); TSHA text: https://www.txgenwebcounties.com/dallas/towns-communities/TSHA/tsha-seagoville.htm
- Federal Correctional Institution Seagoville: opened October 10, 1940 as the Federal Reformatory for Women; held foreign
  nationals in WWII; now a low-security men's prison with a satellite camp; one of the city's largest employers.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Correctional_Institution,_Seagoville
- City Hall 702 N. Hwy 175 (council agenda header).
- Electric: NOT verified (Oncor vs a co-op in the Kaufman County part). Page uses the generic safety box wording
  ("Oncor or your local electric utility") and tells readers to check their bill.

## Permits and registration (City of Seagoville)
- Permitting Department handles building permits, contractor registration and certificates of occupancy.
  Source: https://www.seagoville.us/64/Building-Permits-and-Inspections
- Permits now online: create an account, pull permits, register as a contractor and request inspections online
  (seagovilletx.viewpointcloud.com). Source: https://www.seagoville.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=967
- Inspections line 972-287-6829. FAQ: permits required when constructing, remodeling or enlarging; cosmetic work no;
  when in doubt check with Building Inspections. Source: https://www.seagoville.us/Faq.aspx?QID=82
- No roofing-specific permit rule or fee found: page tells reader to ask the city.

## Storm history
- September 8, 2010 (remnants period of that week): SPC storm report log lists a tornado southwest of Seagoville (live
  video) and a 65 mph wind report in the city. Seagoville police: 4.5-mile swath of damage in the city; an apartment complex
  reported about 10 units damaged; a resident's garage roof torn off and landed on his gazebo.
  Sources: https://spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/100908_rpts.html ; https://www.dallasnews.com/news/news/2010/09/09/Man-outruns-tornado-that-was-on-4238

## Permits (City of Irving, cityofirving.org)
- A city notice lists reroofing among common projects that need a building permit; permits and
  inspections are required whether the work is done by a contractor or by the homeowner.
  https://www.cityofirving.org/CivicAlerts/?Mobile=ON
- Permits are applied for through the city's online permit portal. Inspections Department questions:
  (972) 721-2371, Irving-Permits@cityofirving.org; City Hall, 825 W. Irving Blvd. (from the city FAQ
  result). https://www.cityofirving.org/Faq.aspx?QID=788
- Fee schedule (2022 version in results): re-roofing and roofing overlay permits $100 single-family and
  townhouse, $100 duplex, $150 multifamily, $150 commercial, per structure. Confirm current fees.
  https://www.cityofirving.org/4067/Fee-Schedule
- City FAQ on roof decking: when decking is replaced during a re-roof, the decking requirement is the
  same as for new construction; Irving follows the International Residential Code rather than a local
  decking-thickness amendment. https://www.cityofirving.org/Faq.aspx?QID=788
- City says most contractors that must be licensed are electrical, mechanical, irrigation and plumbing
  contractors (state licenses, TDLR). Roofers are not on that list. No separate city roofing
  contractor registration was found in results (not confirmed either way; page tells readers to ask
  the Inspections Department).

## Storm history
- Oct 20 2019 North Dallas tornado: NWS Fort Worth survey lists the start point as "3 ENE Irving",
  Dallas County; formed in northwest Dallas near State Highway 348 and Luna Road, moved east-northeast
  across I-35E toward Richardson; peak wind estimate 140 mph; path 15.76 miles; max width 1,300 yards;
  ended 9:30 p.m. CDT. https://www.weather.gov/media/fwd/torclimo/PNS/PNS_October_20_2019.pdf
  Page states only that it formed just east of Irving's city limits area, in northwest Dallas.

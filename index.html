Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free roof inspections across DFW?

Call and you will be put through to a local roofer who can inspect the roof. Many roofers inspect storm damage at no charge, but confirm on the call before they come out, and ask for the findings in writing.

My roof was damaged in the last DFW hail storm — what should I do?

Get the roof inspected soon, and photograph any damage yourself. Call your insurance company to open the claim. A roofer can document the damage and meet your adjuster on site, but in Texas the claim itself is between you and your insurer. Read your policy for the deductible and whether it pays replacement cost or actual cash value.

How long does a full roof replacement take in DFW?

Many residential replacements in Dallas-Fort Worth take a day or two, depending on the size of the roof, the weather and whether decking has to be replaced. Ask the roofer for the schedule before work starts.

What roofing brands do you install in DFW?

Most local roofers install major brands such as GAF, Owens Corning and CertainTeed. In hail country, ask about impact-resistant (Class 4) shingles, which some insurers discount.

Do you serve all DFW cities including Frisco, McKinney, and Plano?

Yes. The line takes calls from across the DFW metroplex, including the North Dallas suburbs, the Mid-Cities, Arlington, Irving and Grand Prairie.
